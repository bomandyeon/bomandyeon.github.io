사주 · Korean saju, explained

What is saju?

Short answer

Saju (사주, 'four pillars') is the Korean system that turns your birth year, month, day and hour into eight characters, and reads them as a personality profile, with the day you were born giving your core type.

Saju in one paragraph

Saju is the Korean name for the four pillars of destiny, the same system the Chinese call Bazi. The name sounds grand, but in daily Korean life it works more like a personality language. People look up their saju for fun, check a couple's compatibility (궁합, gunghap) before a wedding, and visit saju cafés with friends the way others might do a tarot night. At Bom & Yeon we treat it the way most people treat MBTI: a mirror for self-understanding, not a prediction.

Your birth moment becomes four pillars: year, month, day and hour. Each pillar is a pair of characters from the old calendar, a heavenly stem on top and an earthly branch below. Four pairs make eight characters, which is why saju is also called palja (팔자), 'eight characters'.

Your birthday type: the one character that is you

Of those eight characters, one stands for you: the heavenly stem of your birth day. Saju calls it the Day Master (일간). We call it your birthday type, because that is what it is. There are ten, each with an element (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal or Water) and a yin or yang side. Each has an image that makes it easy to remember.

The five elements, briefly

Every character in a chart carries one of five elements. They feed and check each other in two cycles: Wood feeds Fire, Fire makes Earth, Earth holds Metal, Metal carries Water, Water feeds Wood; and Wood breaks Earth, Earth dams Water, Water puts out Fire, Fire melts Metal, Metal cuts Wood. Compatibility in saju is mostly a story about those two cycles.

What saju is not

It is not a verdict on your life. Traditional readers use saju for timing and advice; we use it as a language for personality and relationships, with jokes. Nothing here should decide anything about your health, money or relationships. Two people with the same birthday type can be very different, the same way two people with the same MBTI are.

It is also not one-size-fits-all. Your full chart has eight characters, and the 60 day pillars already split each birthday type into six versions. Your birthday type is the headline, not the whole article.

FAQ

Is saju the same as Chinese astrology?

It comes from the same root, the four pillars system known in Chinese as Bazi. Korean saju uses the same calendar maths and adds its own vocabulary and habits, like gunghap for couples.

Do I need my birth time for saju?

Not for your birthday type. That comes from the date alone. The birth hour adds the fourth pillar, and it matters for the day only if you were born close to midnight.

Is saju accurate?

Saju is a traditional framework, not a science, and nothing in it has been proven to predict events. Many people find the personality descriptions useful for reflection, the same way people use MBTI. Treat it as a mirror, not a forecast.

How many saju types are there?

Ten birthday types (Day Masters). Paired with the twelve branches they form sixty day pillars, and a full chart has eight characters, so the number of complete charts runs into the hundreds of thousands.
