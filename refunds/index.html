Refunds

Last updated 2026-10-24.

If a reading doesn't open, opens with the wrong birthday, or you were charged twice, email yeon@bomandyeon.com within 30 days with your receipt and we'll fix it or refund you in full. If you simply didn't enjoy it, write to us anyway within 30 days. We would rather refund you than keep money from someone who's unhappy.

Readings are sold through Gumroad, so refunds go back to your original payment method through Gumroad and usually arrive in 5 to 10 business days. A refunded license key stops opening the reading.

This 30-day promise applies on top of any legal right you have, including after you've opened and started reading. See Terms.
