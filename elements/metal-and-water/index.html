Metal and Water: compatibility from the Metal side

Metal produces Water. Condensation forms on cold steel, and springs rise from stone. If you are a Metal Day Master, Gyeong or Sin, a Water Day Master, Im (壬, the ocean) or Gye (癸, the rain), is your Output, the element your energy flows into when you express yourself. With Water you become more articulate, more creative and more relaxed. This is one of the most elegant pairings in saju: the classics have a soft spot for Metal and Water together.

Metal feeds Water in the generating cycle, so Water is Metal's Output.

BomYou bring out each other's brilliance. Your conversations are the kind people remember for years.

Chemistry

From your side, Water is Eating God (식신) if you share polarity and Hurting Officer (상관) if you do not. Output energy is wit, talent and self-expression. Water partners are intuitive, adaptable and emotionally fluent, and they draw out your inner world. The Qiong Tong Bao Jian loves Sin with Im, the jewel washed clean by clear water, a picture of brilliance and beauty. Gyeong with Gye is steel cooled by rain, sharp and calm. Metal-Water charts are classically linked with intelligence, and this pair often has great conversations.

Friction

Metal gives, Water flows away with it. You may feel drained, providing structure and support while your partner drifts. Water people can be hard to pin down, moody or vague, which frustrates your need for clarity. Too much Water can also make Metal feel cold and isolated, the classic chill of a winter blade. And if your Water partner never offers warmth back, you may become critical and distant.

In love

In love you are the source and they are the stream. It works when they appreciate your steadiness and when you allow yourself to be moved by their depth. Make sure the giving goes both ways, and bring in some Fire, warmth and fun, so the relationship does not get too cool.

As friends

As friends, you have the best late-night conversations. They help you say what you feel; you help them decide what to do about it.

YeonYou cannot hold water in a clenched fist. Relax your grip and see if they stay. They usually do.

How to make it work

Earth refills you, so steady routines and grounding people keep you from running dry. Wood, Water's output, gives you shared growth: projects, learning, travel. Ask for clarity when you need it. Water can give it once it knows you want it.

The Day Master pairs

Read from the Metal side, the four Day Master pairings here are Gyeong with Im (Eating God), Gyeong with Gye (Hurting Officer), Sin with Im (Hurting Officer), and Sin with Gye (Eating God), and each of those pairings links to its own compatibility page.
