Earth and Earth: compatibility from the Earth side

Short answer

Two Earth birthday types together are a landscape: a mountain beside a valley, or two fields that share a fence. Earth and Earth are the same element, so each is a peer to the other.

Two Earth birthday types (Day Masters) together are a landscape: a mountain beside a valley, or two fields that share a fence. Earth is the element of stability, patience, loyalty and holding things together. Mu (戊) is the mountain, big and immovable; Gi (己) is the garden field, soft and fertile. When two of you meet, life slows down to a pace you both like. Nobody rushes, nobody flakes, and the snacks are always sorted.

Earth and Earth are the same element, so each is a peer to the other.

BomTogether you build something steady and safe, the kind of life people quietly wish they had.

Chemistry

You understand each other's need for security. Neither of you needs constant novelty, and both of you show love through reliability and practical care. Another Earth is a peer: Friend (비견) if polarity matches, Rob Wealth (겁재) if it does not. You trust each other quickly once trust is earned, and you build well together: homes, savings, traditions, a garden that actually survives the summer. Mu with Gi is a mountain sheltering a field, a protective, layered pairing where one gives shelter and the other gives growth.

Friction

Two Earths can get stuck. You may both resist change, both wait for the other to make the move, and both dig in when you disagree. Mu with Mu is two mountains, steady and silent, with conflicts that last for weeks because neither will budge. Gi with Gi is two fields, comfortable and a little muddy, prone to worry and overthinking. Without Wood to break up the soil or Fire to warm it, the relationship can feel safe and slightly gray. You may also both swallow feelings rather than name them.

In love

Romance between two Earth birthday types is steady, loyal and quietly sweet. The risk is routine turning into rut. Schedule novelty the way you schedule bills: a new city once a year, a new restaurant once a month, a new topic at dinner once a week. It sounds unromantic and works beautifully for Earth.

As friends

As friends you are the dependable core of any group, the people who show up early to help set up and stay late to clean. Lifelong, low drama, high trust.

YeonTwo mountains staring at each other is not a conversation. Someone say something. Today.

How to make it work

Bring Wood and Fire in on purpose. Wood is growth: a shared goal, a class, a challenge. Fire is warmth: celebration, affection, saying the nice thing out loud. And practice speaking first in conflicts. Someone has to move, and it is fine if it is you this time.

The birthday type pairs

Read from the Earth side, the four birthday type pairings here are Mu with Mu (Friend), Mu with Gi (Rob Wealth), Gi with Mu (Rob Wealth), and Gi with Gi (Friend), and each of those pairings links to its own compatibility page.

Is Earth and Earth a good saju match?

It is a peer match: very stable and loyal, with a risk of stagnation and silent standoffs. Adding novelty and warmth keeps it lively.

What is different about Mu with Gi?

Mu is yang Earth, the mountain, and Gi is yin Earth, the field. They are Rob Wealth to each other, a peer bond that tends to feel protective and complementary.
