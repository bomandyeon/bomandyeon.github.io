Earth and Wood: compatibility from the Earth side

Wood controls Earth, so if you are an Earth birthday type, Mu (戊, the mountain) or Gi (己, the garden field), a Wood birthday type, Gap or Eul, is your Officer: the element that breaks up your soil, pushes you, and makes things grow on you. It can feel like pressure. It can also feel like finally having a reason to move. Classical texts insist that Earth without Wood is barren, a mountain with no forest.

BomThey bring new life into your world, and you give them a place to put down roots. That is a beautiful exchange.

Chemistry

In Ten God terms, Wood is Seven Killings (편관) if you share polarity and Direct Officer (정관) if you do not. Officer energy brings ambition, standards and change. Wood partners are full of plans, opinions and forward motion, and they pull you out of comfortable routines. The heavenly combination here is Gi with Gap (甲己合): a garden field holding a tall tree, one of saju’s most celebrated pairings. Mu with Eul, a mountain covered in flowering vines, is the other opposite-polarity partner-star pair, softer and more charming.

Friction

Wood pushes, Earth resists. If your Wood partner keeps trying to change your habits, your pace or your plans, you may dig in and become immovable. Mu with Gap is the hardest version: pine against mountain, two big wills, constant renegotiation. Gi with Eul is a field overrun with vines, where you feel crowded and drained. You might also feel your steadiness is undervalued, as if being reliable were boring.

In love

In love, Wood gives you growth and you give Wood roots. They make your life bigger; you make theirs safer. It works when change is invited rather than imposed. Tell them which parts of you are open for renovation and which are load-bearing walls.

As friends

As friends, Wood signs you both up for the class, and you are the one who actually finishes it. They get you moving; you keep them honest.

YeonBeing solid is not the same as being stuck. If they suggest one change, try it before you build a wall.

How to make it work

Fire is the bridge: Wood feeds Fire and Fire feeds Earth. Warm, fun, shared experiences turn their pressure into support for you. Ask for appreciation directly. Wood people forget that steady people also need to hear that they matter.
