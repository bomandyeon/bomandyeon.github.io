Earth and Water: compatibility from the Earth side

Short answer

Earth dams Water. Earth controls Water in the controlling cycle, so Water is Earth's Wealth.

Earth dams Water. If you are an Earth birthday type (Day Master), Mu or Gi, a Water birthday type, Im (壬, the ocean) or Gye (癸, the rain), is your Wealth, the element you control and give shape to. A river needs banks, and a reservoir needs walls. There is real attraction here: you offer structure and safety, and they offer depth, flow and a little mystery. There is also a classic tug of war between holding and flowing.

Earth controls Water in the controlling cycle, so Water is Earth's Wealth.

BomYou give their depth a safe place to rest, and they bring life into your steady world. That balance is rare.

Chemistry

In Ten God terms, Water is Indirect Wealth (편재) if you share polarity and Direct Wealth (정재) if you do not. Wealth is what you manage, enjoy and protect. Water partners are intuitive and adaptable, and they bring emotional depth into your steady world. The heavenly combination here is Mu with Gye (戊癸合): a mountain and the rain on its slopes, a famously magnetic pair. Gi with Im is the other partner-star pairing, a field irrigated by a river. The Qiong Tong Bao Jian even recommends 戊 Earth as the embankment strong Im Water needs.

Friction

Mu with Im is the strong-willed version, a mountain trying to hold back an ocean, with constant pressure on the walls. Gi with Gye is a field and a drizzle, gentle, but prone to mud and worry. You may try to control your Water partner's moods, schedule or spending; they may feel trapped and slip away. Water also changes direction more easily than Earth, which can look flaky to someone who values consistency.

In love

In love you are the shore and they are the tide. It works when the banks are generous: structure that guides rather than confines. Give them room to flow, and ask them to tell you where they are going. They will, once they trust the walls are there to help.

As friends

As friends, Water brings the deep talk and you bring the practical next step. They make you more reflective; you make them more grounded.

YeonYou are a riverbank, not a dam with a security guard. Loosen the walls before they start looking for cracks.

How to make it work

Metal is the bridge: Earth feeds Metal and Metal feeds Water. Clear agreements, shared standards and honest talks turn your control into nourishment. Let some things stay fluid. Not every plan needs concrete.

The birthday type pairs

Read from the Earth side, the four birthday type pairings here are Mu with Im (Indirect Wealth), Mu with Gye (Direct Wealth), Gi with Im (Direct Wealth), and Gi with Gye (Indirect Wealth), and each of those pairings links to its own compatibility page.
