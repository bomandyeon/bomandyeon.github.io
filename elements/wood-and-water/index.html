Wood and Water: compatibility from the Wood side

Water feeds Wood. For a Wood Day Master, Gap or Eul, a Water Day Master, Im (壬, the ocean) or Gye (癸, the rain), is your Resource: the element that nourishes you, protects you and helps you grow. Picture a tree beside a river or a vine after spring rain. This is one of the gentlest pairings in the cycle. You feel understood, supported and calmer around them, and they enjoy watching you flourish.

Water feeds Wood in the generating cycle, so Water is Wood's Resource.

BomThey help you grow and you show them what their care can build. That kind of quiet support changes lives.

Chemistry

From your side, Water is Resource: Indirect Resource (편인) if you share polarity, Direct Resource (정인) if you do not. Resource stars are about learning, care, intuition and safety. Water people tend to be thoughtful, emotionally fluent and patient, which gives your restless growth somewhere to rest. Gap with Gye is a favorite image: rain on a pine, the reason it grows tall. Eul with Im is a flower on a riverbank, rooted in endless supply. You often feel smarter and kinder around them.

Friction

Too much water rots roots. You can lean on a Water partner so much that you stop taking initiative, or wait for them to make the hard calls. Water can also feel unappreciated, giving and giving while you get the credit for growing. And Water's moods, deep and changeable, can confuse a Wood person who wants a clear plan and a straight answer.

In love

In love this pair feels like home. Water listens; Wood grows. Your partner may feel like a teacher, a therapist and a best friend rolled into one. Keep it balanced by giving back: plan things, take the lead sometimes, ask about their inner world as often as they ask about yours.

As friends

As friends, they are the one you call after a bad day, and you are the one who drags them out of their head and into a hiking trail.

YeonThey are a river, not a lifeguard. Stop waiting to be rescued and make one decision on your own this week.

How to make it work

Fire and Earth keep the balance. Fire, meaning fun and expression, stops the relationship from becoming all depth and no play. Earth, meaning practical plans, gives the water banks. Notice when you are leaning and lean back the other way.

The Day Master pairs

Read from the Wood side, the four Day Master pairings here are Gap with Im (Indirect Resource), Gap with Gye (Direct Resource), Eul with Im (Direct Resource), and Eul with Gye (Indirect Resource), and each of those pairings links to its own compatibility page.
