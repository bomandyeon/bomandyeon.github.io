Earth and Metal: compatibility from the Earth side

Earth produces Metal. Earth feeds Metal in the generating cycle, so Metal is Earth’s Output.

Earth produces Metal. Ore comes out of mountains, and gems form in the ground. If you are an Earth birthday type (Day Master), Mu or Gi, a Metal birthday type, Gyeong (庚, the sword) or Sin (辛, the jewel), is your Output, the element you naturally create and express. This pairing often feels like being a proud parent, a mentor or a patron: you give them a base, and they become something sharp and shining.

Earth feeds Metal in the generating cycle, so Metal is Earth’s Output.

BomYou give them a foundation and they show the world what you helped build. That is real partnership.

Chemistry

From your side, Metal is Eating God (식신) if you share polarity and Hurting Officer (상관) if you do not. Output stars are creativity, talent and generosity. Metal partners bring clarity, decisiveness and style, and they turn your steady support into visible results. Gi with Gyeong is a field yielding an axe, practical and productive; Mu with Sin is a mountain holding a hidden gem, a classic image of quiet richness. Many Earth people say a Metal partner helps them finally finish things.

Friction

Earth gives, Metal takes shape and sometimes walks off with the credit. You may feel drained or overlooked while they shine. Metal people can be critical, precise and emotionally cool, which can wound an Earth person who expresses love through steady care and expects the same back. And if you give too much, you can end up, as the classics put it about Sin, burying the jewel: smothering a Metal partner with protection they did not ask for.

In love

In love you are the safe harbor and they are the bright edge. It works when they acknowledge what you give and when you let them have their independence. Metal people need space to be sharp; Earth people need to hear they are valued. Both are easy to provide once named.

As friends

As friends, you are their biggest supporter, and they are the friend who tells you the truth about your ex, your haircut and your business plan, kindly but clearly.

YeonSupport is not suffocation. Let your jewel breathe. They know how to shine without three layers of soil on top.

How to make it work

Fire refills you, so warmth, encouragement and fun outside the relationship keep you from running empty. Water, Metal’s output, gives the two of you shared depth: long talks, reflection, a slower pace. Celebrate what you have built together, not just what they achieved.

The birthday type pairs

Read from the Earth side, the four birthday type pairings here are Mu with Gyeong (Eating God), Mu with Sin (Hurting Officer), Gi with Gyeong (Hurting Officer), and Gi with Sin (Eating God), and each of those pairings links to its own compatibility page.
