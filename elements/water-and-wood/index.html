Water and Wood: compatibility from the Water side

Water feeds Wood. If you are a Water Day Master, Im or Gye, a Wood Day Master, Gap (甲, the tall pine) or Eul (乙, the climbing flower), is your Output: the element you naturally nourish. You water their roots, and they grow tall and green. This is a generous, fulfilling pairing where your depth turns into something visible. Many Water people say they feel most useful and most creative with a Wood partner.

Water feeds Wood in the generating cycle, so Wood is Water's Output.

BomYour care helps them grow into who they are meant to be. That kind of nourishment is a real gift.

Chemistry

From your side, Wood is Eating God (식신) if you share polarity and Hurting Officer (상관) if you do not. Output energy means creativity, generosity and expression. Wood partners are idealistic and driven, and they give your emotional depth a direction. Gye with Gap is rain on a pine, the reason it grows tall. Im with Eul is a river feeding a riverbank garden, endless supply for something delicate. You often help them grow in ways they could not manage alone, and they make your care tangible. There is also a quiet pride in this pairing. Watching someone you love become more confident, more successful or simply more themselves, and knowing your patience helped, is one of the most satisfying feelings a Water person can have. Wood partners who notice that, and say so, become very hard to leave.

Friction

Water drains into Wood. You may give and give, offering emotional support, advice and patience, while they get taller and busier. Wood people can be stubborn and focused on their own goals, and they may not notice when you are running low. You might also feel your depth is underappreciated, as if you were a resource rather than a partner.

In love

In love you are the nourisher and they are the grower. It works when they see your care and give back, and when you remember you are allowed to need things too. Ask for attention directly. Wood people respond well to clear requests.

As friends

As friends, you help them through crises and they help you get out of your head and into action.

YeonYou are not their irrigation system. Turn off the tap for a week and see who checks on you.

How to make it work

Metal refills you: structure, clarity and supportive people outside the relationship. Fire, Wood's output, gives the relationship shared fun and celebration. Make sure your own growth is on the agenda too.

The Day Master pairs

Read from the Water side, the four Day Master pairings here are Im with Gap (Eating God), Im with Eul (Hurting Officer), Gye with Gap (Hurting Officer), and Gye with Eul (Eating God), and each of those pairings links to its own compatibility page.

Is Water good for Wood in saju?

Yes, Water feeds Wood. For a Water Day Master, Wood is Output, linked to creativity and generosity.

Why can Water feel drained with Wood?

Energy flows from Water to Wood. If care does not come back, it feels one-sided. Clear requests help.
