Earth and Fire: compatibility from the Earth side

Short answer

Fire feeds Earth. Fire feeds Earth in the generating cycle, so Fire is Earth's Resource.

Fire feeds Earth. If you are an Earth birthday type (Day Master), Mu or Gi, a Fire birthday type, Byeong (丙, the sun) or Jeong (丁, the candle), is your Resource: the element that warms, supports and energizes you. Picture a sunny field or a mountain glowing at sunset. This is one of the most comforting pairings in saju. Fire people make you feel more confident, more cheerful and less stuck in your own head.

Fire feeds Earth in the generating cycle, so Fire is Earth's Resource.

BomThey warm you up and you give their fire somewhere solid to land. That is a pairing people envy.

Chemistry

From your side, Fire is Indirect Resource (편인) if you share polarity and Direct Resource (정인) if you do not. Resource energy means encouragement, learning and safety. Fire partners bring warmth, enthusiasm and a sense that things are possible. Gi with Byeong is a favorite image: sun on the garden field, the condition for every harvest. Mu with Jeong is the lamp inside a mountain temple, quiet and steady, warmth in a big, solid space. Earth born in winter especially benefits from Fire, according to the classics.

Friction

You can lean on Fire's warmth and stop generating your own. Fire may also burn through plans faster than you like, changing direction while you are still building the foundation. Their moods and spontaneity can feel chaotic to someone who values consistency. And if they feel their warmth goes unacknowledged, they may look for an audience elsewhere, at least emotionally. Byeong Fire paired with Mu Earth can also turn into a quiet contest of wills, since both are yang and both like to set the tone. The sun wants to warm everything at once; the mountain wants to stay exactly where it is. Neither is wrong. Agree in advance which decisions belong to whom.

In love

In love Fire makes you feel adored and alive, and you make Fire feel safe and appreciated. It is a cozy, affectionate pair. Keep it balanced by showing your appreciation out loud and by joining in their adventures sometimes, even when the couch is calling.

As friends

As friends, Fire drags you to the new restaurant, and you are the one who remembers the name and books it again next month.

YeonDo not just bask. Say thank you to your sun once in a while, and maybe plan the date yourself.

How to make it work

Metal, your output, gives the relationship shared results: projects, savings, a home you both care about. Water keeps Fire from overheating. Make sure the warmth flows both ways, with your steadiness offered as a gift, not just taken for granted.
