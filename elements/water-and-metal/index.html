Water and Metal: compatibility from the Water side

Metal produces Water. If you are a Water Day Master, Im or Gye, a Metal Day Master, Gyeong (庚, the sword) or Sin (辛, the jewel), is your Resource: the element that feeds, steadies and protects you. Picture a spring rising from a crack in white rock, or rain collecting on a cold iron gate. With Metal people you often feel clearer, calmer and more focused, as if your thoughts finally have edges.

Metal feeds Water in the generating cycle, so Metal is Water's Resource.

BomThey give you a steady place to stand and you show them the beauty of what flows. That is a truly supportive bond.

Chemistry

From your side, Metal is Indirect Resource (편인) if you share polarity and Direct Resource (정인) if you do not. Resource energy means learning, protection and intuition. Metal partners are decisive, honest and principled, which gives your fluid inner world something solid to rely on. The Qiong Tong Bao Jian describes Gye fed by Sin as the source of a stream (辛金發源), a pure, beautiful image. Im with Gyeong is a river fed by mountain ore, strong and clear. Metal-Water pairs are classically linked to intelligence, and you often think brilliantly together.

Friction

You can lean too hard. A Metal partner may become your decision-maker, your critic and your safety net all at once, and you stop trusting your own instincts. Metal people can also be blunt and cool, which can sting a sensitive Water person. They may find you vague or slow to commit. And if they feel their steady support goes unnoticed, they may harden into criticism.

In love

In love they give you clarity and you give them depth. You make them feel understood; they make you feel safe. It works when you thank them for their honesty and when they soften it with warmth. Keep your own voice in decisions. Their clarity is a resource, not a replacement for yours.

As friends

As friends, Metal is the one you text before a big decision, and you are the one they call when they cannot explain what they are feeling.

YeonAsk for advice, sure. Then make the decision yourself. They are your source, not your manager.

How to make it work

Wood, your output, keeps the relationship growing: shared projects, travel, creativity. Earth, Metal's resource, keeps your Metal partner grounded and generous. Make sure the support flows both ways.

The Day Master pairs

Read from the Water side, the four Day Master pairings here are Im with Gyeong (Indirect Resource), Im with Sin (Direct Resource), Gye with Gyeong (Direct Resource), and Gye with Sin (Indirect Resource), and each of those pairings links to its own compatibility page.
