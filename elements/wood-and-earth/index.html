Wood and Earth: compatibility from the Wood side

Short answer

Wood controls Earth. Wood controls Earth in the controlling cycle, so Earth is Wood's Wealth.

Wood controls Earth. In nature, roots break up soil and hold it together at the same time. When you, a Wood birthday type (Day Master), meet an Earth birthday type, Mu (戊, the mountain) or Gi (己, the garden field), you are meeting your Wealth element. Earth is what Wood works on, shapes and depends on. There is real attraction here, the pull between someone who wants to build and someone who can be built on, plus a power question you will want to talk about early.

Wood controls Earth in the controlling cycle, so Earth is Wood's Wealth.

BomYou bring the vision and they bring the ground to plant it in. Together you can build things that actually last.

Chemistry

From your side, Earth is Wealth: Indirect Wealth (편재) or Direct Wealth (정재). Wealth stars are about what you can hold, shape and take care of, so Earth people make you feel capable and grounded. They are practical, patient and reliable, which calms your restless growth. The classic match here is Gap with Gi, one of the five heavenly combinations (甲己合): the tall tree rooted in a soft field. It is among the most praised pairings in Korean saju, the definition of two people who just fit.

Friction

Control can tip into pressure. Wood people can treat Earth partners as a project: improving their habits, steering their plans, fixing their spreadsheets. Earth may feel managed or depleted, especially Mu Earth, the mountain, which does not love being rearranged. Gap with Mu is two strong wills, a pine trying to split a mountain. You may also take Earth's steadiness for granted until it quietly runs out.

In love

In love, Earth gives you roots and you give Earth growth and direction. You make their life more interesting; they make yours more stable. The best version is a partnership where you build something tangible together and both feel in charge of part of it. Ask before you fix anything about them.

As friends

As friends, you bring the plans and they bring the follow-through, plus snacks. Earth friends will tell you, kindly, when your idea needs a budget.

YeonYour partner is soil, not a renovation project. Put down the shovel and ask what they want to grow.

How to make it work

Fire is the bridge here: Wood feeds Fire and Fire feeds Earth, so warmth, shared fun and generosity turn your control into nourishment. Compliment their reliability out loud. Let them make some decisions you would have made differently.

The birthday type pairs

Read from the Wood side, the four birthday type pairings here are Gap with Mu (Indirect Wealth), Gap with Gi (Direct Wealth), Eul with Mu (Direct Wealth), and Eul with Gi (Indirect Wealth), and each of those pairings links to its own compatibility page.

Why is Gap Wood and Gi Earth considered a great match?

It can lean that way if nobody pays attention. In healthy couples it shows up as Wood bringing direction and Earth bringing stability, with both sharing decisions.
