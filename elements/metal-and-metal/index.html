Metal and Metal: compatibility from the Metal side

Short answer

Two Metal birthday types together are a sword and a sword, or a sword and a jewel, or two gems in the same case. Metal and Metal are the same element, so each is a peer to the other.

Two Metal birthday types (Day Masters) together are a sword and a sword, or a sword and a jewel, or two gems in the same case. Metal is the element of clarity, precision, standards and justice. Gyeong (庚) is raw steel, direct and tough; Sin (辛) is the refined jewel, elegant and exacting. When two of you meet, there is instant respect. You both value honesty, competence and people who do what they say.

Metal and Metal are the same element, so each is a peer to the other.

BomYou respect each other deeply, and that respect makes you both better. It is the foundation of a very strong bond.

Chemistry

Neither of you has to explain why details matter. You can make decisions quickly, give each other blunt feedback and trust that it is meant well. Another Metal is a peer: Friend (비견) if polarity matches, Rob Wealth (겁재) if it does not. Peers sharpen each other. You push each other to higher standards and you celebrate quality, whether it is a well-made coat or a well-argued point. Gyeong with Sin is a blade and a jewel, strength beside beauty, often a pair that admires each other's very different kind of excellence.

Friction

Two blades clash. You can both be critical, both insist on being right and both withdraw behind cool composure when hurt. Gyeong with Gyeong is two swords crossing, tough, competitive and slow to apologize. Sin with Sin is two jewels, each a little too aware of the other's flaws. Neither of you naturally brings Fire, warmth and forgiveness, or Water, emotional flow, so conflicts can turn into polite, frosty standoffs. The relationship can feel like a well-run office with excellent lighting and no heating.

In love

Romance between two Metal birthday types is loyal, honest and quietly intense. The risk is coldness. Make affection a habit: say the soft thing, hold hands in public, leave a note that is not a to-do list. Agree that being kind matters more than being correct, at least at home.

As friends

As friends you are the group's quality control: the honest review, the best recommendation, the friend who notices the typo. Lifelong respect, very little drama.

YeonTwo people who are always right make a very quiet house. Apologize first this time. It will not chip your edge.

How to make it work

Bring in Water and Fire on purpose. Water is expression: talk about feelings, write things down, take long walks. Fire is warmth: celebrate, laugh, forgive quickly. Take turns being the one who softens first.
