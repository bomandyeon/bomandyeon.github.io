Water and Water: compatibility from the Water side

Short answer

Two Water birthday types together are a whole weather system: an ocean and the rain falling on it, or two rivers meeting. Water and Water are the same element, so each is a peer to the other.

Two Water birthday types (Day Masters) together are a whole weather system: an ocean and the rain falling on it, or two rivers meeting. Water is the element of depth, intuition, adaptability and emotion. Im (壬) is the ocean, vast and powerful; Gye (癸) is the rain, soft and pervasive. When two of you meet, conversation goes deep fast. You understand moods without explanation, and silence between you feels comfortable rather than awkward.

Water and Water are the same element, so each is a peer to the other.

BomYou understand each other at a level most people never reach. That depth is a rare and beautiful thing.

Chemistry

You get each other emotionally. Neither of you needs things spelled out, and both of you appreciate subtlety, mystery and long talks at night. Another Water is a peer: Friend (비견) if polarity matches, Rob Wealth (겁재) if it does not. Peers deepen each other. You share books, theories, playlists and unspoken jokes. Im with Gye is the ocean and the rain, one big and one gentle, often a pair that blends seamlessly: you can spend a whole Sunday together and barely notice the hours.

Friction

Two Waters can flood. Moods amplify, worries multiply and nobody builds the banks. Im with Im is two oceans, intense and occasionally overwhelming, with emotional storms that roll on for days. Gye with Gye is two rains, gentle but prone to drizzly indecision and passive avoidance. Neither of you naturally brings Earth, structure and decisions, or Fire, warmth and action, so plans can drift, and conflicts can go underground rather than get resolved.

In love

Romance between two Water birthday types is intimate, intuitive and deep. The risk is drifting without direction or merging so completely you lose your separate shapes. Set shared goals with dates on them. Keep separate friends and hobbies. Practice saying the problem out loud before it becomes a tide.

As friends

As friends you are each other's therapist, librarian and late-night philosopher. Lifelong, very deep, occasionally hard to reach by phone.

YeonTwo oceans and zero coastline is just a flood with good conversation. Somebody make a plan.

How to make it work

Bring in Earth and Fire on purpose. Earth is structure: calendars, budgets, decisions with deadlines. Fire is warmth and action: going out, celebrating, doing instead of just discussing. Take turns being the one who decides.
