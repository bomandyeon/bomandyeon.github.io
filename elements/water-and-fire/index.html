Water and Fire: compatibility from the Water side

Water controls Fire. If you are a Water Day Master, Im or Gye, a Fire Day Master, Byeong (丙, the sun) or Jeong (丁, the candle), is your Wealth: the element you temper, balance and enjoy. It is the most cinematic pairing in the cycle. Steam rises, sparks hiss, and the two of you end up talking until 3 a.m. about everything. You are drawn to their warmth and visibility; they are drawn to your depth and calm. You also have the power to dim them, which is worth handling with care.

Water controls Fire in the controlling cycle, so Fire is Water's Wealth.

BomYou bring calm to their fire, and they bring light to your depths. When this pairing works, it is unforgettable.

Chemistry

From your side, Fire is Indirect Wealth (편재) if you share polarity and Direct Wealth (정재) if you do not. Wealth is what you manage, enjoy and invest your energy in. Fire partners are vivid, generous and bold, and they pull you out of your inner world into actual parties. The heavenly combination is Im with Jeong (丁壬合), the ocean and the candle, an old symbol of romance. Gye with Byeong is rain in sunshine, which Korean folklore calls the day the tiger gets married, a sweet and slightly magical image. And the classics praise Im with Byeong: sunlight dancing on a great river.

Friction

Same-polarity pairs feel the most friction. Im with Byeong is technically a heavenly stem clash (丙壬沖), ocean against sun, with strong attraction and stronger opinions. Gye with Jeong is another clash (丁癸沖), rain on a candle, intimate and prone to small, painful misunderstandings. You may cool their enthusiasm without meaning to, with a realistic comment or a pause that reads as disapproval. They may overwhelm you with intensity, urgency and the need for an immediate reply. You process feelings privately; they process them out loud.

In love

In love this pair is magnetic and balancing. You calm them; they light you up. It works when your calm is support rather than criticism, and when they give you time to think instead of demanding an instant reaction. Say your warm thoughts out loud. Fire people need to hear them, not deduce them.

As friends

As friends, they are the one who drags you to the concert, and you are the one they call when the concert ends in a crisis.

YeonDo not put out their fire just because you are tired. A quiet 'not now, later' is kinder than an ice bucket.

How to make it work

Wood is the bridge: Water feeds Wood and Wood feeds Fire. Shared learning and growth turn your control into fuel for them. Agree on a conflict rhythm, a short check-in now and the full talk tomorrow, so nobody feels shut out and nobody feels ambushed.

The Day Master pairs

Read from the Water side, the four Day Master pairings here are Im with Byeong (Indirect Wealth), Im with Jeong (Direct Wealth), Gye with Byeong (Direct Wealth), and Gye with Jeong (Indirect Wealth), and each of those pairings links to its own compatibility page.

Is Water and Fire a bad saju match?

Water controls Fire, which brings tension, but also balance. Im with Jeong is a heavenly combination, and classical texts praise Im with Byeong.

Which Water and Fire pairs clash?

Im with Byeong and Gye with Jeong are two of the four classic heavenly stem clashes. The opposite-polarity pairs tend to feel softer.
