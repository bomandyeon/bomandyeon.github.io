Short answer

In saju, two elements either feed each other, check each other or mirror each other, and that relation is the backbone of compatibility. Your element is the element of your birthday type.

How the five elements relate in saju compatibility
RelationPairsIn a couple it feels like
Feeds (generating cycle)Water to Wood, Wood to Fire, Fire to Earth, Earth to Metal, Metal to WaterEasy flow. One gives, the other grows.
Checks (controlling cycle)Wood to Earth, Earth to Water, Water to Fire, Fire to Metal, Metal to WoodFriction and pull. Growth through challenge.
Same elementWood and Wood, Fire and Fire, and so onA mirror. Instant understanding, shared blind spots.
