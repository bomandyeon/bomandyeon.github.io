Why a Sword type ex keeps coming back (and when they never do)

The Sword (Gyeong Metal 庚): Blunt, loyal and protective. Says the hard thing out loud, forgives slowly, and shows love by fixing your problems.

Short answer

A Sword type ex comes back when they feel the breakup was unfinished business, usually directly and without games, but never if they felt betrayed, because for a Sword trust broken once is a door welded shut.

Nobody can tell you whether one particular person will come back, and Bom and Yeon won't pretend to. What a birthday type can do is explain the pattern: how this person tends to leave, what tends to pull them back, and what to do with the phone in your hand.

Why a Sword ex comes back (or doesn't)

The Sword hates loose ends. If the relationship ended in a fight they did not get to finish, or over something they think was fixable, they often return with a plain message: 'I think we ended badly. Can we talk?' No hints, no story replies. Swords say what they want.

What keeps them away is betrayal. A lie, a broken promise, being made a fool of in public. Swords can forgive bluntness, distance and even bad timing, but they rarely forgive a breach of trust. If that was why it ended, the silence is final and it is not a strategy.

BomUnder the blunt message is someone who does not give up on people easily. That is loyalty, in armour.

What it looks like when they reach out

  • A direct message proposing a conversation, often with a time and place.
  • Showing up to solve a real problem: a legal letter, a moving van, a broken lock.
  • Defending you in front of others even after the breakup.
  • Telling you, bluntly, what they think went wrong.

What to do with the message

Answer as directly as they asked. Swords respect a clear yes or no and lose patience with 'maybe, let me think' that drags on for weeks. If you meet, expect honesty that stings a little and give honesty back. If you do not want to reopen it, say so in one sentence. They will respect it more than kindness wrapped in fog.

If you are the Sword

If you are the Sword, being right about why it ended is not the same as being close again. Lead with what you missed, not with your analysis.

YeonThey will send 'we should talk' like it is a meeting invite. Bring an agenda and a box of tissues.

The Metal pattern

Metal types cut cleanly. The Sword is blunt and direct, the Jewel cool and polished. Both treat trust as the line that cannot be crossed, and both are very hard to move once they decide.

FAQ

Do Sword type exes come back?

Swords often return if they feel things ended unfairly or unfinished, and they do it directly. Saju cannot predict one person's choice, but betrayal is the line most Swords do not cross back over.

Why is my Sword ex so cold after the breakup?

Coldness is how a Sword protects a wound. They cut cleanly to stop the bleeding. It does not mean they felt nothing.

Should I contact my Sword ex?

If you do, be direct and specific. Swords respond to clarity. Vague or emotional hints usually get a short, puzzled reply.
