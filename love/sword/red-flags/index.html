Sword red flags: honesty with sharp edges

The Sword (Gyeong Metal 庚): Blunt, loyal and protective. Says the hard thing out loud, forgives slowly, and shows love by fixing your problems.

Short answer

The main Sword red flags are brutal honesty that wounds, judging people on a single mistake, and needing to win every argument; their loyalty is fierce, but so is their edge.

Every birthday type has red flags, including yours. The useful question is which ones are habits you can talk about and which ones wear a relationship down. Yeon wrote the list. Bom made her add the green flags.

The red flags, one by one

🚩 Brutal 'honesty'

What it looks like: They say something harsh and call it being real.

What it usually means: Directness without care. Ask them to aim for honest and kind.

🚩 One-strike judgments

What it looks like: One late reply and they decide you are untrustworthy.

What it usually means: Fear of betrayal. Watch whether they can give second chances.

🚩 Winning the argument

What it looks like: They win the fight and lose the evening.

What it usually means: Pride. Can they say 'I was wrong'?

🚩 Protective to controlling

What it looks like: Protecting you starts to feel like managing you.

What it usually means: The line to watch. Protection should leave you free.

YeonIf every argument has a winner and it is always them, you are not in a relationship, you are in a tournament.

Green flags that balance it

  • They defend you when you are not in the room.
  • They tell you the truth when nobody else will.
  • They are loyal once they choose you.

BomTheir honesty is a form of respect. Help them add some softness to the delivery.

The Metal pattern

Metal red flags are sharp edges: harsh honesty and one-strike judgments from the Sword, invisible standards and polite coldness from the Jewel.

FAQ

Is a Sword a red flag partner?

No. Swords are loyal, honest and protective. Their risk is harshness and rigid judgment, which matter most in how they handle conflict.

What is the biggest Sword red flag?

Judging people on a single mistake. It can make a relationship feel like walking on glass.

How do I date a Sword?

Be honest, be direct and push back when they are harsh. Swords respect people who can stand their ground.
