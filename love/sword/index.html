The Sword in love

Blunt, loyal and protective. Says the hard thing out loud, forgives slowly, and shows love by fixing your problems.

Short answer

The Sword is the Korean birthday type of people born on a yang Metal day (Gyeong Metal, 庚). In love: blunt, loyal and protective. Says the hard thing out loud, forgives slowly, and shows love by fixing your problems.
