How a Sword acts with a crush: direct, protective, slightly scary

The Sword (Gyeong Metal 庚): Blunt, loyal and protective. Says the hard thing out loud, forgives slowly, and shows love by fixing your problems.

Short answer

A Sword with a crush usually tells you directly or shows it through protection, like defending you, solving your problems and staying close, all while looking completely calm.

Some people flirt like a firework. Others flirt like a spreadsheet. If you know someone's birthday type, you know which signals to look for, and which ones you have been missing because they did not look like flirting at all.

Signs a Sword likes you

  • They ask you out plainly, with a day and time.
  • They get protective when someone is rude to you.
  • They offer blunt advice about your life, which is them caring.
  • They seem cold at first, then show up with exactly what you needed.

BomA Sword's crush is like having a bodyguard who also remembers your coffee order.

How to catch a Sword's attention

  • Be honest and confident. Swords like people who do not crumble.
  • Disagree with them sometimes. They respect pushback.
  • Say yes or no clearly. They hate games.

If you are the Sword with the crush

If you are the Sword with the crush, your directness is a gift. Just add one soft sentence so they know it is affection and not an interview.

YeonThey asked you out like they were booking a meeting room. Romantic, in their way.

The Metal pattern

Metal flirts with precision. The Sword asks you out plainly; the Jewel watches to see if you will make a real effort first.

FAQ

How do I know if a Sword likes me?

They will probably tell you. If not, look for protection, blunt advice and showing up when you need help.

Do Swords make the first move?

Often, yes. They are one of the more direct types and prefer to state their interest.

What attracts a Sword?

Honesty, confidence and loyalty. They like partners who can handle directness and push back.
