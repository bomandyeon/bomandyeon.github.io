Why a Sword stops talking to you: the clean cut

The Sword (Gyeong Metal 庚): Blunt, loyal and protective. Says the hard thing out loud, forgives slowly, and shows love by fixing your problems.

Short answer

A Sword who stops talking has usually decided you broke their trust, even over something that seemed small to you; it is a clean cut rather than a game, and they rarely reopen it without a sincere apology.

Silence means different things from different people. For some types it is a goodbye, for others a sulk, for others a nap that lasted four days. This page reads the silence through the birthday type, so you can tell a pause from an ending and send one sensible message instead of nine.

Why a Sword goes quiet

Swords decide fast. A late reply, an inconsistency, a white lie: any of these can make a Sword decide someone is untrustworthy. They do not argue about it. They cut, cleanly, and move on.

This can feel brutal. But from the Sword's side, it is honesty: they do not want to pretend things are fine. If they did not explain, it is often because they believed the reason was obvious.

If you send one message, send this

'I think I let you down. I am not going to make excuses. I am sorry, and I would like to hear your side if you are open to it.'

Send it once. If nothing comes back, that is an answer too, and you did not spend a month guessing.

If you are the Sword

If you are the Sword, a clean cut feels honest to you. To them it is a cliff with no warning sign. Say why, even in one line.

The Metal pattern

Metal silence is a cut, not a fade. The Sword cuts after a breach of trust; the Jewel goes politely cold after something careless. Both expect you to know why.

FAQ

Do Sword types ghost?

They cut contact rather than fade, usually after feeling betrayed. It is a decision, not drama.

Will a Sword come back after cutting contact?

Rarely without a sincere, direct apology. Saju cannot predict what a specific person will do, and pushing hard usually makes it worse.

Why didn't my Sword explain?

Many Swords think the reason is obvious. Asking once, directly and calmly, is the best way to get an answer.
