The Sword in a situationship: why they say exactly what they want

The Sword (Gyeong Metal 庚): Blunt, loyal and protective. Says the hard thing out loud, forgives slowly, and shows love by fixing your problems.

Short answer

A Sword rarely stays in a situationship by accident; if things are undefined, it is usually because they are not sure yet, and they will often tell you so plainly if you ask.

A situationship is the space between 'just talking' and 'together', and every birthday type gets stuck there for a different reason. Here is how this one behaves in the grey zone, the tells to look for, and words that tend to get a straight answer.

How a Sword does 'undefined'

Swords do not flirt much. They state. 'I like you. Dinner Thursday?' That directness means most Swords do not drift into situationships the way softer types do. If a Sword is keeping it undefined, they are usually honest about why: bad timing, a recent breakup, uncertainty.

The problem is that their honesty can be brutal, and their actions can contradict it. A Sword may say 'I am not looking for anything serious' while protecting you, fixing things and texting daily. They believe the words; the behaviour says something else. Ask them to look at the gap.

Yeon'I'm not looking for anything serious,' says the person who changed your tyre in the rain. Sure.

The tells

  • They said clearly that they 'don't want anything serious' but act like a partner.
  • They get protective when someone treats you badly.
  • They say what they think, even when it is not romantic.
  • They respect you more when you push back.

Lines that get a straight answer

  • 'You said you did not want anything serious. Your actions say otherwise. Which is it?'
  • 'I need an honest answer, and I know you can give one.'
  • 'If this is not going anywhere, tell me straight. I can handle it.'

BomA Sword who keeps showing up is not confused about you. They are confused about timing.

If you are the Sword

If you are the Sword, your honesty is a strength, but your actions also make promises. Check that what you say and what you do tell the same story.

The Metal pattern

Metal likes clarity. The Sword will usually tell you exactly where things stand; the Jewel expects you to show it, clearly and out loud. Neither enjoys 'let's see where it goes' for long.

FAQ

Do Swords end up in situationships?

Less often than most types, because they tend to state what they want. When they do, it usually involves timing or uncertainty they are willing to talk about.

How do I get a clear answer from a Sword?

Ask a direct question. Swords respect directness and usually answer honestly, even if it stings.

Is a Sword's bluntness a sign they don't care?

No. Bluntness is how they show respect. A Sword who bothers to be honest with you usually cares.
