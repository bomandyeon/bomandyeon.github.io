Ghosting, read by birthday type

Why a Jewel goes cold: the polite ghost

The Jewel (Sin Metal 辛): Elegant, sensitive and hard to forget. Wants to be chosen clearly and out loud, and goes politely cold when hurt.

Short answer

A Jewel rarely ghosts completely; they go politely cold, replying with perfect manners and no warmth, which usually means you hurt them with something careless.

Silence means different things from different people. For some types it is a goodbye, for others a sulk, for others a nap that lasted four days. This page reads the silence through the birthday type, so you can tell a pause from an ending and send one sensible message instead of nine.

Why a Jewel goes quiet

Jewels go cold and polite when hurt. They will still reply, with impeccable manners, but the warmth is gone. It is a signal: something you did or said landed badly.

True ghosting happens when they feel deeply disrespected. They decide you are not worth explaining to, and they leave with their dignity intact.

If you send one message, send this

'I feel like I was careless with you. I am sorry. I would like to understand what I got wrong.'

Send it once. If nothing comes back, that is an answer too, and you did not spend a month guessing.

If you are the Jewel

If you are the Jewel, polite coldness is very hard to read. People feel the chill but not the reason. Name the reason.

The Metal pattern

Metal silence is a cut, not a fade. The Sword cuts after a breach of trust; the Jewel goes politely cold after something careless. Both expect you to know why.

FAQ

Do Jewel types ghost?

They more often go politely cold. Full ghosting usually follows feeling disrespected.

How do I fix things with a cold Jewel?

A specific, sincere apology. Jewels respond to people who notice what they did and own it.

Why won't my Jewel tell me what's wrong?

Explaining feels like lowering their standards. Asking gently and specifically helps.
