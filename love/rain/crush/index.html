How a Rain acts with a crush: quiet attention, deep questions

The Rain (Gye Water 癸): Intuitive, gentle and quietly perceptive. Senses what is wrong before anyone says it, and analyses text tone like a contract.

Short answer

A Rain with a crush notices everything about you, asks deep and gentle questions, and stays close quietly, hoping you will sense what they rarely say out loud.

Some people flirt like a firework. Others flirt like a spreadsheet. If you know someone's birthday type, you know which signals to look for, and which ones you have been missing because they did not look like flirting at all.

Signs a Rain likes you

  • They remember small emotional details: what made you sad, what you fear.
  • They ask questions that go below the surface.
  • They are calm and soft around you.
  • They seem to know how you are feeling before you say it.

BomIf a Rain has noticed you, you have been seen with unusual care.

How to catch a Rain's attention

  • Talk about real things. Rain types fall for emotional depth.
  • Be gentle and consistent. They notice tone.
  • Say what you feel. They will not always ask.

If you are the Rain with the crush

If you are the Rain with the crush, they cannot sense what you feel as well as you can sense theirs. Say it, gently, once.

YeonThey know your favourite song, your childhood fear and your tea order. They have not said 'I like you'. Help them out.

The Water pattern

Water flirts through depth. Deep questions, late conversations and the feeling that someone has seen something in you nobody else has.

FAQ

How do I know if a Rain likes me?

Look for quiet attention and deep questions. Their interest is gentle, not loud.

Do Rain types make the first move?

Rarely. They hope you will notice. A gentle signal from you helps.

What attracts a Rain?

Emotional depth, kindness and consistency. They want someone who can share real fears and soft spots.
