Ghosting, read by birthday type

Why a Rain ghosts so gently you barely notice

The Rain (Gye Water 癸): Intuitive, gentle and quietly perceptive. Senses what is wrong before anyone says it, and analyses text tone like a contract.

Short answer

A Rain ghosts softly, fading so gently that you may think it is still going, because a direct goodbye feels too harsh for them and they hope you will understand without words.

Silence means different things from different people. For some types it is a goodbye, for others a sulk, for others a nap that lasted four days. This page reads the silence through the birthday type, so you can tell a pause from an ending and send one sensible message instead of nine.

Why a Rain goes quiet

The Rain knows when something is not working, often before the other person. Instead of confronting it, they slowly fade: fewer replies, softer messages, longer gaps.

It feels kind to them, and in a way it is, but it can leave the other person confused. The Rain hopes you will understand without needing an explanation.

If you send one message, send this

'I have noticed we are drifting. I would really like to know how you feel, even if it is hard to say.'

Send it once. If nothing comes back, that is an answer too, and you did not spend a month guessing.

If you are the Rain

If you are the Rain, gentle fading feels kind, but the other person keeps hoping. A clear, soft goodbye is kinder than rain that never stops.

The Water pattern

Water drifts rather than slams. The Ocean disappears to recharge; the Rain fades so gently you barely notice. Both make silence harder to read than most.

FAQ

Do Rain types ghost?

They fade softly more than they vanish. It feels gentle to them, but it can leave the other person confused.

How do I tell if a Rain is drifting?

Look for gradually softer, less frequent replies. A direct, gentle question helps.

Can I reconnect with a Rain who faded?

Sometimes. A sincere, gentle message has the best chance. Saju cannot predict a specific outcome.
