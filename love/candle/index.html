Yin Fire · Jeong Fire 丁

The Candle in love

Quiet, deep and intensely loyal. Falls rarely and completely, reads meaning into everything, and remembers how you made them feel.

Short answer

The Candle is the Korean birthday type of people born on a yin Fire day (Jeong Fire, 丁). In love: quiet, deep and intensely loyal. Falls rarely and completely, reads meaning into everything, and remembers how you made them feel.

Ex

Why a Candle ex keeps a flame burning for you

A Candle ex often holds on to feelings long after the breakup, because they fall rarely and deeply; if they reach out, it is usually a quiet, carefully written message rather than a dramatic return.

Situationship

The Candle in a situationship: deep talks, no definition

A Candle in a situationship feels emotionally committed long before it is official, so the undefined status hurts them more than they admit and usually more than the other person realises.

Read it

Ghosting

Why a Candle goes quiet after a small thing

A Candle who suddenly goes quiet is usually hurt by something small you may not have noticed, like a careless comment, and is waiting to see if you will care enough to ask.

Read it

Red flags

Candle red flags: deep feelings, silent assumptions

The main Candle red flags are assuming what you meant without asking, holding on to old flames, and giving everything to one person; they come from depth and sensitivity, not coldness.

Crush

How a Candle acts with a crush: quietly, intensely, unmistakably

A Candle with a crush watches more than they talk, remembers everything you say, and shows it through small, personal gestures like a song, a note or a perfectly chosen gift.
