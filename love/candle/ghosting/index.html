Ghosting, read by birthday type

Why a Candle goes quiet after a small thing

The Candle (Jeong Fire 丁): Quiet, deep and intensely loyal. Falls rarely and completely, reads meaning into everything, and remembers how you made them feel.

Short answer

A Candle who suddenly goes quiet is usually hurt by something small you may not have noticed, like a careless comment, and is waiting to see if you will care enough to ask.

Silence means different things from different people. For some types it is a goodbye, for others a sulk, for others a nap that lasted four days. This page reads the silence through the birthday type, so you can tell a pause from an ending and send one sensible message instead of nine.

Why a Candle goes quiet

Candles are more sensitive than they look. A joke that landed wrong, a forgotten detail, a reply that felt cold: they absorb it, smile, change the subject, and then pull back to protect the flame.

Real ghosting from a Candle happens when they decide you cannot be trusted with their depth. That decision can be quiet and final, and they rarely explain it, because explaining would mean being vulnerable to the same person again.

Send it once. If nothing comes back, that is an answer too, and you did not spend a month guessing.

If you are the Candle

If you are the Candle, the other person probably has no idea which sentence hurt you. Telling them is not drama. It is the only way they can do better.

The Fire pattern

When Fire goes dark, it is usually because it ran out of fuel: no warmth coming back (the Sun) or a hurt nobody noticed (the Candle). Fire rarely goes cold without a reason it thinks is obvious.

FAQ

Do Candle types ghost?

Sometimes, when they feel unsafe being vulnerable. More often they go quiet after being hurt and wait to see if you notice.

How do I fix things with a quiet Candle?

Ask sincerely and specifically. Candles respond to someone who cares enough to wonder what went wrong.

Why didn't my Candle tell me what was wrong?

Saying it means risking more hurt. Many Candles hope you will notice instead. That is a pattern to talk about, kindly, once things settle.
