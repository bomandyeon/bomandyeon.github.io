Red flags, read by birthday type

Candle red flags: deep feelings, silent assumptions

The Candle (Jeong Fire 丁): Quiet, deep and intensely loyal. Falls rarely and completely, reads meaning into everything, and remembers how you made them feel.

Short answer

The main Candle red flags are assuming what you meant without asking, holding on to old flames, and giving everything to one person; they come from depth and sensitivity, not coldness.

Every birthday type has red flags, including yours. The useful question is which ones are habits you can talk about and which ones wear a relationship down. Yeon wrote the list. Bom made her add the green flags.

The red flags, one by one

🚩 Mind-reading

What it looks like: They decide what you meant and react to that, not to what you said.

What it usually means: Sensitivity. Encourage checking before reacting.

🚩 The eternal flame

What it looks like: An ex still lives rent-free in their head years later.

What it usually means: Loyalty to a memory. Fine if it stays a memory.

🚩 All eggs, one basket

What it looks like: Their world shrinks to you; friends fade.

What it usually means: Intensity. Healthy love leaves room for other people.

🚩 The quiet wound

What it looks like: Hurt shows up as distance, never words.

What it usually means: Watch whether they can learn to say it out loud.

YeonIf they are hurt by what you meant instead of what you said, you are dating their imagination too.

Green flags that balance it

  • They remember the things that matter to you.
  • They are fiercely loyal once they trust you.
  • Their care is personal and thoughtful, never generic.

BomBeing loved by a Candle is like being remembered forever. Just make sure they can also tell you things.

The Fire pattern

Fire red flags are about heat: too much too soon from the Sun, too much meaning read into small things by the Candle.

FAQ

Is a Candle a red flag partner?

No. Candles are loyal and deep. Their risk is reading too much into things. Clear communication fixes most of it.

What is the biggest Candle red flag?

Deciding what you meant without asking, then pulling away. It is fixable if they learn to check first.

How do I date a Candle well?

Be consistent and gentle with your words, ask how they are actually doing, and leave room for them to keep their own friends.
