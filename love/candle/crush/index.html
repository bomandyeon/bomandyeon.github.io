Crushes, read by birthday type

How a Candle acts with a crush: quietly, intensely, unmistakably

The Candle (Jeong Fire 丁): Quiet, deep and intensely loyal. Falls rarely and completely, reads meaning into everything, and remembers how you made them feel.

Short answer

A Candle with a crush watches more than they talk, remembers everything you say, and shows it through small, personal gestures like a song, a note or a perfectly chosen gift.

Some people flirt like a firework. Others flirt like a spreadsheet. If you know someone's birthday type, you know which signals to look for, and which ones you have been missing because they did not look like flirting at all.

Signs a Candle likes you

  • They listen intensely and remember tiny details.
  • They send you songs that seem to say something.
  • They are warmer one-on-one than in groups.
  • They give a small gift that is exactly right.

BomIf a Candle chose you, you are seen in a way most people never are.

How to catch a Candle's attention

  • Talk about real things. Candles fall for depth, not banter alone.
  • Be gentle with your jokes. They remember the careless ones.
  • Create quiet one-on-one time. A Candle opens up in candlelight, not at parties.

If you are the Candle with the crush

If you are the Candle with the crush, they may not know the songs were for them. One honest sentence is worth twelve hidden ones.

The Fire pattern

Fire is easy to spot if you know the style. The Sun lights up the whole room for you. The Candle lights one small, perfect corner.

FAQ

How do I know if a Candle likes me?

Look for focused attention, remembered details and personal gestures. They show feelings through care, not loud flirting.

Do Candles make the first move?

Not often directly. They give hints and hope. A gentle, sincere signal from you makes it easier.

What attracts a Candle?

Depth, kindness and consistency. They want someone who can sit in silence and still feel like company.
