Why a Tall Pine goes quiet, and whether it is ghosting

The Tall Pine (Gap Wood 甲): Loyal, proud and steady. Protects first, talks later, and would rather fix your problem than say how they feel.

Short answer

A Tall Pine who goes silent is usually wounded and waiting for an apology, not disappearing for good; real ghosting from this type is rare and happens when they have lost respect for the situation.

Silence means different things from different people. For some types it is a goodbye, for others a sulk, for others a nap that lasted four days. This page reads the silence through the birthday type, so you can tell a pause from an ending and send one sensible message instead of nine.

Why a Tall Pine goes quiet

The Tall Pine's main move when hurt is to stand still. They do not argue, they withdraw. In their head the silence is a dignified pause; on your phone it looks exactly like being ghosted.

Full ghosting, the kind with no explanation ever, happens mostly early on, before they feel they owe you a conversation, or after a moment where they felt humiliated. Past the first few weeks, a Tall Pine usually believes in a proper ending, even if it comes late.

If you send one message, send this

Send it once. If nothing comes back, that is an answer too, and you did not spend a month guessing.

If you are the Tall Pine

If you are the Tall Pine, silence feels like restraint to you. To everyone else it is a closed door with no sign on it. A one-line 'I need a few days' keeps your dignity and their sanity.

The Wood pattern

Wood does not snap; it bends or stands still. That is why Wood silences tend to be pauses, hurt or hesitant, more often than clean exits.

FAQ

Do Tall Pine types ghost people?

Less than most. They prefer a formal ending. What looks like ghosting is often a long, hurt silence that they intend to resolve eventually.

How long does a Tall Pine stay silent?

There is no fixed timer. Saju describes tendencies, not schedules. A Tall Pine stays quiet until they feel their pride is safe, which a calm, direct message usually speeds up.

Should I wait or reach out?

Reach out once, clearly and without blame. If you get nothing back, you have your answer and you did not spend weeks guessing.
