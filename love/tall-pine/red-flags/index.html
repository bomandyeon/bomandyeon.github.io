Tall Pine red flags in dating, and which ones you can live with

The Tall Pine (Gap Wood 甲): Loyal, proud and steady. Protects first, talks later, and would rather fix your problem than say how they feel.

Short answer

The main Tall Pine red flags are pride that refuses to apologise, advice where comfort was needed, and silence used as punishment; the first two soften with trust, the third is the one to watch.

Every birthday type has red flags, including yours. The useful question is which ones are habits you can talk about and which ones wear a relationship down. Yeon wrote the list. Bom made her add the green flags.

The red flags, one by one

🚩 Never apologising first

What it looks like: Arguments end with both of you waiting, and you always crack first.

What it usually means: Pride, not contempt. Fine if they repair later, a problem if they never do.

🚩 Solving instead of listening

What it looks like: You share a bad day, you get a five-step plan.

What it usually means: Their way of caring. Easy to fix by saying 'I just need you to listen tonight'.

🚩 Silent treatment

What it looks like: Days of cold politeness after a small slight.

What it usually means: The one to take seriously. Withdrawal as punishment wears a relationship down.

🚩 Needing to be the tallest tree

What it looks like: They get prickly when you outshine them in public.

What it usually means: Insecurity in a strong costume. Watch whether they can celebrate your wins.

Green flags that balance it

  • They keep promises, including small boring ones.
  • They defend you when you are not in the room.
  • When they finally apologise, they mean it and change something.

BomThe loyalty is real. You just have to tell them when you need a hug instead of a plan.

The Wood pattern

Wood red flags are mostly about growth gone sideways: pride that will not bend (Tall Pine) or bending so much there is no shape left (Climbing Flower).

FAQ

Is a Tall Pine a red flag partner?

No type is a red flag by itself. The Tall Pine's risks are pride and withdrawal; their strengths are loyalty and follow-through. Judge the person by how they repair after conflict.

What is the biggest Tall Pine red flag?

Silence used as punishment. Needing space is healthy; making someone earn back your attention with days of coldness is not.

Can a Tall Pine change?

People change habits, not birthdays. A Tall Pine who names their pride and practises apologising first is very good to be with.
