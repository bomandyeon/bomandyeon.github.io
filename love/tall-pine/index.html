The Tall Pine in love

Yang Wood · Gap Wood 甲

Loyal, proud and steady. Protects first, talks later, and would rather fix your problem than say how they feel.

The Tall Pine is the Korean birthday type of people born on a yang Wood day (Gap Wood, 甲). In love: loyal, proud and steady. Protects first, talks later, and would rather fix your problem than say how they feel.

Why a Tall Pine Ex Is So Hard to Get Over

A Tall Pine ex rarely comes back fast, because pride makes them wait for you to move first; when they do return, it is usually months later and dressed up as a practical favour.

The Tall Pine in a situationship: steady, kind, and somehow still undefined

A Tall Pine in a situationship usually acts like a partner (reliable, protective, present) while avoiding the label, because they want to be sure before they commit and hate taking back a promise.

Why a Tall Pine goes quiet, and whether it is ghosting

A Tall Pine who goes silent is usually wounded and waiting for an apology, not disappearing for good; real ghosting from this type is rare and happens when they have lost respect for the situation.

Tall Pine red flags in dating, and which ones you can live with

The main Tall Pine red flags are pride that refuses to apologise, advice where comfort was needed, and silence used as punishment; the first two soften with trust, the third is the one to watch.

How a Tall Pine acts with a crush (spoiler: like your helpful neighbour)

A Tall Pine with a crush shows it by helping, not flirting: they fix things for you, remember details, and stay near you while acting as if nothing is going on.
