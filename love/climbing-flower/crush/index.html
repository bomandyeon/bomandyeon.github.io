How a Climbing Flower acts with a crush: questions, questions, questions

The Climbing Flower (Eul Wood 乙): Sweet, adaptable and quietly unstoppable. Bends to fit, remembers everything, and finds a way around every wall.

Short answer

A Climbing Flower with a crush flirts by asking you questions and remembering every answer, then bringing up a tiny detail weeks later that makes you feel completely seen.

Some people flirt like a firework. Others flirt like a spreadsheet. If you know someone's birthday type, you know which signals to look for, and which ones you have been missing because they did not look like flirting at all.

Signs a Climbing Flower likes you

  • They ask follow-up questions about things you mentioned once.
  • They mirror your taste: your playlist, your coffee, your slang.
  • They laugh at your jokes a beat too long.
  • They find small reasons to be near you without making it obvious.

BomBeing remembered by a Climbing Flower is a whole experience. Enjoy it.

How to catch a Climbing Flower's attention

  • Be steady and clear. Climbing Flowers fall for people who do not play games.
  • Ask about them too, and remember the answer. They notice who notices.
  • Name a specific plan. They are more likely to say yes to Thursday at seven than to 'sometime'.

If you are the Climbing Flower with the crush

If you are the Climbing Flower with the crush, your attention is wonderful, but they may think you are just this lovely with everyone. Let one sentence be unmistakable.

The Wood pattern

Wood flirts through attention and help. If someone keeps nudging your life forward, fixing, asking, remembering, a Wood type may be quietly courting you.

FAQ

How do I know if a Climbing Flower likes me?

Look for remembered details and genuine questions. They are attentive to everyone, but with a crush the attention becomes specific and personal.

Do Climbing Flowers make the first move?

Usually indirectly. They create chances to be near you and hope you take the hint. A clear invitation from you helps a lot.

What does a Climbing Flower want in a partner?

Steadiness, clarity and kindness. They like someone strong enough to lean on and gentle enough not to crush the flowers.
