Does a Climbing Flower ghost? The soft fade, explained

The Climbing Flower (Eul Wood 乙): Sweet, adaptable and quietly unstoppable. Bends to fit, remembers everything, and finds a way around every wall.

Short answer

A Climbing Flower rarely ghosts abruptly; they fade, replying a little later and a little lighter each time, because a direct goodbye feels too harsh to them.

Silence means different things from different people. For some types it is a goodbye, for others a sulk, for others a nap that lasted four days. This page reads the silence through the birthday type, so you can tell a pause from an ending and send one sensible message instead of nine.

Why a Climbing Flower goes quiet

Hurting someone's feelings is close to unbearable for this type, so instead of ending things clearly they let the replies get shorter. A heart instead of an answer. 'So busy lately!' A week, then two.

Sometimes the fade is a test. The Climbing Flower pulls back slightly to see whether you will follow. If you do not, they take it as proof you did not care, even though they never said anything was wrong.

If you send one message, send this

'You seem a bit further away lately. I would rather have an honest answer than guess, whatever it is.'

Send it once. If nothing comes back, that is an answer too, and you did not spend a month guessing.

If you are the Climbing Flower

If you are the Climbing Flower, a fade feels kinder to you than a goodbye. It is usually crueller, because it leaves the other person hoping. Two honest sentences are the gentle option.

The Wood pattern

Wood does not snap; it bends or stands still. That is why Wood silences tend to be pauses, hurt or hesitant, more often than clean exits.

FAQ

Do Climbing Flower types ghost?

They are more likely to fade than to vanish. The replies thin out gradually. A direct, kind question usually gets you an honest answer.

Is my Climbing Flower testing me?

Possibly. This type sometimes withdraws to see if you notice. Saying 'I noticed you pulled back, are we okay?' passes the test without playing the game.

How do I stop a slow fade?

Name it. You cannot force someone to stay, but you can stop the guessing by asking once, clearly.
