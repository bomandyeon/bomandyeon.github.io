The Climbing Flower is the Korean birthday type of people born on a yin Wood day (Eul Wood, 乙). In love: sweet, adaptable and quietly unstoppable. Bends to fit, remembers everything, and finds a way around every wall.

Why a Climbing Flower Ex Is So Hard to Get Over

A Climbing Flower ex tends to drift back gently (a story reply, a ‘this reminded me of you’) because they adapt instead of cutting ties, and they hold on to connection long after the relationship ends.
