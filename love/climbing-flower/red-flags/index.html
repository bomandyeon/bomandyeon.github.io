Climbing Flower red flags: sweetness with a long memory

The Climbing Flower (Eul Wood 乙): Sweet, adaptable and quietly unstoppable. Bends to fit, remembers everything, and finds a way around every wall.

Short answer

The main Climbing Flower red flags are hiding what they want, keeping a quiet list of disappointments, and testing you with distance; all three come from fear of conflict, not malice.

Every birthday type has red flags, including yours. The useful question is which ones are habits you can talk about and which ones wear a relationship down. Yeon wrote the list. Bom made her add the green flags.

The red flags, one by one

🚩 'Whatever you want'

What it looks like: They agree to plans, then resent them later.

What it usually means: Fear of being a burden. Ask twice and wait for the real answer.

🚩 The silent ledger

What it looks like: A forgotten text from March comes up in a fight in October.

What it usually means: They stored it instead of saying it. Ask for small honesty often.

🚩 Distance tests

What it looks like: They go cool to see if you will chase.

What it usually means: Insecurity. Fine once, tiring as a habit.

🚩 Shape-shifting

What it looks like: Their taste, plans and opinions start matching yours.

What it usually means: Watch that they still have a self in the relationship.

Green flags that balance it

  • They notice what you need before you say it.
  • They are loyal to friends and remember everyone's birthdays.
  • Once they feel safe, they become surprisingly direct and funny.

The Wood pattern

Wood red flags are mostly about growth gone sideways: pride that will not bend (Tall Pine) or bending so much there is no shape left (Climbing Flower).

FAQ

Is the Climbing Flower a red flag?

No. Their red flags are about avoiding conflict. With a partner who makes honesty safe, they are one of the most attentive types to be with.

What is the biggest Climbing Flower red flag?

The silent ledger: storing hurts instead of mentioning them. It turns small issues into big fights later.

How do I handle a Climbing Flower's tests?

Do not play along or punish them. Name what you notice and reassure once. Then ask them to tell you directly next time.
