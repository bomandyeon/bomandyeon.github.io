How a Sun acts with a crush: everyone knows except you

The Sun (Byeong Fire 丙): Warm, loud and generous. Loves big and fast, hates being ignored, and lights up every room including the group chat.

Short answer

A Sun with a crush is obvious to everyone around them: they light up when you arrive, text you first, and make plans with you at the speed of a group chat.

Some people flirt like a firework. Others flirt like a spreadsheet. If you know someone's birthday type, you know which signals to look for, and which ones you have been missing because they did not look like flirting at all.

Signs a Sun likes you

  • They text first, often, and with a lot of exclamation marks.
  • They tell their friends about you, and their friends act weird around you.
  • They invite you to everything, including things you are not sure you were meant for.
  • They compliment you in public, loudly.

BomA Sun's crush is the warmest place to stand. Enjoy the light.

How to catch a Sun's attention

  • Receive the warmth and give some back. Suns need to feel their attention lands.
  • Be a little hard to read, not cold. A tiny bit of mystery keeps a Sun curious.
  • Say yes to the spontaneous plan. Suns fall for people who can keep up.

If you are the Sun with the crush

If you are the Sun with the crush, you probably already told three people. Consider telling the one who matters, but give them room to answer at their own speed.

YeonIf you are wondering whether a Sun likes you, ask the barista. Everyone else already knows.

The Fire pattern

Fire is easy to spot if you know the style. The Sun lights up the whole room for you. The Candle lights one small, perfect corner.

FAQ

How do I know if a Sun likes me?

It will be visible: frequent texts, invitations, public compliments, and a lot of attention in group settings. Suns rarely hide a crush.

Do Suns make the first move?

Often, yes. They are among the most likely types to confess or ask you out directly.

What does a Sun want in a partner?

Someone who enjoys their warmth and gives some back, can keep up with plans, and stays steady when the Sun gets restless.
