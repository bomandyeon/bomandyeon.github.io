The Sun in love

Warm, loud and generous. Loves big and fast, hates being ignored, and lights up every room including the group chat.

Short answer

The Sun is the Korean birthday type of people born on a yang Fire day (Byeong Fire, 丙). In love: warm, loud and generous. Loves big and fast, hates being ignored, and lights up every room including the group chat.

Ex

Why a Sun ex comes back loudly, or not at all

A Sun ex tends to come back quickly and openly if they still feel warmth, or moves on visibly and fast if they feel unappreciated; there is rarely a quiet middle.

Why a Sun stops texting: when the brightest person goes dark

Sun red flags: when warmth turns into weather

The main Sun red flags are confusing intensity with compatibility, creating drama when life gets calm, and needing constant attention; their warmth is real, but so is the sunburn.
