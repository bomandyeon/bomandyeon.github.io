Ghosting, read by birthday type

Why a Sun stops texting: when the brightest person goes dark

The Sun (Byeong Fire 丙): Warm, loud and generous. Loves big and fast, hates being ignored, and lights up every room including the group chat.

Short answer

A Sun going quiet usually means they felt unappreciated or got swept into something new; it is rarely cold calculation, and they often reappear with an enthusiastic explanation.

Silence means different things from different people. For some types it is a goodbye, for others a sulk, for others a nap that lasted four days. This page reads the silence through the birthday type, so you can tell a pause from an ending and send one sensible message instead of nine.

Why a Sun goes quiet

Suns are generous but not endless. If they shine and shine and get little back (late replies, flat reactions, plans always on them) they eventually stop, and the silence feels sudden because their attention was so loud before.

The other reason is distraction. A Sun's life is full of people and plans, and a new excitement can take over. That is not an excuse, but it means the silence is often less personal than it feels.

Send it once. If nothing comes back, that is an answer too, and you did not spend a month guessing.

If you are the Sun

If you are the Sun, the person you went quiet on remembers the warmth, which makes the silence colder. A short 'I am not in the right place' is more generous than vanishing.

The Fire pattern

When Fire goes dark, it is usually because it ran out of fuel: no warmth coming back (the Sun) or a hurt nobody noticed (the Candle). Fire rarely goes cold without a reason it thinks is obvious.

FAQ

Do Sun types ghost?

They can, especially early on if the spark fades. More often they go quiet because they feel unappreciated or overwhelmed, and come back later.

Will a Sun come back after ghosting?

Suns often resurface with an explanation. Saju cannot tell you if a particular person will. Decide whether their explanation is enough for you.

What should I text a Sun who went quiet?

Something warm and light. Suns respond to warmth far better than to guilt or long accusations.
