Ghosting, read by birthday type

Why an Ocean disappears for days (and whether they are gone)

The Ocean (Im Water 壬): Free, curious and magnetic. Falls fast, dives deep, then needs air. Hates labels that feel like paperwork.

Short answer

An Ocean disappearing for a few days is usually them recharging, not ghosting; but if they go quiet right after things got serious, it can be a sign they feel trapped.

Silence means different things from different people. For some types it is a goodbye, for others a sulk, for others a nap that lasted four days. This page reads the silence through the birthday type, so you can tell a pause from an ending and send one sensible message instead of nine.

Why a Ocean goes quiet

Oceans need air. They will disappear for three days to recharge and forget to mention it first. That is not malice; it is how they restore themselves.

Real ghosting from an Ocean tends to happen when they feel trapped, usually right after a big step: meeting your friends, a 'what are we' talk, a holiday plan. If they do not resurface after a week or two, they might be drifting away.

Send it once. If nothing comes back, that is an answer too, and you did not spend a month guessing.

The Water pattern

Water drifts rather than slams. The Ocean disappears to recharge; the Rain fades so gently you barely notice. Both make silence harder to read than most.

FAQ

Do Ocean types ghost?

They disappear to recharge often, which can feel like ghosting. Long silences after big steps are more worrying.

How long does an Ocean disappear?

Saju cannot give a number. Many Oceans resurface within days. If it stretches into weeks, it is worth a direct question.

What should I say to an Ocean who disappeared?

Something light and honest. Pressure pushes them further away; calm honesty brings them back.
