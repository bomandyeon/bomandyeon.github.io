Ghosting, read by birthday type

Does a Mountain ghost? Why the hill suddenly isn't there

The Mountain (Mu Earth 戊): Calm, solid and protective. Slow to start, very hard to move, and shows love by quietly taking care of things.

Short answer

A Mountain does not usually ghost out of drama; they stop responding when they feel played or disrespected, and once they have quietly withdrawn, they rarely come back.

Silence means different things from different people. For some types it is a goodbye, for others a sulk, for others a nap that lasted four days. This page reads the silence through the birthday type, so you can tell a pause from an ending and send one sensible message instead of nine.

Why a Mountain goes quiet

Mountains are patient with flaws and impatient with games. If you play hot and cold, test them or keep them guessing, they will not argue. They will simply stop climbing. No announcement, no fight. The mountain just is not there anymore.

That kind of silence can feel like ghosting, and in practice it is. But for a Mountain it is a calm decision, not a sulk. They are not waiting for you to chase. They have already moved.

If you send one message, send this

'I realise I have been inconsistent. I am sorry. If you are open to it, I would like to talk honestly.'

Send it once. If nothing comes back, that is an answer too, and you did not spend a month guessing.

If you are the Mountain

If you are the Mountain, quietly withdrawing feels clean to you. To the other person it is a cliff edge in fog. One sentence explaining why is enough.

The Earth pattern

Earth rarely leaves on impulse. When it goes quiet, it is usually a decision made over time (the Mountain) or a reply drafted eleven times and never sent (the Garden Field).

FAQ

Do Mountain types ghost?

They can withdraw silently when they feel played or disrespected. They rarely do it on impulse, so their silence is usually a decision.

Will a Mountain come back after going quiet?

Not often, once they have decided. A sincere, specific apology is the only thing that has a chance. Saju cannot promise any outcome.

Why did my Mountain stop talking without a fight?

Because Mountains avoid conflict and leave instead. They may think the reason was obvious, even if it was not to you.
