Questions

Will it tell me if my ex is coming back?

No. Nothing honest can. Another person’s choices aren’t written in your birth date, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something. Bom and Yeon look at your side instead: why it ended for you, why you keep thinking about them, and what you can do either way.

Do I need to know my ex’s birthday?

No, it’s optional. Your comic is built from your own year, month and day pillars, your birth season and your element balance. Add their birthday and you get one extra chapter on how your two patterns met, and why it hurt the way it did.

Is this fortune-telling?

No. We use saju as a personality language for self-reflection, a bit like MBTI with centuries of practice behind it. It’s for entertainment and self-understanding, not predictions.

Do I need to know my birth time?

No. Your Day Master comes from your birth date, and we read three pillars from it: year, month and day. If you know your birth time, add it under “Birth time and place” in the form. That adds a fourth pillar, which shifts your element balance and strength count a little. Born between 11 pm and midnight? Saju schools disagree on your day. We follow the common Korean rule and count you as the next day when you give a time.

Will it tell me whether to text them?

No, that call stays yours. If they ever reach out, it gives you questions to ask yourself and three ready-made replies, so whatever you choose is actually your choice.

How complete is the reading?

A full Korean saju reading uses four pillars (year, month, day and hour), eight characters in total. With your birth date alone we read three pillars, which is how many Koreans read saju when the hour is unknown. Add your birth time and we read all four. We don’t include your 10-year luck cycles (대운), which traditional readers use for timing and which also depend on a male or female setting we don’t ask for. Solar terms are converted to the time zone you were born in. Where a reading looks at two people, it compares your Day Masters and notes your day branches; a traditional compatibility reading (궁합) compares both whole charts.

How long is the reading?

10 illustrated chapters. Each chapter opens with a scene and then Bom and Yeon talk it through. What each chapter says depends on your chart and answers. Read it in one go or a chapter at a time, and save it as a PDF to keep.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: who ended it; how long ago did it end; what do you want right now, honestly. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.

Why these three?

In Korean saju, your birthday type (Day Master) is the heavenly stem of the day you were born, and it works like a personality type. Three of the ten souls have an especially hard time sitting with an unfinished conversation. Jeong Fire, the candle, needs emotional closure; an open loop feels like a draft in the room, and the urge to fix it is physical. Sin Metal, the jewel, replays what went wrong in high definition and wants to correct the record, politely, at 1:14 a.m. Gye Water, the rain, feels everything a little late and all at once, so the text arrives weeks after the breakup, just when everyone assumed the chapter had closed.

What saju can and cannot tell you

Saju can describe your habits: how you attach, what you do when you feel unfinished, which kind of silence you find unbearable. It cannot read anyone else’s heart, and it cannot tell you whether a relationship will restart. Nobody honest can. What it can do is help you notice the moment you are about to act on a pattern instead of a decision. That pause is the whole point.

A better question than “should I?”

Ask what you want the text to do. If the answer is “make me feel better tonight”, write it in your notes app and wait until morning. If it is a real apology or a real question you are ready to hear any answer to, that is a different message, and you will write it better after sleep. Candles, jewels and rain all make clearer choices in daylight.

The other seven souls

Not a Jeong, Sin or Gye? You have your own pattern. Gap Wood goes silent and calls it dignity. Byeong Fire posts a glowing photo instead of texting. Mu Earth waits for the other person to move first, for years if necessary. Find your soul to see yours, written by Bom, who will be kind about it, and Yeon, who will not.
