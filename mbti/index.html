16 x 10

MBTI meets your saju soul

MBTI tells you how you process the world. Saju, the Korean four pillars reading, tells you what you are made of: one of ten Day Masters, the heavenly stem of the day you were born. Put them together and things get specific. An INFP who is a candle is not the same person as an INFP who is an ocean. Find your row, find your soul, and tell us we are wrong. Bom wrote the nice ones. Yeon wrote the rest.
