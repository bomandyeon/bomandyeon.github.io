Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-22.

Your birthday

Your chart is calculated in your browser. The birthdays you enter are saved in your browser's local storage so you don't have to type them again. They aren't sent to us unless you subscribe to the Daily Soul (see below). Reading links don't show your birthday either: the address carries only a short random code, and a link we email you (when you ask for your chapters by email) carries a scrambled copy so it opens on your other devices. You can clear them any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Email (Daily Soul)

If you subscribe, your email address, your Day Master, your birth date and where you signed up are sent to Kit (ConvertKit), the service that sends our emails. We use them only to send the Daily Soul and occasional news about readings. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To delete your data, unsubscribe or email yeon@bomandyeon.com.

Checkout (Gumroad)

Readings are sold through Gumroad, which is the merchant of record: Gumroad is the seller on your receipt, takes the payment and handles sales tax and VAT. When you check out, Gumroad gets your email address and payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card number. Gumroad tells us your email address and which reading you got, so we can help if something goes wrong. Your license key is checked with Gumroad when you open a reading and then saved in your browser so you don't need it again on that device.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address as part of delivering the pages. Our fonts are hosted on this site, so no font service sees your visit. The only advertising tool is the Meta Pixel, and only if you say yes (below).

Visitor counts (GoatCounter)

We use GoatCounter to see how many people visit and which pages and buttons they use, like how many readings get opened or share images saved. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect personal data. It doesn't follow you to other sites, and your IP address isn't stored. What it gets is the page address without anything after the question mark (if you came from one of our own links with a "src" tag, that short tag is counted once), the site you came from, and your browser, screen size and rough location. Button counts carry only a short name such as "reveal" or "share", never your birthday, your name or your answers. You can read GoatCounter's privacy policy, and if you use a content blocker, it can block GoatCounter and the site works just the same.

Meta Pixel and cookies (only if you accept)

We may advertise on Instagram and Facebook. To see which ads bring people here, we can use the Meta Pixel from Meta Platforms Ireland. It loads only after you tap "Accept" in our cookie banner; before that, and if you tap "Decline", nothing from Meta loads. If you accept, Meta sets a cookie (_fbp) and gets the page address, your browser and device details, your IP address and a few actions on this site: that a page was viewed, that the free comic was shown and read to chapter 2, that the buy button was tapped (with the reading and its price), and that an email was saved in the "email me my chapters" box (the fact only, not the address). Never your birthday, your name or your answers. Meta can link this to your Meta account and uses it under its own privacy policy. Our legal basis is your consent. To change your mind, use "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page: "Decline" stops the pixel and deletes its cookies on this site. If you never see a banner, the pixel isn't switched on.

Ad tags: if you arrive from one of our ads or links, the link's campaign tags (like utm_source=meta) are saved in your browser and added to the Gumroad checkout link, so we can tell which ad led to a sale. They name the ad, not you. Gumroad can also run its own Meta Pixel on its checkout pages, under Gumroad's privacy policy.

Third parties

Saju readings never ask about anyone else's private life beyond a birthday you choose to enter for compatibility. Please only enter birthdays you know with the person's blessing.
