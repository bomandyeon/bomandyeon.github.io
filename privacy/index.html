Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-20.

Your birthday

Your chart is calculated in your browser. The birthdays you enter are saved in your browser's local storage so you don't have to type them again. They aren't sent to us unless you subscribe to the Daily Soul (see below). You can clear them any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Email (Daily Soul)

If you subscribe, your email address, your Day Master, your birth date and where you signed up are sent to Kit (ConvertKit), the service that sends our emails. We use them only to send the Daily Soul and occasional news about readings. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To delete your data, unsubscribe or email yeon@bomandyeon.com.

Payments

Readings are currently a free founding reader gift. When paid readings start, payment will be handled by Stripe; we will never see or store your card number.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services receive your IP address as part of delivering the pages. We don't run advertising trackers.

Third parties

Saju readings never ask about anyone else's private life beyond a birthday you choose to enter for compatibility. Please only enter birthdays you know with the person's blessing.
