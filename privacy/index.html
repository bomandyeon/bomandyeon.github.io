Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-20.

Your birthday

Your chart is calculated in your browser. The birthdays you enter are saved in your browser's local storage so you don't have to type them again. They aren't sent to us unless you subscribe to the Daily Soul (see below). You can clear them any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Email (Daily Soul)

If you subscribe, your email address, your Day Master, your birth date and where you signed up are sent to Kit (ConvertKit), the service that sends our emails. We use them only to send the Daily Soul and occasional news about readings. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To delete your data, unsubscribe or email yeon@bomandyeon.com.

Payments

Readings are currently a free founding reader gift. When paid readings start, payment will be handled by Stripe; we will never see or store your card number.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address as part of delivering the pages. Our fonts are hosted on this site, so no font service sees your visit. We don't run advertising trackers.

Visitor counts (GoatCounter)

We use GoatCounter to see how many people visit and which pages and buttons they use, like how many readings get opened or share images saved. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect personal data. It doesn't follow you to other sites, and your IP address isn't stored. What it gets is the page address without anything after the question mark (so a birthday in a reading link stays with you; if you came from one of our own links with a "src" tag, that short tag is counted once), the site you came from, and your browser, screen size and rough location. Button counts carry only a short name such as "reveal" or "share", never your birthday, your name or your answers. You can read GoatCounter's privacy policy, and if you use a content blocker, it can block GoatCounter and the site works just the same.

Third parties

Saju readings never ask about anyone else's private life beyond a birthday you choose to enter for compatibility. Please only enter birthdays you know with the person's blessing.
