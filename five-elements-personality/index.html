Which element are you? Five elements personality in saju

Short answer

Your saju element is the element of your birthday type: Wood (the Tall Pine, the Climbing Flower), Fire (the Sun, the Candle), Earth (the Mountain, the Garden Field), Metal (the Sword, the Jewel) or Water (the Ocean, the Rain).

The five, in one line each

Wood grows: principled, curious, always reaching. Fire shines: warm, expressive, quick to feel. Earth holds: steady, loyal, practical. Metal cuts: clear, honest, high standards. Water flows: intuitive, deep, adaptable.

FAQ

How do I know my element?

It is the element of your birthday type, worked out from your birth date. The free check tells you in seconds.

Can I have more than one element?

Your chart has eight characters with various elements. Your main element is your birthday type's; the others add colour, and some charts are missing an element entirely.

Which elements are compatible?

Elements that feed each other (Water and Wood, Wood and Fire) tend to flow easily. Elements that check each other create friction and, often, attraction.
