The Jewel: the Sin Metal birthday type

Elegant, sensitive and hard to forget. Wants to be chosen clearly and out loud, and goes politely cold when hurt.

LoveRed flagsWorkMatchesDay pillarsFAQ

Short answer

The Jewel (Sin Metal, 辛) is the Korean birthday type of people born on a yin Metal day. In one line: Elegant, sensitive and hard to forget. Wants to be chosen clearly and out loud, and goes politely cold when hurt.

Sin (辛) is yin Metal, the eighth heavenly stem. Where Gyeong is raw iron, Sin is the finished piece: the jewel, the fine needle, the silver ring that catches light from across the room. If your birthday type (Day Master) is Sin, you have taste, standards and feelings far deeper than your polished surface suggests.

BomYou make the world more beautiful just by having standards. And the way you love, quietly and completely, deserves a whole drama series.

Personality

Sin Metal people are refined. You notice quality in everything: fabric, fonts, manners, word choice, the way someone treats a waiter. You have a clear sense of what is beautiful and what is cheap, and you would rather have one perfect thing than ten average ones. People often describe you as elegant, cool or a little intimidating before they get to know you.

Then they get to know you, and they discover you cry at every drama finale and remember the one unkind comment someone made in 2019. You are sensitive, precise and principled. You hold yourself to high standards and quietly hope others will notice the work it takes to look this effortless.

The shadow side of a jewel is that it scratches easily, or at least feels like it does. You can be perfectionistic, self-critical and quick to feel slighted. You want to be valued, and when you are not, you either withdraw into icy politeness or say something cutting with surgical accuracy. You also find it hard to show the messy, unfinished parts of yourself.

In the elements, Earth produces Metal, so calm, solid people steady you. Your Metal flows into Water, meaning your intelligence and expression. You shape Wood, meaning money and practical goals. And Fire is your pressure and polish. Your heavenly combination is Byeong Fire (丙 and 辛 combine): the sun that makes your jewel sparkle, the warm person who sees your value at first glance.

The Jewel in love

High standards, deep feelings. You want to be chosen clearly and out loud. Not maybe, not "let's see where it goes", not a 2 a.m. "you up?". You want effort, consistency and someone who is proud to stand next to you.

You can seem hard to approach, so people either admire you from a distance or try too hard. Neither works. What works is someone who is sincere, attentive and not intimidated by your taste. Once you feel safe, you are tender, loyal and surprisingly playful. Very few people get to see you laugh until you cannot breathe.

Your risk is waiting to be perfectly understood before you open up. That is a long wait. Let someone see a flaw early. You will be shocked how often it makes them like you more. With Byeong Fire, your classic match, you find warmth that does not demand you dull your shine.

Red flags

  • You test people with tiny standards they do not know exist, then fail them silently.
  • You remember a careless comment for years and bring it up at the worst moment.
  • You go cold and polite when hurt, which is far scarier than yelling.
  • You want grand gestures but will not admit you want them.
  • You reject people before they can reject you, then call it high standards.

YeonPerfect is a hiding place. Nobody falls in love with a display case. Let someone see the scratch.

At work

You excel where precision and taste matter. Sin Metal thrives in design, fashion, beauty, jewelry, editing, finance, law, research, luxury hospitality, any work where details define quality. You produce polished results and spot errors others miss. Under pressure you often shine brightest, which surprises people. Your growth edge is finishing before it is perfect; done and excellent beats flawless and late.

Money style

You love quality and will save for months to buy the right thing rather than three wrong ones. You are good at managing money when you have a goal, and you dislike financial chaos. Your risk is spending on image when you feel insecure, buying the status piece to prove something. Ask yourself whether you want the thing or the feeling, then buy the cheaper of the two.

As a friend

Selective and loyal. You may not have many friends, but the ones you have are carefully chosen and deeply valued. You give thoughtful gifts, honest style advice and the kind of support that is calm and practical. Let your friends see you on a bad day with no makeup on your feelings. They will love you more, not less.

FAQ

What is the Sin Metal personality?

Sin (辛) is yin Metal, symbolized by a jewel or fine blade. Sin birthday types tend to be elegant, perceptive, principled and sensitive, with high standards for themselves and others.

What is Sin Metal's best match?

Byeong Fire (丙) is the classic heavenly combination for Sin. Mu Earth (戊) is also supportive, since Earth produces Metal. Jeong Fire (丁) is more challenging, because yin Fire melts yin Metal directly.

Why do Sin Metal people seem cold?

Sin protects a very sensitive core with a polished surface. The coolness is usually caution, not lack of feeling. Once they feel safe and valued, Sin people are warm and deeply loyal.
