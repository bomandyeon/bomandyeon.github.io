Ten Day Masters · 일간

The 10 saju souls

In saju, the heavenly stem of the day you were born is your Day Master. There are ten. Think of them as personality types, with about a thousand years of notes.

甲

Gap Wood · The Tall Pine

Gap (甲) is the first of the ten heavenly stems, and honestly, it acts like it. In Korean saju your Day Master is the heavenly stem of the day you were born, the one character in your chart that stands for you.

Read Gap Wood乙

Eul Wood · The Climbing Flower

Eul (乙) is yin Wood, the second of the ten heavenly stems. Where Gap is a tall tree, Eul is the vine, the climbing rose, the grass that comes back through concrete.

Read Eul Wood丙

Byeong Fire · The Sun

Byeong (丙) is yang Fire, the third heavenly stem, and the ancient picture for it is simply the sun. Not a candle, not a bonfire: the thing in the sky that shines on everyone, whether they asked for it or not.

Read Byeong Fire丁

Jeong Fire · The Candle

Jeong (丁) is yin Fire, the fourth heavenly stem. If Byeong is the sun, Jeong is the candle, the lantern, the stars on a clear night.

Read Jeong Fire戊

Mu Earth · The Mountain

Mu (戊) is yang Earth, the fifth heavenly stem, and its image is the mountain. Big, calm, very hard to move, and the place everyone runs to when the weather turns.

Read Mu Earth己

Gi Earth · The Garden Field

Gi (己) is yin Earth, the sixth heavenly stem. Mu is the mountain; Gi is the field at its foot: soft soil, garden beds, the ground where things actually grow.

Read Gi Earth庚

Gyeong Metal · The Sword

Gyeong (庚) is yang Metal, the seventh heavenly stem. Its image is raw metal, the axe, the sword, iron that has not been polished yet.

Read Gyeong Metal辛

Sin Metal · The Jewel

Sin (辛) is yin Metal, the eighth heavenly stem. Where Gyeong is raw iron, Sin is the finished piece: the jewel, the fine needle, the silver ring that catches light from across the room.

Read Sin Metal壬

Im Water · The Ocean

Im (壬) is yang Water, the ninth heavenly stem, and its image is the ocean, or a great river that never stops moving. If your Day Master is Im, you are curious, restless and harder to pin down than you seem.

Read Im Water癸

Gye Water · The Rain

Gye (癸) is yin Water, the tenth and last heavenly stem. If Im is the ocean, Gye is the rain, the morning mist, the dew on a window.

Read Gye Water
