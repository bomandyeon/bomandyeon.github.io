Mu Earth and Gi Earth Compatibility: The Mountain and the Garden Soil

70%

Mountain and valley: one landscape, two moods

This is the same number the birthday check on the home page shows: it compares your two Day Masters, and it is the same whichever of you types first. With both birthdays we also note your day branches (the "spouse palace") separately. A traditional 궁합 reading compares both whole charts.

Both are Earth, so values align: loyalty, stability, home. Mu is the mountain, big and unmoving. Gi is the valley soil, soft, fertile and attentive. Together they form a whole landscape. The match is reliable and caring, with a few quiet frictions.

BomYou're each other's safe place. Keep noticing the little things.

Chemistry

Gi feels protected by Mu's calm strength; Mu feels cared for by Gi's attention to detail. Both like routine, good food and comfortable homes. Gi brings warmth and practicality; Mu brings steadiness and security. You build a life that feels safe and grounded. It's the pair that hosts the best low-key dinners: Gi plans the menu down to the napkins, Mu carries the table outside and makes everyone feel welcome.

Where it clashes

Gi worries; Mu says don't worry, which doesn't help. Mu can be stubborn and slow; Gi can be anxious and overthinking. Gi does a lot of invisible work and may feel Mu doesn't notice. Both avoid conflict, so issues go unaddressed. When Gi finally says 'I'm tired of doing everything', Mu is honestly surprised, because Mu thought silence meant all was well.

Texting style

Gi asks caring questions; Mu replies with short, solid answers. Mu: ask a question back. Gi: trust that Mu's brevity isn't indifference.

YeonMu, help with the dishes. Gi, stop worrying about the dishes.

What Mu Earth needs

Mu Earth needs Gi to trust their judgment and not worry everything to death.

What Gi Earth needs

Gi Earth needs Mu to notice and share the small daily work. Practical help is love language for Gi.

Long term

Earth with Earth tends to build something real: a home, savings, a family, a routine that holds through hard years. It stays warm when both speak up early and Mu stays emotionally present.

FAQ

Are Mu Earth and Gi Earth compatible?

Yes, it's a solid match. Sharing the Earth element brings shared values and stability. The yang-yin difference adds balance, though Gi's worry and Mu's stubbornness need care.

What does Gi Earth need from Mu Earth?

Recognition of everyday effort and a real share of practical tasks. Gi gives a lot quietly, and Mu noticing makes Gi feel valued.
