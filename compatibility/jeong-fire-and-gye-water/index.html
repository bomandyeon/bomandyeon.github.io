Jeong Fire and Gye Water Compatibility: The Candle and the Rain

47%

Rain on the candle: tender, sad-eyed, easily put out

This is the same number the birthday check on the home page shows: it compares your two Day Masters, and it is the same whichever of you types first. With both birthdays we also note your day branches (the "spouse palace") separately. A traditional 궁합 reading compares both whole charts.

Water controls Fire, and rain on a candle is the most direct version of it. Both are yin: sensitive, private and emotionally rich. This is a tender, intimate match with a real risk of dampening each other's spirits.

BomYou two feel things beautifully. Hold each other gently.

Chemistry

Gye is intuitive, gentle and mysterious; Jeong is focused, warm and devoted. You understand each other's emotional depth instantly. Late-night talks, shared secrets, a sense of being truly seen. Jeong's warmth draws Gye out; Gye's empathy soothes Jeong. It's the kind of relationship with a shared notebook of song lyrics, or a habit of walking in the rain, because neither of you minds getting a little wet.

Where it clashes

Gye's moods can put out Jeong's flame. Jeong's intensity can feel like pressure to Gye. Both withdraw when hurt, so conflicts go silent and stay unresolved. Gye's mystery can trigger Jeong's anxiety; Jeong's need for clarity can feel invasive to Gye.

Texting style

Emotional, poetic and full of feelings, with long silences in between. Agree that silence is allowed, but not as punishment.

YeonTwo sad songs on repeat. Go outside. Call a funny friend.

What Jeong Fire needs

Jeong Fire needs clarity and consistency. Gye, tell Jeong where they stand, even briefly.

What Gye Water needs

Gye Water needs gentleness and privacy. Jeong, don't push for every secret.

Long term

It can last as a deep, soulful partnership, but it needs outside sunshine: friends, routines, things that lift both moods. Two yin souls together must be careful not to sink into the same low. Practical tip: give each other one lightness job. One plans the fun, one picks the comedy for movie night. Joy needs an owner in this pair.

FAQ

Why is Jeong Fire and Gye Water a challenging match?

Water controls Fire and both are yin, so moods can dampen each other and conflicts go quiet. Emotional depth is high, but the pair needs outside energy and clear communication.

Can Jeong Fire and Gye Water make it work?

Yes, with care. Their empathy is a real strength. Clear communication, respect for privacy, and plenty of outside joy keep the flame alive.
