Sin Metal and Im Water Compatibility: The Polished Jewel and the Ocean

80%

A jewel washed in the river: clarity, sparkle, freedom

This is the same number the birthday check on the home page shows: it compares your two Day Masters, and it is the same whichever of you types first. With both birthdays we also note your day branches (the "spouse palace") separately. A traditional 궁합 reading compares both whole charts.

Metal generates Water, and in classical saju Sin Metal shines brightest when washed by Im Water. With yin and yang balanced, this is a sparkling, intelligent match: Sin brings refinement, Im brings depth and freedom.

BomThe jewel shines brighter in water. You two make each other sparkle.

Chemistry

Im is expansive, curious and witty; Sin is refined, perceptive and sharp. Conversations are electric. Im makes Sin feel free and less judged; Sin gives Im elegance and focus. You admire each other's minds and enjoy cultured adventures together. Im is often the first person who makes Sin laugh at their own perfectionism, and Sin is often the first person who gets Im to finish a project beautifully.

Where it clashes

Im's unpredictability can unsettle Sin, who likes things polished and planned. Sin's critiques can annoy free-spirited Im. Im may forget details that matter to Sin. Sin may feel neglected when Im drifts. The specific flash point: Im shows up late, underdressed and full of a brilliant new idea, while Sin has been planning the evening for a week. Neither is wrong; both feel unseen.

Texting style

Witty, clever exchanges, with Im's bursts and silences. Sin: don't read too much into gaps. Im: notice the effort in Sin's messages.

YeonIm, remember the anniversary. Sin, let the ocean be messy.

What Sin Metal needs

Sin Metal needs attention to details and appreciation of effort. Im, remember the small things.

What Im Water needs

Im Water needs freedom and acceptance. Sin, don't polish the ocean. Im also needs Sin's critique to come with a smile, and not mid-adventure.

Long term

This tends to become a bright, intellectually rich partnership with plenty of adventure and wit. It shines when Im is attentive and Sin is relaxed. Travel suits this pair perfectly: Sin chooses the hotel, Im finds the hidden beach, and both come home closer.
