The Sun (Byeong Fire) and the Jewel (Sin Metal) score 90 out of 100 in Korean saju compatibility. In short: heavenly Combination 丙辛: sunlight on a jewel.

丙 and 辛 form one of the five heavenly combinations, traditionally said to merge into Water: something deep and flowing. Fire controls Metal, yet here the sun doesn’t melt the jewel; it makes it shine. The attraction is dazzling, admiring and often instant.

Chemistry

Sin is refined, precise and quietly dazzling. Byeong is warm, open and generous with attention. Byeong lights up Sin, and Sin feels truly appreciated, maybe for the first time. Sin gives Byeong sophistication and a reason to slow down and look closely. You make each other look and feel beautiful. Friends tend to notice that Sin laughs more around Byeong, and that Byeong actually finishes a sentence quietly around Sin.

Where it clashes

Byeong’s heat can still burn: careless words, forgotten details, too much noise. Sin is sensitive to criticism and to being overlooked. When Sin feels neglected, they withdraw or turn sharp. Byeong can find Sin’s standards exhausting or picky. Both hate being ignored, in different ways.

Texting style

Byeong sends warm, frequent, excited messages. Sin replies beautifully, a little delayed. Byeong: notice the effort in Sin’s words. Sin: a quick response matters to the sun.

YeonByeong, notice the earrings. Sin, stop testing the sun to see if it’s paying attention.

What Byeong Fire needs

Byeong Fire needs Sin to soften critiques and enjoy the warmth without picking it apart.

What Sin Metal needs

Sin Metal needs to be noticed and admired in specific, sincere ways. Generic praise doesn’t count; details do.

Long term

Combination pairs tend to return to each other and deepen with time. This one can become a stylish, affectionate and admired partnership. It holds when Byeong stays attentive and Sin stays trusting.

FAQ

What does the 丙辛 combination mean?

丙 Byeong Fire and 辛 Sin Metal form one of the five heavenly stem combinations in saju, traditionally linked to Water. It signals strong, admiring attraction and a bond that tends to deepen.

Is Byeong Fire and Sin Metal the best match for Sin?

It’s one of the highest-scoring matches for Sin Metal. Byeong’s warmth helps Sin feel seen. The pair needs Byeong to stay attentive to Sin’s sensitivity to stay strong.
