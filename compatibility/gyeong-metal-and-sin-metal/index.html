Sword + Jewel compatibility: Raw Steel and the Polished Jewel

Steel and silver: same metal, different polish

Short answer

The Sword (Gyeong Metal) and the Jewel (Sin Metal) score 64 out of 100 in Korean saju compatibility. In short: steel and silver: same metal, different polish.

Both are Metal, so both value honesty, standards and loyalty. Gyeong is raw steel, tough and direct. Sin is polished silver, refined and precise. The match has strong mutual respect and a constant small argument about how things should be said.

BomYou two have such strong values. Respect each other's style and you're unstoppable.

Chemistry

Gyeong admires Sin's elegance and sharp mind; Sin admires Gyeong's courage and decisiveness. You share a clear sense of right and wrong. Sin brings style and diplomacy; Gyeong brings protection and action. Together you're formidable at work, in friendships and in getting things done. At a party you're the duo that gets the room moving: Gyeong makes the decisive call, Sin makes it look effortless.

Where it clashes

Gyeong's bluntness offends Sin's refinement. Sin's precision can feel like nitpicking to Gyeong. Gyeong may scratch Sin's polish carelessly; Sin may cut Gyeong with quiet sarcasm. Both are proud and neither apologizes first. A typical fight starts with Gyeong saying something true in the worst possible way, and Sin answering with one perfectly cold sentence. Both are then too proud to talk for a day.

Texting style

Gyeong: short and direct. Sin: careful and crafted. Gyeong: soften the delivery. Sin: say it plainly; Gyeong misses subtext.

YeonGyeong, polish your words a bit. Sin, stop sanding everything down.

What Gyeong Metal needs

Gyeong Metal needs Sin to be direct rather than critical. Plain requests work better than polished hints.

What Sin Metal needs

Sin Metal needs Gyeong to be gentle with their sensitivity. Tone matters as much as content.

Long term

Metal with Metal often builds a strong, respectful, effective partnership over time. It stays warm when Gyeong is gentler and Sin is more straightforward. The respect underneath is real, which gives this pair an excellent base to repair from.

FAQ

Are Gyeong Metal and Sin Metal compatible?

Fairly. Sharing Metal brings shared values and respect. The yang-yin style difference causes friction around tone and criticism, which needs care.

What do Gyeong Metal and Sin Metal fight about?

Tone more than content. Gyeong is blunt, Sin is sensitive and precise. Agreeing on how to deliver feedback solves most arguments.
