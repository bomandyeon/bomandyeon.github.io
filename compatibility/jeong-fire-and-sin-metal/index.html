Jeong Fire and Sin Metal Compatibility: The Candle and the Polished Jewel

52%

A flame near silver: beautiful, delicate, easily scratched

This is the same number the birthday check on the home page shows: it compares your two Day Masters, and it is the same whichever of you types first. With both birthdays we also note your day branches (the "spouse palace") separately. A traditional 궁합 reading compares both whole charts.

Fire controls Metal, and a candle too close to fine silver can tarnish it. Both are yin: sensitive, perceptive and private. This is an elegant but delicate match, full of mutual fascination and easily bruised feelings.

BomYou both see the world so beautifully. Show each other that same kindness.

Chemistry

Both notice details others miss. Jeong admires Sin's taste and refinement; Sin admires Jeong's depth and devotion. Conversations are subtle and layered. You enjoy beautiful things together and bond over private observations about people. There's often a shared private humor, a running commentary about the world that only the two of you find hilarious, and that bond is worth a lot.

Where it clashes

Jeong's intensity can feel like pressure to Sin, who values poise. Sin's critiques sting Jeong, who takes them personally. Both are sensitive and both withdraw when hurt. Sin may find Jeong possessive; Jeong may find Sin cold. Tension simmers without anyone saying anything. The flash point tends to be public: a comment at dinner with friends that one of you takes as a review of the other. Keep feedback for the walk home, and keep it short.

Texting style

Carefully crafted, overanalyzed and rich in subtext. Both reread before sending. Lower the stakes: a silly selfie can break a week of tension.

YeonTwo people waiting to be apologized to. Someone go first.

What Jeong Fire needs

Jeong Fire needs warmth and reassurance from Sin. A cool tone, even when meant neutrally, reads as rejection.

What Sin Metal needs

Sin Metal needs space and gentle feedback. Jeong, don't crowd the jewel or demand constant closeness.

Long term

It takes conscious work, but two sensitive people who learn each other's signals can become a quietly refined couple. Patience and plain words are the polish here.

FAQ

Why is Jeong Fire and Sin Metal a hard match?

Fire controls Metal and both are yin, so sensitivity runs high and critiques hurt. Both withdraw when upset, making repair slow. Gentle, direct talk helps a lot.

What do Jeong Fire and Sin Metal have in common?

Both are perceptive, detail-oriented and private. They share an eye for beauty and depth. That common ground is a strong base when they learn to communicate openly.
