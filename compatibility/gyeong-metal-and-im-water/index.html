Sword + Ocean compatibility: Raw Steel and the Ocean

76%

Cold steel, deep water: clear minds, strong currents

With both birthdays we also note your day branches (the partner seat (spouse palace)) separately. A traditional 궁합 reading compares both whole charts.

Short answer

The Sword (Gyeong Metal) and the Ocean (Im Water) score 76 out of 100 in Korean saju compatibility. In short: cold steel, deep water: clear minds, strong currents.

Metal generates Water, and both are yang: raw steel and the open ocean. Gyeong gives Im structure, courage and clarity; Im gives Gyeong depth, ideas and freedom. It's a bold, adventurous match between two independent people who respect each other's strength.

BomYou two push each other to be bolder. That's a beautiful dynamic.

Chemistry

Im admires Gyeong's decisiveness and loyalty; Gyeong admires Im's intelligence and broad perspective. Both love challenges and new experiences. You make an adventurous team: travel, big projects, intense conversations. Neither is clingy, which suits both. You're the couple that does a marathon on Saturday and an all-night debate about ethics on Sunday. Im's ideas give Gyeong's energy a direction, and Gyeong's grit makes Im's ideas actually happen.

Where it clashes

Gyeong wants commitment and clear plans; Im wants freedom and flexibility. Gyeong's bluntness can chill Im; Im's unpredictability frustrates Gyeong. Both are independent, so emotional closeness can lag behind practical partnership. The danger is that you become excellent teammates and lonely lovers. Plenty of action, not enough tenderness.

Texting style

Gyeong: direct and brief. Im: bursts and silences. Agree on a minimum check-in and respect the rest.

YeonGyeong, you can't command the ocean. Im, a plan is not a prison.

What Gyeong Metal needs

Gyeong Metal needs reliability and follow-through from Im. Keep the promises that matter. Gyeong also needs to hear where Im is heading, even when the answer is 'I don't know yet'.

What Im Water needs

Im Water needs freedom without suspicion. Gyeong, trust Im's independence.

Long term

This can mature into an adventurous partnership with a lot of respect and freedom on both sides. It deepens when both make room for emotional intimacy, not just shared action. A weekly evening with no plans, no projects and no phones does more for this pair than any grand gesture.

FAQ

Is Gyeong Metal good for Im Water?

Yes. Metal generates Water, so Gyeong's strength supports Im's flow. Both yang and independent, they make a bold, adventurous team.

How do Gyeong Metal and Im Water build intimacy?

By slowing down sometimes. Both are action-oriented and independent. Quiet time, honest talk about feelings, and small rituals deepen the bond.
