Mu Earth and Sin Metal Compatibility: The Mountain and the Polished Jewel

78%

The mountain guards the gem: steady love for a precious thing

This is the same number the birthday check on the home page shows: it compares your two Day Masters, and it is the same whichever of you types first. With both birthdays we also note your day branches (the "spouse palace") separately. A traditional 궁합 reading compares both whole charts.

Earth generates Metal, and the mountain hides its most precious gems deep inside. Mu protects and nurtures Sin; Sin brings refinement, beauty and precision to Mu's life. With yang and yin balanced, this is a caring, stable and quietly elegant match.

BomThe mountain protects its gem. That's a love story right there.

Chemistry

Sin feels safe and valued with Mu, who doesn't criticize or rush. Mu is fascinated by Sin's taste and sharp mind. Sin makes Mu's life more beautiful; Mu makes Sin's life more secure. There's tenderness here: the strong protecting the delicate, and the delicate polishing the strong. Mu often becomes Sin's calmest place: the person who doesn't comment on the outfit, just holds the door. That quiet acceptance is rare for Sin.

Where it clashes

Sin can be critical of Mu's simplicity; Mu can find Sin high-maintenance. Mu's slow emotional responses can leave Sin feeling unappreciated. Sin's sensitivity can puzzle Mu, who doesn't always know what went wrong. Sin may hint for weeks that something matters, and Mu will miss every hint. Plain words save months here.

Texting style

Sin crafts messages carefully and wants a thoughtful reply. Mu replies briefly. Mu: compliment specifically. Sin: understand that Mu's short reply still means care.

YeonMu, 'nice' is not a compliment. Sin, the mountain is not a fixer-upper.

What Mu Earth needs

Mu Earth needs acceptance of their straightforward nature. Sin, don't try to polish the mountain.

What Sin Metal needs

Sin Metal needs appreciation and admiration, specifically and often. Mu, notice the details.

Long term

Over time, this pair often becomes a beautifully balanced partnership with a stable home and a refined lifestyle. It deepens when Mu shows appreciation and Sin shows patience. Sin tends to bring the beauty into the home and Mu makes sure it's still standing in thirty years.

FAQ

Is Mu Earth compatible with Sin Metal?

Yes. Earth generates Metal, and the yang-yin balance creates a protective, caring match. Mu's appreciation and Sin's patience make it work beautifully.

What does Sin Metal need from Mu Earth?

Specific appreciation and admiration. Sin puts care into details and wants it noticed. Mu adding thoughtful compliments makes Sin feel truly valued.
