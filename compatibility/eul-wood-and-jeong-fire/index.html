Eul Wood and Jeong Fire Compatibility: The Vine and the Candle

75%

Flowers by candlelight: soft, romantic, a little fragile

Type score for the two Day Masters. A check with both birthdays also reads your day branches, so your own number can move a few points.

Wood feeds Fire, here in its most delicate form: the vine and the candle, both yin. It's an intimate, soft and very romantic pairing. Both are perceptive, both feel deeply, and both prefer hints to confrontation. That makes it beautiful, and occasionally a guessing game.

BomYou two make love feel like a handwritten letter. Keep writing to each other, even after years.

Chemistry

Jeong notices everything about Eul: the mood shift, the new earrings, the line in a song. Eul feels truly seen. Eul, in turn, keeps Jeong's flame lit with care, encouragement and small kindnesses. Dates tend to be cosy and private, conversations long and personal. It's the kind of love that fits in a small room with good lighting.

Where it clashes

Both avoid direct conflict. Eul goes vague, Jeong goes quiet, and resentment simmers underneath. Jeong can become possessive, the flame wanting all of Eul's attention, while Eul likes a wide social circle. Two yin souls can also amplify each other's worries late at night.

Texting style

Thoughtful, long and emotionally loaded. Reading between lines is your sport. Agree on a code word for 'I'm actually upset' so nothing hides behind emojis.

YeonTwo people sulking politely in the same room is not peace. Use your words, both of you.

What Eul Wood needs

Eul Wood needs freedom to keep friends and social life without it being read as distance. A flame that trusts doesn't need to guard.

What Jeong Fire needs

Jeong Fire needs loyalty they can see: being prioritised, being included, being told the truth before others hear it.

Long term

Over time two yin souls like this become a deeply bonded, creative and private couple. It strengthens when both speak plainly about hurts early, and when Eul's sociability and Jeong's intensity are accepted as features, not threats.

FAQ

Are Eul Wood and Jeong Fire compatible?

Yes, it scores well. Wood feeds Fire, and the shared yin sensitivity creates a tender, intimate bond. The weak point is indirect communication, so speaking early about hurts matters a lot.

Why does Jeong Fire get jealous with Eul Wood?

Jeong's love is focused and intense, while Eul spreads warmth widely. Jeong may read Eul's friendliness as interest elsewhere. Clear signs of priority from Eul and trust from Jeong settle it.
