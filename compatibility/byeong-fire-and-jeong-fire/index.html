Byeong Fire and Jeong Fire Compatibility: The Sun and the Candle

66%

The sun and the candle: same fire, different rooms

This is the same number the birthday check on the home page shows: it compares your two Day Masters, and it is the same whichever of you types first. With both birthdays we also note your day branches (the "spouse palace") separately. A traditional 궁합 reading compares both whole charts.

Both are Fire, so passion is never the problem. Byeong is the sun, warming everyone at once. Jeong is the candle, warming one person at a time with focus. Together the fire is strong, but the sun can easily outshine the candle without noticing.

BomBoth of you know how to love fiercely. You just do it in different volumes. Respect both.

Chemistry

Jeong is drawn to Byeong's openness and confidence; it's a relief to be near someone who doesn't hide. Byeong is fascinated by Jeong's depth and the intensity of their attention. Jeong remembers every detail; Byeong makes every day feel like a holiday. You understand each other's emotional heat, the need to feel things fully.

Where it clashes

Byeong spreads warmth widely; Jeong wants it focused. Jeong may feel like one of Byeong's many friends rather than the one. Byeong may find Jeong's intensity heavy or possessive. Two fires can also flare together in arguments, quickly and hotly, with Jeong remembering the details for much longer.

Texting style

Byeong sends lots of short, loud messages. Jeong sends fewer, deeper ones and notices when Byeong replies to everyone else first. Byeong: reply to Jeong first. Jeong: assume good intent.

YeonByeong, Jeong isn't one of your followers. Jeong, brunch with friends is not a betrayal.

What Byeong Fire needs

Byeong Fire needs freedom to be social and loud without guilt, and a partner who enjoys the spotlight with them.

What Jeong Fire needs

Jeong Fire needs one-on-one time and focused attention. A private ritual, just the two of you, means more than any party.

Long term

Fire with fire stays passionate for years. It works when Byeong learns to dim the light for intimate moments and Jeong learns that Byeong's broad warmth isn't a threat to their special place.

FAQ

Are Byeong Fire and Jeong Fire compatible?

Fairly. Sharing the Fire element brings passion and mutual understanding. The yang sun and yin candle differ in how they show love, broad versus focused, which needs conscious balance.

Why does Jeong Fire feel overlooked by Byeong Fire?

Byeong's warmth goes everywhere at once, and Jeong wants a dedicated flame. Regular one-on-one time and small signs of priority from Byeong solve most of this.
