Short answer

The Climbing Flower (Eul Wood) and the Climbing Flower (Eul Wood) score 62 out of 100 in Korean saju compatibility. In short: two vines in one garden: sweet, social and tangled.

Two Eul Wood souls are all charm and adaptability. They read each other perfectly, laugh at the same moments and never run out of things to talk about. The question is what two vines climb when there is no tree in the picture.

BomYou understand each other in a way most people never get. Kindness is your default, and that is rare.

Chemistry

It starts like a best-friendship that turned romantic: group chats, shared playlists, a dozen mutual friends within a month. Both are socially smart and emotionally tuned in, so the relationship feels light and kind. Nobody bulldozes anyone. You are each other's favorite person to gossip with and the softest place to land after a bad day.

Where it clashes

Nobody wants to decide. Where to eat, where to live, whether to move in, all of it floats. Both avoid open conflict, so small irritations go into side comments and quiet comparisons. Two Eul can also become dependent on each other's approval, and get jealous of outside attention, because both know exactly how charming the other can be.

Texting style

Endless. Voice notes, stickers, links, 'look at this', 'omg same'. It is lovely and it is also where big topics get dodged. Have the serious conversations face to face.

YeonSomebody pick the restaurant. I am begging. Also, saying 'I'm fine' with that face is not fine.

What Eul Wood needs

The first Eul Wood needs their partner to make a decision sometimes, and stick to it. Being chosen clearly is deeply calming for a vine.

What Eul Wood needs

The second Eul Wood needs reassurance about outside friendships. Show them they're your first call, and the jealousy fades on its own.

Long term

This pair can last as a warm, social, flexible partnership with a big circle around it. It needs some outside structure: shared goals, a calendar, even a friend who asks 'so when are you two actually moving?'

FAQ

Can two Eul Wood partners be happy together?

Yes. The match is affectionate and easy, with lots of empathy and shared social life. The weak point is drift: without someone taking the lead on decisions, the relationship can stay pleasant but stuck.

Why do two Eul Wood people get jealous?

Both are skilled at charm and reading people, so each knows what the other is capable of with others. Clear reassurance and a few visible commitments calm this quickly.
