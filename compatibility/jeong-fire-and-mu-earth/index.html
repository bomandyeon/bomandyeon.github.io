Chemistry

Mu makes Jeong feel safe from the wind. Jeong's flame can finally burn steadily without fear of being blown out. Texting style

What Mu Earth needs

Long term

Pairs like this tend to age well, slowly building a home that feels like a sanctuary. The mountain shields the flame, the flame warms the mountain, and the years make both more certain.
