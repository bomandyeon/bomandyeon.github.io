Mu Earth and Gyeong Metal Compatibility: The Mountain and Raw Steel

75%

The mountain holds the ore: strength protecting strength

This is the same number the birthday check on the home page shows: it compares your two Day Masters, and it is the same whichever of you types first. With both birthdays we also note your day branches (the "spouse palace") separately. A traditional 궁합 reading compares both whole charts.

Earth generates Metal, and ore is born inside mountains. Mu gives Gyeong stability, patience and protection; Gyeong gives Mu energy, direction and decisive action. Both are yang, both are strong. Together they're a formidable, loyal team.

BomYou two make each other stronger. That's what the generating cycle is all about.

Chemistry

Gyeong admires Mu's calm and reliability; Mu admires Gyeong's courage and decisiveness. Both value loyalty and honesty above almost anything. Neither plays games. There's mutual respect from the start and a sense of being with someone who won't crack under pressure. In a crisis you're the couple everyone calls: Gyeong charges in to solve it, Mu stays calm and keeps everyone fed. Few pairs handle pressure this well.

Where it clashes

Gyeong can be impatient with Mu's slowness; Mu can be stubborn about Gyeong's push for change. Gyeong's bluntness can bruise Mu, who doesn't show hurt easily. Both are proud, so apologies are rare. Arguments are short but heavy. The classic standoff: Gyeong wants to decide tonight, Mu wants to sleep on it for a week. Both see the other as unreasonable.

Texting style

Short and to the point on both sides. Efficient, sometimes too efficient. Add one personal line per day.

YeonGyeong, chill. Mu, decide. Both of you, say sorry sometimes.

What Mu Earth needs

Mu Earth needs Gyeong to slow down sometimes and respect Mu's pace and decisions. Mu also needs time to process before answering. Pushing for an instant reply gets a 'no' every time.

What Gyeong Metal needs

Gyeong Metal needs action and decisiveness from Mu. Make the call and stick to it.

Long term

The bond tends to strengthen with each challenge faced together. Build shared goals and celebrate them, and this pair becomes a lifelong team. These pairs often build something physical together, a house, a business, a renovated flat, and that shared work keeps them close.

FAQ

Is Mu Earth good for Gyeong Metal?

Yes. Earth generates Metal, so Mu naturally supports Gyeong's strength and ambitions. The pair is loyal and strong, with pace differences to manage.

How do Mu Earth and Gyeong Metal handle conflict?

Directly but stubbornly. Both hate apologizing. Agreeing to revisit fights after a cool-down, and each owning their part, keeps things healthy.
