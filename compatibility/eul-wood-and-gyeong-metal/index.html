Short answer

The Climbing Flower (Eul Wood) and the Sword (Gyeong Metal) score 87 out of 100 in Korean saju compatibility. In short: heavenly Combination 乙庚: the vine wraps the blade.

乙 and 庚 are one of the five heavenly combinations, said to merge into Metal: something strong and refined. On paper Metal cuts Wood. In practice the soft vine and the raw steel are drawn together with surprising force, the gentle and the tough finding each other irresistible.

BomSteel that learns gentleness and a vine that learns courage. 乙庚 is a classic love story for a reason.

Chemistry

Gyeong is direct, protective and brave. Eul is graceful, socially skilled and emotionally fluent. Each has what the other lacks. Gyeong feels softened and civilized by Eul; Eul feels safe and backed up by Gyeong. The attraction is quick, sometimes against both people's better judgment.

Where it clashes

Gyeong's bluntness can cut Eul deeply. Eul's indirectness can frustrate Gyeong, who wants straight answers. Gyeong may try to control or 'toughen up' Eul; Eul may manipulate around Gyeong's rules. When hurt, Gyeong gets harsher and Eul gets slipperier. The fix is almost boring: Gyeong waits ten minutes before replying when annoyed, and Eul answers the actual question asked.

Texting style

Gyeong: 'Where are you.' Eul: a sticker and a voice note about a cat. Gyeong should add warmth, Eul should give a straight answer first and the cat after.

YeonGyeong, stop treating feelings like a weakness to fix. Eul, stop treating rules like suggestions.

What Eul Wood needs

Eul Wood needs gentleness in delivery. Gyeong's honesty is welcome, but tone is everything for a vine.

What Gyeong Metal needs

Gyeong Metal needs clarity and loyalty. Tell Gyeong the plan and the truth, even the inconvenient version.

Long term

Combination pairs often circle back to each other and grow more solid with time. This one can become a strong, protective partnership with surprising tenderness. It holds when Gyeong softens and Eul straightens.

FAQ

What is the 乙庚 combination in saju?

乙 Eul Wood and 庚 Gyeong Metal form one of the five heavenly stem combinations, traditionally linked to Metal. It signals strong attraction between opposites and a bond that tends to return and strengthen.

Is Eul Wood and Gyeong Metal a good match?

It's a high-scoring match thanks to the combination. The tension is real, since Metal controls Wood, but the pull is stronger. Soft delivery from Gyeong and direct honesty from Eul make it work.
