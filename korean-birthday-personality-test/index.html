What you get

Your birthday type and its image, a short reveal from Bom (warm) and Yeon (blunt), and links to your full profile: personality, love, red flags, work, friends and best matches. If you add a birth time, the reveal also shows your day pillar, one of sixty.

How it is different from a quiz

There are no questions to game. A quiz gives you the type you hoped for; a birthday test gives you the one your date points to, which is sometimes more interesting. Think of it as a second opinion next to MBTI. Comparison here: saju vs MBTI.

FAQ

Is the Korean birthday test free?

Yes. The birthday type check and all type pages are free. Longer comic readings have a free preview.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Your birthday stays in your browser. An email is only needed if you choose the Daily Soul newsletter.

Is this the same test as on TikTok?

Many 'Korean birthday' trends use simplified tables. Ours does the real day count with solar terms, so the result matches a traditional Korean calendar.
