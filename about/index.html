What is saju?

Saju (사주, "four pillars") is the Korean way of reading a birth date. The year, month, day and hour you were born each become a pair of characters from the old Chinese calendar: one heavenly stem and one earthly branch. Eight characters in total, which is why it's also called palja, "eight characters".

Each character carries one of five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) and a yin or yang side. How they balance is the shape of your chart.

What's a Day Master?

The heavenly stem of your birth day is the character that stands for you. There are ten, so there are ten souls: Gap Wood, Eul Wood, Byeong Fire, Jeong Fire, Mu Earth, Gi Earth, Gyeong Metal, Sin Metal, Im Water and Gye Water. Meet all ten.

How we calculate

We use real solar terms (the exact moments the sun crosses each 15° point, computed from astronomical data) to set the year and month pillars, so someone born on February 3 and someone born on February 5 can get different charts. A solar term happens at one moment for the whole planet, so we convert it to the time zone you were born in: your browser's time zone by default, or the birthplace you pick under "Birth time and place". Your day pillar uses the date on your local calendar.

If you don't know your birth hour, we read the other three pillars, which is how many Koreans use saju too. If you were born on a solar-term day, we tell you, because then your month pillar depends on the time. If you add your birth time, we read all four pillars and follow the common Korean rule that 11 pm starts the next day; some schools disagree, so births between 11 pm and midnight can have a different Day Master depending on who you ask.

What our readings leave out

We don't calculate your 10-year luck cycles (대운), which traditional readers use for timing and which need a birth hour and a male or female setting we don't ask for. Our strength count (strong or weak) is a simplified version of the classical method, worked out from the pillars we have. Compatibility compares two Day Masters and notes your day branches; a full Korean compatibility reading (궁합) compares both whole charts. Saju itself, as a way of reading a birth chart, is roughly a thousand years old; the calendar it uses is much older.

What we don't claim

Saju here is a language for talking about personality, relationships and timing, with a lot of jokes. It is not a prediction, a diagnosis or advice. Nothing on this site should decide anything about your health, money, safety or relationships for you. For entertainment and self-reflection.
