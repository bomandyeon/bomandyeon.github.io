2027 · Year of the Fire Goat

Your 2027, by soul

2027 is a 丁未 (Jeong-Mi) year: yin Fire on top, the Goat below. The Goat branch 未 is yin Earth and carries hidden 己 Earth, 丁 Fire and 乙 Wood, so the year runs warm and dry, Fire feeding Earth. In saju the saju year starts at Ipchun, around 4 February 2027, not on 1 January. How the Fire Goat treats you depends on what your Day Master is made of: Fire feeds Earth, melts Metal, is fed by Wood and is checked by Water. Below is your soul's year, in the stem and branch language of the old almanacs and the plain language of your group chat. Best months follow the solar calendar, so each one begins around the 4th to 8th of that month. For entertainment and self-reflection.

甲

Gap Wood: Your talent goes public. 丁 is your Hurting Officer (output) and the Goat's 己 is your Direct Wealth: Wood feeds the year's Fire, and that Fire turns into Earth, which is money you can hold.

Love
You are more expressive and more attractive than usual, and your words have more edge than usual too. Singles meet people through work, classes or creative circles. Couples drift toward practical talks: money, a shared flat, what next year looks like. Soften the delivery and these talks go well.
Work
Pitch the idea you have been rehearsing in the shower. Side projects, portfolios, anything with your name on it gets noticed, and in the second half of the year visibility starts paying. You will be impatient with slow bosses and slow systems. Use that energy to build, not to burn bridges.
Money
Direct Wealth in the branch rewards steady, honest work. Income linked to something you made or taught is favored. Keep the boring savings habit; a Wood that gives everything to Fire needs roots.
Watch out for
Saying the sharp thing in the wrong room. A Fire year makes Wood burn bright, and also burn out if you give every ounce to output. Schedule rest like a meeting.
Best months
February, March, December

Make something with your name on it, and actually finish it.

乙

Eul Wood: An easy, pleasurable, money-friendly year. 丁 is your Eating God (gentle output) and the Goat's 己 is your Indirect Wealth: your skills flow into Fire and come back as opportunity.

Love
Warm, playful, social. Singles have genuine choice and more invitations than usual. Couples rediscover fun through trips, food and shared projects. The only trap is attention spread across too many people. Choose, then enjoy.
Work
Your work sells itself through people who simply like you. Freelance gigs, creative products and new clients arrive through friends of friends. Say yes, then get organized fast, because the Goat also hides an Eul in its storage: someone with your exact skills is chasing the same doors.
Money
Indirect Wealth means money from unexpected directions: a side hustle, a commission, a hobby that turns paid. Track it, because easy money also leaves easily.
Watch out for
Comfort turning into drift. You will feel lighter than you have in a while. Keep one ambitious goal on the wall so pleasure does not swallow the year.
Best months
February, March, December

Turn the thing you do for fun into the thing people pay for.

More on Eul Wood
丙

Byeong Fire: Fire meets fire. 丁 is your Rob Wealth (a competitive peer) and the Goat's 己 is your Hurting Officer: your voice gets louder, and so does the competition.

Love
Bold and dramatic. You may fall for someone other people want too, or feel a jealous spark inside an existing bond. Say what you feel plainly, never cruelly. Couples who compete less and play more have a great year.
Work
Rivals sharpen you. Colleagues push, opportunities come with competition, and you do your best work when there is something to win. Perform, publish, present. Read contracts twice, because Rob Wealth years love a handshake deal that costs you later.
Money
Money moves fast. Rob Wealth can mean shared costs, friends borrowing, or spending to keep up. Separate your accounts from your social life and you keep what you earn.
Watch out for
Ego fights over small things. Too much Fire dries everything out. Let the quieter months of late summer and autumn cool you down and turn heat into results.
Best months
September, October, November

Pick one big goal and let the competition fuel it, not you.

More on Byeong Fire
丁

Jeong Fire: Your own element returns. 丁 is your Friend (Bigyeon) and the Goat's 己 is your Eating God: confidence plus creativity, a year of feeling at home in yourself.

Love
You know what you want and you finally show it. Singles meet people through friends and shared hobbies. Couples settle into a softer, more playful rhythm. Small stubborn arguments are the only weather to watch.
Work
Independent projects, teaching, cooking, design, anything handmade and heartfelt does well. Partnerships of equals work. Micromanagers do not. You stop asking permission this year, and people respect it.
Money
Eating God years reward talent with steady, modest gains. Peers may ask you to share costs or lend money; a Friend year makes you generous, so set a limit in advance.
Watch out for
Too much Fire can make a candle flicker. Protect your quiet time and do not let other people's urgency set your pace. Water months near the end of the year bring welcome balance.
Best months
August, September, December

Share the thing you made. Your small flame lights more people than you think.

More on Jeong Fire
戊

Mu Earth: Fire feeds Earth. 丁 is your Direct Resource and the Goat's 己 is your Rob Wealth: support arrives, and so does competition for the same seat.

Love
You attract nurturing people and feel more open to being cared for than usual. A gentle person may enter through work, study or family. Couples deepen trust, often around family plans. Let yourself be the one who is looked after.
Work
This is your mentor year. A course, a qualification, a senior person who believes in you: take it. At the same time, peers want the same chair, so stay generous without handing over your best ideas for free.
Money
Rob Wealth in the branch means money can leak to friends, partners and shared plans. Lend only what you can forget. Invest in skills, which nobody can borrow from you.
Watch out for
Getting too comfortable. A Fire-heavy year can make Earth dry and stubborn. The months when Metal and Water arrive, in autumn and winter, are where you turn support into action.
Best months
September, November, December

Accept the help. Mountains grow taller when something lifts them.

More on Mu Earth
己

Gi Earth: An inward, self-defining year. 丁 is your Indirect Resource and the Goat's 己 is your own element: unusual learning, strong intuition and a sharper sense of who you are.

Love
You need understanding more than excitement. Singles connect through study, creative or quiet circles. Couples need honest talks about space and closeness. If you feel unseen, say it before you withdraw.
Work
Niche skills pay off: research, healing-adjacent work, something you taught yourself at midnight. You may question your current path. That is not a crisis, it is a recalibration. Write your hunches down; this year they tend to be right.
Money
A Friend in the branch means shared money gets complicated. Keep your own fund, and be wary of group investments that rely on trust more than paperwork.
Watch out for
Overthinking in a warm, dry year. Get out of your head and into your hands. Spring and autumn months bring the Wood and Metal that move things forward.
Best months
April, October, November

Claim your own direction, even if it surprises the people who think they know you.

More on Gi Earth
庚

Gyeong Metal: One of the classic recognition years. 丁 is your Direct Officer and the Goat's 己 is your Direct Resource: Fire forges your Metal, and Earth supports you while it does.

Love
A serious year. Singles meet committed, grown-up types. Many couples make it official: moving in, meeting the family, a ring or at least a very adult conversation. You want structure in love this year, and that is fine.
Work
Responsibility, promotion, a formal role, a title. People in charge notice your reliability. Accept the structure, negotiate fairly and set your boundaries on day one, because Officer years like to add duties quietly.
Money
Steady rather than spectacular. Formal income, contracts and raises are favored. A good year to sort paperwork and long-term plans.
Watch out for
Carrying pressure silently. Fire forges Metal, and forging is hot work. Rest on purpose and say no to one extra task per month.
Best months
April, August, September

Take the role you were offered, or quietly hoping for, and set the terms early.

More on Gyeong Metal
辛

Sin Metal: The forge year. 丁 is your Seven Killings (a strong challenge) and the Goat's 己 is your Indirect Resource: pressure shapes you into something more valuable.

Love
Intense attraction, often to strong or complicated people. Choose the one whose strength makes you feel safe, not small. Couples face one real test and come out closer if they talk directly.
Work
Deadlines, tough people and higher stakes. Handle one big challenge beautifully and it becomes your reputation for years. Specialist training or a niche skill protects you. Take criticism professionally, not personally.
Money
Resource in the branch favors learning over spending. Invest in a qualification or a skill. Avoid buying status to feel in control when work feels out of control.
Watch out for
Yin Fire melts yin Metal directly, so this year presses on your sensitive spots. Quiet routines and trusted people are your armor. Autumn, the Metal season, gives you back your edge.
Best months
August, September, October

Pressure makes jewels. Let this year cut you into your best shape.

More on Sin Metal
壬

Im Water: A pull toward commitment. 丁 is your Direct Wealth and combines with your own 壬 (丁壬 is a heavenly stem combination), while the Goat's 己 is your Direct Officer.

Love
One of your strongest relationship years. The stem combination is a classic sign of attraction: singles meet someone serious and magnetic, couples choose each other more deeply. The year asks you to stop keeping things vague.
Work
Steady money and responsibility: a stable contract, a raise, a role with a clear title. Reward follows reliability, which is not your favorite word, but this year it pays.
Money
Direct Wealth favors salaries, savings and planned purchases over gambles. Build something that lasts: a fund, a home deposit, a long-term plan.
Watch out for
The tug between freedom and security. Fire and Earth weigh on Water this year, so protect your energy and keep winter months for refilling.
Best months
July, November, December

Commit to the person, the plan or the place you keep circling.

More on Im Water
癸

Gye Water: Opportunity under pressure. 丁 is your Indirect Wealth and the Goat's 己 is your Seven Killings: money and chances arrive, often with a demanding person attached.

Love
Chemistry is quick and strong. You attract confident, intense people. Enjoy it, and let consistent actions, not intensity, earn your trust. Couples need clear boundaries around time and expectations.
Work
Side deals, sales, travel and sudden openings, frequently alongside a tough boss or a tight deadline. Move fast but read the fine print. Your intuition about people is a professional asset this year.
Money
Indirect Wealth brings irregular income. Put a share of every unexpected payment aside for calm, because Seven Killings years also bring unexpected costs.
Watch out for
The 丁癸 clash. The year's stem 丁 clashes with your 癸, one of the four classic stem clashes, so expect friction between what you want and what others expect, and plans that need a second try. On top of that, yin Earth muddies yin Water, so guard your sleep, your boundaries and your inner quiet.
Best months
October, November, December

Take the opportunity, set your own terms, and keep a little aside for peace.
