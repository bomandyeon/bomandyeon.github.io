Your 2027, by soul

2027 · Year of the Fire Goat

2027 is a 丁未 (Jeong-Mi) year: yin Fire on top, the Goat below. The Goat branch 未 is yin Earth and carries hidden 己 Earth, 丁 Fire and 乙 Wood, so the year runs warm and dry, Fire feeding Earth. In saju the saju year starts at Ipchun, around 4 February 2027, not on 1 January. How the Fire Goat treats you depends on what your Day Master is made of: Fire feeds Earth, melts Metal, is fed by Wood and is checked by Water. Below is your soul’s year, in the stem and branch language of the old almanacs and the plain language of your group chat. Best months follow the solar calendar, so each one begins around the 4th to 8th of that month. For entertainment and self-reflection.

甲

Gap Wood: Your talent wants an audience. 丁 is your Hurting Officer (output) and the Goat’s 己 is your Direct Wealth: Wood feeds the year’s Fire, and that Fire turns into Earth, the element saju links with what you can build and keep.

Love
You may feel more expressive than usual, and your words can carry more edge than usual too. If you’re single, work, classes or creative circles suit how you connect this year. In a couple, practical talks come up easily: a shared flat, what next year looks like. Soften the delivery and they go better.
Work
Pitch the idea you have been rehearsing in the shower. A year to put your name on things: side projects, portfolios, finished work. You may be impatient with slow bosses and slow systems. Use that energy to build, not to burn bridges.
Money habits
Direct Wealth in saju is about how you handle what you earn and keep, not a forecast of income. Your pattern this year: you may want to pour everything into making things. Keep the boring savings habit anyway; a Wood that gives everything to Fire needs roots.
Watch out for
Saying the sharp thing in the wrong room. A Fire year makes Wood burn bright, and also burn out if you give every ounce to output. Schedule rest like a meeting.
Best months
February, March, December

Make something with your name on it, and actually finish it.

乙

Eul Wood: An easy, pleasurable, sociable year. 丁 is your Eating God (gentle output) and the Goat’s 己 is your Indirect Wealth: your skills flow into Fire, and the year leans toward enjoying what you make.

Love
Warm, playful, social. You may feel more open to invitations than usual. In a couple, fun through trips, food and shared projects suits this year. The only trap is attention spread across too many people. Choose, then enjoy.
Work
Your work travels best through people who simply like you, so friends of friends matter. Say yes, then get organized fast, because the Goat also hides an Eul in its storage: someone with your exact skills may be chasing the same doors.
Money habits
Indirect Wealth in saju is about how you handle irregular, unplanned things, not a forecast of money. Your tendency this year: easy come, easy go. Notice where it goes, and keep one simple rule for anything unplanned.
Watch out for
Comfort turning into drift. You will feel lighter than you have in a while. Keep one ambitious goal on the wall so pleasure does not swallow the year.
Best months
February, March, December

Take the thing you do for fun seriously enough to finish it.

More on Eul Wood
丙

Byeong Fire: Fire meets fire. 丁 is your Rob Wealth (a competitive peer) and the Goat’s 己 is your Hurting Officer: your voice gets louder, and so does the competition.

Love
Bold and dramatic. You may fall for someone other people want too, or feel a jealous spark inside an existing bond. Say what you feel plainly, never cruelly. Couples who compete less and play more have a great year.
Work
Rivals sharpen you. Colleagues push, opportunities come with competition, and you do your best work when there is something to win. Perform, publish, present. Read contracts twice, because Rob Wealth years love a handshake deal that costs you later.
Money habits
Rob Wealth in saju describes how you share and compete with peers, not what you will earn. Your tendency this year: shared costs, friends borrowing, spending to keep up. Separate your accounts from your social life and it stays simple.
Watch out for
Ego fights over small things. Too much Fire dries everything out. Let the quieter months of late summer and autumn cool you down and turn heat into results.
Best months
September, October, November

Pick one big goal and let the competition fuel it, not you.

More on Byeong Fire
丁

Jeong Fire: Your own element returns. 丁 is your Friend (Bigyeon) and the Goat’s 己 is your Eating God: confidence plus creativity, a year of feeling at home in yourself.

Love
You know what you want and you’re more likely to show it. If you’re single, friends and shared hobbies suit how you connect. In a couple, a softer, more playful rhythm fits this year. Small stubborn arguments are the only weather to watch.
Work
Independent projects, teaching, cooking, design, anything handmade and heartfelt does well. Partnerships of equals work. Micromanagers do not. You stop asking permission this year, and people respect it.
Money habits
A Friend year in saju is about peers and generosity, not a forecast of money. Your tendency this year: being generous with friends who ask you to share costs or lend. Set a limit in advance and you never have to decide on the spot.
Watch out for
Too much Fire can make a candle flicker. Protect your quiet time and do not let other people’s urgency set your pace. Water months near the end of the year bring welcome balance.
Best months
August, September, December

Share the thing you made. Your small flame lights more people than you think.

More on Jeong Fire
戊

Mu Earth: Fire feeds Earth. 丁 is your Direct Resource and the Goat’s 己 is your Rob Wealth: support arrives, and so does competition for the same seat.

Love
You may feel more open to being cared for than usual, and drawn to nurturing people. In a couple, trust around family plans can deepen. Let yourself be the one who is looked after.
Work
This is your mentor year. A course, a qualification, a senior person who believes in you: take it. At the same time, peers want the same chair, so stay generous without handing over your best ideas for free.
Money habits
Rob Wealth in saju describes sharing and competing with peers, not money coming or going. Your tendency this year: lending to friends, partners and shared plans. Lend only what you can forget, and put your effort into skills nobody can borrow from you.
Watch out for
Getting too comfortable. A Fire-heavy year can make Earth dry and stubborn. The months when Metal and Water arrive, in autumn and winter, are where you turn support into action.
Best months
September, November, December

Accept the help. Mountains grow taller when something lifts them.

More on Mu Earth
己

Gi Earth: An inward, self-defining year. 丁 is your Indirect Resource and the Goat’s 己 is your own element: unusual learning, strong intuition and a sharper sense of who you are.

Love
You need understanding more than excitement. If you’re single, study, creative or quiet circles suit how you connect. In a couple, honest talks about space and closeness help. If you feel unseen, say it before you withdraw.
Work
Niche interests pull at you: research, healing-adjacent work, something you taught yourself at midnight. You may question your current path. That is not a crisis, it is a recalibration. Write your hunches down and test them.
Money habits
A Friend in the branch is about trust between peers, not a money forecast. Your tendency this year: shared plans where trust does the work paperwork should do. Keep your own fund and your own copy of every agreement.
Watch out for
Overthinking in a warm, dry year. Get out of your head and into your hands. Spring and autumn months bring the Wood and Metal that move things forward.
Best months
April, October, November

Claim your own direction, even if it surprises the people who think they know you.

More on Gi Earth
庚

Gyeong Metal: One of the classic recognition years. 丁 is your Direct Officer and the Goat’s 己 is your Direct Resource: Fire forges your Metal, and Earth supports you while it does.

Love
A serious year in tone. You may be drawn to committed, grown-up types and want structure in love, and that is fine. In a couple, very adult conversations come easier than usual.
Work
Officer years in saju are about responsibility and structure. You may be handed more of both. Accept the structure, negotiate fairly and set your boundaries on day one, because Officer years like to add duties quietly.
Money habits
Steady rather than spectacular is your style this year: paperwork, contracts and long-term plans feel more natural than gambles. A good year to sort the boring admin you keep postponing.
Watch out for
Carrying pressure silently. Fire forges Metal, and forging is hot work. Rest on purpose and say no to one extra task per month.
Best months
April, August, September

If a role is offered, or you’re quietly hoping for one, set the terms early.

More on Gyeong Metal
辛

Sin Metal: The forge year. 丁 is your Seven Killings (a strong challenge) and the Goat’s 己 is your Indirect Resource: pressure shapes you into something more valuable.

Love
You may be drawn to strong or complicated people. Choose the one whose strength makes you feel safe, not small. In a couple, talking directly matters more than usual.
Work
Deadlines, tough people and higher stakes. Handle one big challenge beautifully and it becomes your reputation for years. Specialist training or a niche skill protects you. Take criticism professionally, not personally.
Money habits
Resource in the branch is about learning and support, not money. Your tendency this year: buying things to feel in control when work feels out of control. Put that impulse into a skill or a course instead.
Watch out for
Yin Fire melts yin Metal directly, so this year presses on your sensitive spots. Quiet routines and trusted people are your armor. Autumn, the Metal season, gives you back your edge.
Best months
August, September, October

Pressure makes jewels. Let this year cut you into your best shape.

More on Sin Metal
壬

Im Water: A pull toward commitment. 丁 is your Direct Wealth and combines with your own 壬 (丁壬 is a heavenly stem combination), while the Goat’s 己 is your Direct Officer.

Love
The 丁壬 stem combination is read in saju as a pull toward commitment. If you’re single, you may find yourself drawn to someone serious; in a couple, choosing each other more deeply. Either way, the year asks you to stop keeping things vague.
Work
Officer energy means responsibility and clear titles. Reliability is not your favorite word, but this year it suits you: say what you’ll do, then do it.
Money habits
Direct Wealth in saju is about how you handle what you earn and keep, not a forecast. Your tendency this year: planned purchases feel better than gambles. Build something that lasts, like a habit of saving a little on purpose.
Watch out for
The tug between freedom and security. Fire and Earth weigh on Water this year, so protect your energy and keep winter months for refilling.
Best months
July, November, December

Commit to the person, the plan or the place you keep circling.

More on Im Water
癸

Gye Water: Opportunity under pressure. 丁 is your Indirect Wealth and the Goat’s 己 is your Seven Killings: openings and demanding people tend to arrive together.

Love
Chemistry is quick and strong. You attract confident, intense people. Enjoy it, and let consistent actions, not intensity, earn your trust. Couples need clear boundaries around time and expectations.
Work
Fast-moving work suits you this year: sales, travel, sudden openings, often alongside a tough boss or a tight deadline. Move fast but read the fine print. Your read on people is a professional asset.
Money habits
Indirect Wealth in saju is about how you handle irregular, unplanned things, not a money forecast. Your tendency this year: reacting fast. Put a share of anything unplanned aside for calm, so the next surprise costs you less stress.
Watch out for
The 丁癸 clash. The year’s stem 丁 clashes with your 癸, one of the four classic stem clashes, so friction between what you want and what others expect can feel louder. On top of that, yin Earth muddies yin Water, so guard your sleep, your boundaries and your inner quiet.
Best months
October, November, December

Take the opportunity, set your own terms, and keep a little aside for peace.
