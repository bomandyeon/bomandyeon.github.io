What is my Korean birthday type?

Short answer

Your Korean birthday type is one of ten saju types (the Tall Pine, the Climbing Flower, the Sun, the Candle, the Mountain, the Garden Field, the Sword, the Jewel, the Ocean and the Rain), and it is set by the day you were born, not the year.

How your type is worked out

Korean saju counts days in a cycle of sixty that has run without a break for well over two thousand years. Each day carries one of ten heavenly stems, and the stem of your birth day is your birthday type. Because the stems repeat every ten days, someone born ten days after you shares your type. Someone born the day after you does not.

That is also why you cannot work it out from your star sign or your zodiac animal. It needs a proper day count, which the free check does in your browser. Your birthday is not sent anywhere.

FAQ

Is my Korean birthday type the same as my zodiac animal?

No. The animal comes from your birth year and is shared by everyone born that year. Your birthday type comes from your birth day.

Can two people born in the same week have different types?

Yes. The type changes every day and repeats every ten days, so people born a few days apart usually have different types.

Does it matter what time I was born?

Only if you were born late in the evening. Many Korean readers treat 11 pm as the start of the next day, so births between 11 pm and midnight can land on the following day's type.
