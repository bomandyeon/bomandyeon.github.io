Jeong-yu: The Candle on the Silver Tray

Candlelight reflected in silver and gems. Refined, discerning, sociable, and quietly very good with resources.

  • Yin Fire stem
  • Rooster branch · Metal
  • Pillar 34 of 60
  • Partner seat: Indirect Wealth 편재
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Short answer

Jeong-yu (丁酉) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Candle birthday type (Jeong Fire) standing on the Rooster, candlelight reflected in silver and gems. Refined, discerning, sociable, and quietly very good with resources.

Jeong-yu sets the Candle on 酉, the Rooster: pure, polished autumn Metal. Fire controls Metal, so Metal is Jeong's Wealth (편재 here). The image is candlelight glowing on a silver tray at a dinner party: elegant, flattering, a little luxurious. Everything near you looks better.

You have taste and an eye for value. You know which café is good before it is popular and which vintage jacket is worth the price. You are sociable in a curated way, charming in small groups and good at making people feel special. The Rooster is also linked to 도화, the "peach blossom" star in some schools, which is why Jeong-yu people often read as attractive and social. The Qiong Tong Bao Jian notes that Jeong on strong Metal needs Wood to keep burning. Without fuel, the candle melts down fast, and you can exhaust yourself polishing appearances.

BomYou make life look beautiful, and you share that beauty generously. People leave a dinner with you feeling like guests of honor.

Jeong-yu in love

Indirect Wealth in the partner seat (spouse palace) makes you a romantic who enjoys pleasure and beauty. You like dressing up for dates, finding the perfect restaurant and giving gifts that land. You are attentive, flirtatious and fun.

Your risk is valuing a relationship partly by how it looks or by what it offers. You can also get restless and wonder if something better is around the corner. Real intimacy is less polished. Let a partner see you without the lighting. People with Wood energy, nurturing and encouraging, keep your fire fed so you are not running on charm alone.

As a friend

You are the friend with the best recommendations and the nicest gifts. You host beautifully and you remember how each person takes their tea. You can be selective and a bit judgmental about taste. Your friends find this hilarious most of the time.

Red flags

  • You curate your life so well that nobody sees it is hard.
  • You judge a date by the restaurant they picked.
  • You spend energy on appearances and have none left for honesty.
  • You keep a backup option in your head, just in case.

YeonNot everything has to be curated. Let someone see your messy kitchen and your messier feelings. It is called intimacy.

At work

Jeong-yu thrives in fields where taste meets money: fashion, retail, finance, jewelry, hospitality, marketing, art dealing, interior design. You read value well and you negotiate gracefully. Watch for burnout; you make work look effortless when it is not, and people pile more on.

Jeong-yu vs the other Jeong Fire pillars

Jeong-yu is the most refined and resource-minded Jeong pillar. Jeong-myo (丁卯), its clash partner (卯酉), is dreamy and bookish; you are polished and practical. Jeong-hae (丁亥) values principles; you value quality. Jeong-sa (丁巳) also sits near Metal (巳 and 酉 can combine), but it is louder and more competitive. Jeong-chuk (丁丑) and Jeong-mi (丁未) express themselves through care and craft, you through style. Your partner seat holds Wealth, so romance tends to come with good food and better lighting.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Jeong-yu: the 酉 Rooster branch is Metal and hides 庚 (Direct Wealth), 辛 (Indirect Wealth), read from a Jeong Fire point of view. Its main qi, 辛, puts Indirect Wealth (편재 偏財) in the Jeong-yu partner seat. In branch relations, 酉 clashes with 卯 (Rabbit) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 辰 (Dragon), so a partner with a Rabbit or Dragon day branch will feel noticeably different to a Jeong-yu person. 丁酉 is pillar 34 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Is Jeong-yu a 'peach blossom' pillar?

In schools that use the 도화 (peach blossom) star, 酉 is one of its branches, which is why Jeong-yu is often described as charming and socially attractive. We treat it as a personality flavor, not a prediction about romance.

Is Jeong-yu materialistic?

Not necessarily. Having Wealth in the partner seat often reads as appreciating quality and being practical about resources. Whether that becomes materialism depends on the person.

What helps Jeong-yu stay balanced?

Wood, which feeds a candle on Metal. In everyday terms: mentors, learning, rest and people who value you for more than your taste.
