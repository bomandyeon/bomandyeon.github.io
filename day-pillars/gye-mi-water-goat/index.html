Gye-mi: The Summer Rain on Dry Fields

A warm rain falling on parched summer fields. Caring, resilient, quietly anxious, and deeply needed.

Short answer

Gye-mi (癸未) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Rain birthday type (Gye Water) standing on the Goat, a warm rain falling on parched summer fields. Caring, resilient, quietly anxious, and deeply needed.

Gye-mi places the Rain on 未, the Goat: hot, dry earth at the end of summer, holding 丁 Fire and 乙 Wood. Earth controls Water, so this branch is Gye's Seven Killings (편관). Picture a sudden rain in late July after weeks of heat. Dust smells sweet, cracked fields drink, everyone steps outside to feel it. The rain is small compared to the land, but it changes everything it touches.

You are caring and resilient, with a strong sense of responsibility. You tend to show up where you are most needed and you give more than you take. Seven Killings sits right under your birthday type (Day Master), so you live with a quiet pressure: expectations from others and from yourself. The hidden 乙 Wood is your Eating God, which gives you gentleness and creativity, and the 丁 Fire is your Wealth, a practical streak. The caution is evaporation. Rain on hot ground disappears fast, and you can feel stretched thin, anxious or overlooked while everyone else enjoys the green fields.

BomYou arrive exactly when people need you. Your care makes dry seasons bearable for everyone around you.

Gye-mi in love

Seven Killings in your partner seat (spouse palace) often draws you to strong, decisive, even demanding partners. You like feeling protected, and you will work hard to keep someone happy.

The risk is overaccommodating, or staying with someone who makes you feel you must constantly prove yourself. You deserve a partner who is strong and gentle at once. Metal energy, supportive and clear, refills your water; Water energy, emotionally fluent, makes you feel less alone. When you are loved well, you are one of the most devoted pillars in the cycle.

As a friend

You are the friend who shows up with exactly what was needed: soup, a charger, the right words. You notice when someone is struggling. You can overextend and quietly burn out, then feel guilty for needing rest. Your friends would much rather you said, "I'm tapped out," than vanished.

Red flags

  • You fall for people who make you earn their approval.
  • You give until you have evaporated.
  • You worry about everything quietly and call it being prepared.
  • You accept pressure from others as if it were a personality trait.

YeonYou are not a public utility. Someone who makes you audition for their love does not get your rain.

At work

Gye-mi does well in caring and service roles with structure: healthcare support, education, social work, hospitality, project coordination, nonprofit work. You are dependable under pressure. Choose environments with fair leadership. Harsh bosses dry you out; good ones help you thrive.

Gye-mi vs the other Gye Water pillars

Gye-mi feels more outside pressure than most Gye pillars. Gye-chuk (癸丑), its clash partner (丑未), also carries Seven Killings in the partner seat, but on cold wet earth, so it is more stoic and self-contained while you are warmer and more giving. Gye-yu (癸酉) is cerebral, Gye-hae (癸亥) emotionally vast, Gye-sa (癸巳) romantic and practical, Gye-myo (癸卯) playful and expressive. You are the rain that arrives exactly when the field needs it, which is beautiful and sometimes exhausting.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gye-mi: the 未 Goat branch is Earth and hides 丁 (Indirect Wealth), 乙 (Eating God), 己 (Seven Killings), read from a Gye Water point of view. Its main qi, 己, puts Seven Killings (편관 偏官) in the Gye-mi partner seat. In branch relations, 未 clashes with 丑 (Ox) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 午 (Horse), so a partner with a Ox or Horse day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gye-mi person. 癸未 is pillar 20 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Is Seven Killings in the partner seat bad for Gye-mi?

No. It describes intensity and pressure, not misfortune. It mostly means you are drawn to strong partners and need to make sure strength comes with kindness.

Is Gye-mi a weak Day Master?

Hot, dry Earth controls Water, so many Gye-mi charts lean weaker. Metal and Water elsewhere in the chart make a big difference.

What helps Gye-mi feel balanced?

Metal for support and Water for companionship. Rest, steady routines and people who give back are the practical version.
