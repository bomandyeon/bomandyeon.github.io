Jeong-hae: The Lantern on the Night River

A lantern floating on a winter river at night. Principled, magnetic, quietly romantic, and steadier than its flicker suggests.

  • Yin Fire stem
  • Pig branch · Water
  • Pillar 24 of 60
  • Partner seat: Direct Officer 정관
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Short answer

Jeong-hae (丁亥) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Candle birthday type (Jeong Fire) standing on the Pig, a lantern floating on a winter river at night. Principled, magnetic, quietly romantic, and steadier than its flicker suggests.

Jeong-hae places the Candle on 亥, the Pig: deep winter Water with a seed of 甲 Wood inside. On paper Water puts out Fire. But look closer: the main qi of 亥 is 壬 Water, and 丁 and 壬 form one of the five heavenly combinations (丁壬合). So your flame is secretly bonded to the very water around it. The image is a paper lantern drifting on a dark river, the kind released during festivals: fragile-looking, yet it floats.

You are principled, gentle and quietly magnetic. People feel calmer around you. The hidden 甲 Wood, your Direct Resource, keeps you fed with learning and protection, which is why you are more resilient than you look. The partner seat (spouse palace) is Direct Officer (정관), the classical sign of a strong moral compass. Your shadow is self-doubt. When the water rises, you wonder if your light is too small, and you defer to others when your instincts were right all along.

BomYou are the calm in other people's storms. Your quiet integrity makes everyone around you want to be a little better.

Jeong-hae in love

Direct Officer in your partner seat, plus the hidden 丁壬 combination, makes love a central theme for Jeong-hae. You are drawn to steady, principled partners and you bond deeply once you commit. Romance for you is intimate rather than showy: long walks, late talks, a hand on your back in a crowd.

You may give a partner too much authority over your choices. Admiring someone is good. Disappearing into them is not. Keep your own flame. The right person will want to see it burn brighter, not cup their hands around it so tightly it goes out.

As a friend

You are the calm friend, the one people go to when they need wisdom without drama. You are loyal and discreet, and you have a small circle that adores you. You sometimes over-accommodate, so speak up about where you want to eat. Yes, it matters.

Red flags

  • You defer to someone else's judgment even when yours was better.
  • You fall for a person's principles and ignore how they treat you.
  • You keep your light dim so nobody feels outshone.
  • You assume rules are always right because they are rules.

YeonRespecting your partner is lovely. Treating them like a CEO you report to is not. You get a vote too.

At work

Jeong-hae fits structured, meaningful work: public service, nonprofit management, teaching, law, healthcare, research ethics, HR. You are trustworthy and good at holding things together. Your growth area is confidence. Advocate for your own ideas as hard as you advocate for fairness.

Jeong-hae vs the other Jeong Fire pillars

Jeong-hae is the most principled and romantic of the Jeong pillars, thanks to the Officer and the hidden 丁壬 bond in its partner seat. Jeong-sa (丁巳) is its clash (巳亥), loud and self-assured where you are serene. Jeong-myo (丁卯) is more mystical, Jeong-chuk (丁丑) more practical, Jeong-mi (丁未) more social. Jeong-yu (丁酉) is drawn to style and resources; you are drawn to integrity. Of the six, you are most likely to describe your partner as "a good person" before anything else.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Jeong-hae: the 亥 Pig branch is Water and hides 戊 (Hurting Officer), 甲 (Direct Resource), 壬 (Direct Officer), read from a Jeong Fire point of view. Its main qi, 壬, puts Direct Officer (정관 正官) in the Jeong-hae partner seat. In branch relations, 亥 clashes with 巳 (Snake) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 寅 (Tiger), so a partner with a Snake or Tiger day branch will feel noticeably different to a Jeong-hae person. 丁亥 is pillar 24 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

What is the hidden combination in Jeong-hae?

The main hidden stem of 亥 is 壬 Water, and 丁 with 壬 is one of the five heavenly stem combinations (丁壬合). Classical readers see it as a built-in affinity between you and your partner seat.

Doesn't Water put out a candle?

In the simple element cycle, yes. But saju also reads combinations, and 丁 bonds with 壬. Plus 亥 holds 甲 Wood, which feeds Fire. The pillar is more harmonious than it first looks.

What kind of partner suits Jeong-hae?

Someone principled and kind who respects your judgment. You naturally admire integrity. Make sure it comes with warmth and equality, not just good rules.
