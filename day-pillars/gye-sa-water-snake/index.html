Gye-sa: The Rain in Sunshine

Water Snake · 계사 · day pillar

A sunshower in early summer. Warm, romantic, resourceful, and full of surprising contradictions.

  • Yin Water stem
  • Snake branch · Fire
  • Pillar 30 of 60
  • Partner seat: Direct Wealth 정재
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Gye-sa (癸巳) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Rain birthday type (Gye Water) standing on the Snake, a sunshower in early summer. Warm, romantic, resourceful, and full of surprising contradictions.

Gye-sa places the Rain on 巳, the Snake: early summer, Fire rising, with 丙 Fire as the branch’s main qi and 戊 Earth and 庚 Metal inside. Water controls Fire, so this branch is Gye’s Wealth, Direct Wealth (정재) here. And there is a hidden bond: 戊 inside the Snake combines with 癸 (戊癸合), one of the five heavenly combinations. Rain falling through bright sunshine is a sunshower, which Korean folklore calls 호랑이 장가가는 날, the day the tiger gets married. That is your pillar: a little magical, a little contradictory, and lovely to watch.

You are warm, romantic and resourceful. You have a practical streak and a dreamy one, and they coexist without much fuss. The hidden 庚 Metal is your Resource, a source of support and resilience. The hidden 戊 is your Direct Officer, adding a sense of responsibility. The caution is drying out. Small rain under a strong sun can vanish, and you may pour your energy into a partner, a job or a plan until there is little left for you.

Gye-sa in love

Direct Wealth in the partner seat (spouse palace), plus the hidden 戊癸 combination, makes love central for Gye-sa. You want a warm, stable partner and a comfortable life together. You are affectionate, attentive and practical, the partner who plans the date and remembers to book the table.

You may lose yourself in a relationship or give more than you receive. Keep your own friends and routines. A partner with Metal energy refills you; one with Water energy keeps you company emotionally. You shine brightest with someone who appreciates both your romance and your common sense.

As a friend

You are the friend who is both fun and useful: great at parties and great at helping someone budget for a move. You are warm, generous and practical. You can over-give. Let your friends take a turn.

Red flags

  • You pour yourself into your partner and forget to refill.
  • You worry about stability quietly instead of talking about it.
  • You confuse being useful with being loved.
  • You fall for a romantic idea and ignore the practical red flags you would spot in a friend’s relationship.

At work

Gye-sa thrives in finance, design, hospitality, real estate, marketing, client services and creative businesses with a practical side. You combine warmth with competence. Protect your energy; the sun is generous, but it also dries things out.

Gye-sa vs the other Gye Water pillars

Gye-sa is the most romantic and resource-minded Gye pillar, thanks to Direct Wealth and the hidden 戊癸 bond in its partner seat. Gye-hae (癸亥), its clash partner (巳亥), is Water on Water, vast and emotional where you are warm and practical. Gye-yu (癸酉) is cerebral, Gye-myo (癸卯) playful, Gye-mi (癸未) and Gye-chuk (癸丑) pressured by Seven Killings. You look for warmth and stability in love; Gye-yu looks for a mind.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gye-sa: the 巳 Snake branch is Fire and hides 戊 (Direct Officer), 庚 (Direct Resource), 丙 (Direct Wealth), read from a Gye Water point of view. Its main qi, 丙, puts Direct Wealth (정재 正財) in the Gye-sa partner seat. In branch relations, 巳 clashes with 亥 (Pig) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 申 (Monkey), so a partner with a Pig or Monkey day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gye-sa person. 癸巳 is pillar 30 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

What is the hidden combination in Gye-sa?

The 巳 branch holds 戊 Earth, and 戊 with 癸 forms a heavenly stem combination (戊癸合). It is read as a natural pull toward partnership.

Is Gye-sa a weak Day Master?

Water on a Fire branch can lean weaker, though the hidden 庚 Metal offers support. The month you were born in tips the balance one way or the other.

What does Gye-sa need in love?

Warmth, stability and reciprocity. You give a lot of practical care, so a partner who notices and returns it is essential.
