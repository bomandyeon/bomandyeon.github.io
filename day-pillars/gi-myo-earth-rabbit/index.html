Gi-myo: The Garden of Spring Shoots

A garden field bursting with spring shoots. Gentle, adaptable, under pressure, and endlessly good at making things grow.

  • Yin Earth stem
  • Rabbit branch · Wood
  • Pillar 16 of 60
  • Partner seat: Seven Killings 편관
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Short answer

Gi-myo (己卯) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Garden Field birthday type (Gi Earth) standing on the Rabbit, a garden field bursting with spring shoots. Gentle, adaptable, under pressure, and endlessly good at making things grow.

Gi-myo places the Garden Field on 卯, the Rabbit: pure spring Wood. Wood breaks up Earth, so for Gi this branch is Seven Killings (편관), a demanding energy. In plain terms, your garden is crowded with new shoots pushing up through the soil. It is lively, green and beautiful, and also exhausting for the ground that has to feed all of it.

You are gentle and accommodating, with a gift for nurturing people and projects. You feel pressure strongly, from bosses, partners and your own expectations. Sometimes you shrink to keep everyone happy. The Qiong Tong Bao Jian suggests Gi under strong Wood benefits from 丙 Fire, the sun that turns Wood's pressure into growth. In life that means mentors, education and warmth. With that support, Gi-myo turns pressure into flourishing, and your garden becomes the one everyone stops to admire.

BomThings grow wherever you are, people, plants, projects. That is a rare and generous gift.

Gi-myo in love

Seven Killings in the partner seat (spouse palace) often means you are drawn to strong, charismatic, sometimes dominant partners. You like being swept up. You also tend to accommodate too much and lose track of what you want.

You deserve a partner who is strong and gentle. Watch for relationships where you are always adapting and they are never adjusting. When you find someone who respects your boundaries, you are a devoted, tender partner who makes their life bloom.

As a friend

You are the soft, supportive friend, the one who checks in and sends a care package. You are easy to be around and rarely start conflict. You can let people take advantage of your kindness. Practice saying no to small things; it makes the big ones easier.

Red flags

  • You shrink yourself to fit someone else's plans.
  • You fall for strong personalities that walk all over you.
  • You say yes to everything and then feel trampled.
  • You confuse being needed with being valued.

YeonYou are not a doormat with a nice garden. Say no today. Watch how fast the world adjusts.

At work

Gi-myo does well in nurturing and growth-focused roles: teaching, coaching, gardening and landscaping, social work, community programs, customer success. You are patient with people others give up on. Choose supportive workplaces. Harsh bosses feel like weeds you cannot pull; good ones help you thrive.

Gi-myo vs the other Gi Earth pillars

Gi-myo feels the most pressure of the Gi pillars, since its branch is pure Wood. Gi-yu (己酉), its clash partner (卯酉), sits on Metal, which cuts Wood, so Gi-yu is crafty and confident where you are accommodating. Gi-sa (己巳) has Fire support and feels safer. Gi-chuk (己丑) and Gi-mi (己未) stand on their own Earth and are more stubborn. Gi-hae (己亥) is romantic and practical. Only Gi-myo has Seven Killings in the partner seat, which is why your love life tends toward intensity and why boundaries matter so much.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gi-myo: the 卯 Rabbit branch is Wood and hides 甲 (Direct Officer), 乙 (Seven Killings), read from a Gi Earth point of view. Its main qi, 乙, puts Seven Killings (편관 偏官) in the Gi-myo partner seat. In branch relations, 卯 clashes with 酉 (Rooster) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 戌 (Dog), so a partner with a Rooster or Dog day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gi-myo person. 己卯 is pillar 16 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Is Gi-myo a weak day pillar?

Wood in the branch controls Gi Earth, so many Gi-myo charts lean weaker. That depends on the full chart. Weak in saju means sensitive to environment, which is why supportive people matter so much.

Why is Gi-myo drawn to strong partners?

Seven Killings, a powerful partner energy, sits in your partner seat. Being aware of it helps you choose strength that comes with kindness.

What helps Gi-myo thrive?

Fire, which turns Wood's pressure into support. Mentors, learning, warmth and boundaries are the practical version.
