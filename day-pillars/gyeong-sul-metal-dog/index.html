Gyeong-sul: The Sword in the Stone

A sword set into a stone fortress. Formidable, loyal, principled, and dramatic in the best possible way.

  • Yang Metal stem
  • Dog branch · Earth
  • Pillar 47 of 60
  • Spouse palace: Indirect Resource 편인
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Gyeong-sul places the Sword on 戌, the Dog: dry autumn earth storing 丁 Fire and 辛 Metal. Earth produces Metal, so this is Gyeong on its Resource, Indirect Resource (편인) here. It is also one of the four classic 괴강 (魁罡) pillars. Picture a legendary sword set into stone on a mountain ridge in autumn. Only the right person can pull it out. The hidden 丁 Fire is the forge, keeping the blade honed.

You are formidable, principled and fiercely loyal. You have a warrior's code and a scholar's mind. Indirect Resource gives you unusual insight and a taste for niche knowledge. 괴강 energy makes you intense, dramatic and capable of great achievements. The caution is rigidity. Dry Earth plus Metal can be inflexible, and you can turn convictions into walls.

BomYou protect what you love with everything you have. People feel safe in your shadow.

Gyeong-sul in love

Indirect Resource in the spouse palace points to a partner who is unconventional, wise or mysterious. You want someone who intrigues you and earns your respect. You are loyal and protective, a guardian in love.

You can be controlling or emotionally guarded, and you may expect partners to prove themselves. You need someone strong enough to stand beside you and warm enough to help you open up. Water energy softens your edges.

As a friend

You are the friend who protects the pack. You are loyal, honest and occasionally terrifying. You keep promises and expect the same. You can be stubborn and quick to judge. Your friends know you will always have their back.

You are also the friend who quietly knows a lot about something unexpected, medieval history, fountain pens, the rules of a sport nobody plays. Once people get past your armor, they find a curious, almost nerdy mind and a dry sense of humor that only comes out after the second coffee. Let more people see that side earlier. It is the best argument for getting through the gate.

Red flags

  • You make people earn your trust over and over.
  • You treat your convictions as facts.
  • You guard your feelings like a fortress under siege.
  • You go to war over things that needed a conversation.

YeonYou are not King Arthur's sword. People do not need to prove they are worthy before you text back.

At work

Gyeong-sul is suited to leadership, law, security, research, military, specialized expertise and entrepreneurship. You are decisive and brave. Your growth area is flexibility; the strongest swords are tempered, not just hard.

Gyeong-sul vs the other Gyeong Metal pillars

Gyeong-sul is the most formidable and principled Gyeong pillar. Gyeong-jin (庚辰), its clash partner (辰戌), is the other 괴강 Gyeong: wet and strategic where you are dry and protective. Gyeong-o (庚午) is dutiful, Gyeong-sin (庚申) blunt and self-sufficient, Gyeong-in (庚寅) enterprising, Gyeong-ja (庚子) witty. Your spouse palace holds Resource, so you look for a wise or unusual partner, while Gyeong-o looks for a principled one.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gyeong-sul: the 戌 Dog branch is Earth and hides 辛 (Rob Wealth), 丁 (Direct Officer), 戊 (Indirect Resource), read from a Gyeong Metal point of view. Its main qi, 戊, puts Indirect Resource (편인 偏印) in the Gyeong-sul spouse palace. In branch relations, 戌 clashes with 辰 (Dragon) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 卯 (Rabbit), so a partner with a Dragon or Rabbit day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gyeong-sul person. 庚戌 is pillar 47 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

What does 괴강 mean for Gyeong-sul?

괴강 (魁罡) is a classical label for a few day pillars, including 庚戌, associated with strong will, leadership and intensity. We read it as personality, not fate.

Is Gyeong-sul a strong Day Master?

Often, since Earth supports Metal and the branch holds 辛 Metal. Your season of birth still has the final say.

What balances Gyeong-sul?

Water for expression and flexibility, and Fire, which the branch already holds, for refinement. Openness and humility help.
