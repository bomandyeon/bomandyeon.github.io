Im-in: The River Through the Forest

A broad river feeding a spring forest. Generous, expressive, optimistic, and always nurturing something.

  • Yang Water stem
  • Tiger branch · Wood
  • Pillar 39 of 60
  • Spouse palace: Eating God 식신
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Im-in places the Ocean on 寅, the Tiger: the first branch of spring, packed with 甲 Wood, with 丙 Fire and 戊 Earth tucked inside. Water feeds Wood, so this branch is Im's output, the Eating God (식신). Picture a wide river winding through a forest in early spring. Trees drink from its banks, deer come down to the water, sunlight flickers through new leaves. Everything near it grows.

You are generous, expressive and optimistic. You nurture people, ideas and projects almost without trying. Eating God energy makes you creative, easygoing and fond of the good things: food, music, long walks, friends around a table. Some schools link the Tiger branch to 역마, the travel star, and many Im-in people love movement, new cities and changes of scene. The hidden 丙 Fire is your Wealth, so you have a practical, resourceful side too. The caution is overgiving. Water pouring into Wood drains, and you can run dry while everyone around you flourishes and forgets to check on the river.

BomYou help everything around you grow, from friends to houseplants to half-formed ideas. People remember your generosity for years.

Im-in in love

Eating God in the spouse palace makes you an affectionate, playful and generous partner. You love shared adventures, cooking together and laughing at nothing. You naturally support your partner's growth and celebrate their wins loudly.

You can also avoid conflict to keep the mood nice, or say yes to plans you are too tired for. Restlessness may show up when life gets repetitive. A partner with Metal energy, clear and supportive, refills your water. One who shares your love of adventure keeps the spark lit. Most of all, ask to be cared for. You are allowed to receive.

As a friend

You are the friend who feeds people, plans trips and believes in everyone's dreams out loud. Your home is where people end up after a bad week. You can overextend and burn out quietly, then feel guilty for needing a break. Your friends would happily return the favor if you let them.

Red flags

  • You pour into everyone else and forget your own roots.
  • You avoid hard conversations to keep the mood sunny.
  • You get restless the moment life becomes routine.
  • You say yes to every adventure, including the exhausting ones.

YeonA river that only gives turns into a dry riverbed. Take something back this week. A nap counts.

At work

Im-in thrives in teaching, creative fields, travel, hospitality, food, coaching, media and entrepreneurship. You inspire people and make growth feel natural. Protect your energy with real boundaries. Rivers need rain, and so do you.

Im-in vs the other Im Water pillars

Im-in is the most generous and expressive of the Im pillars. Im-sin (壬申), its clash partner (寅申), sits on its Resource and absorbs and analyzes where you give and create. Im-ja (壬子) is deeper and more intense. Im-o (壬午) is romantic and practical, with a hidden combination in its spouse palace. Im-jin (壬辰) and Im-sul (壬戌) carry Seven Killings and live with more pressure. Your spouse palace holds the Eating God, so your relationships lean toward fun and nurturing, while your siblings look for minds, warmth or intensity.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Im-in: the 寅 Tiger branch is Wood and hides 戊 (Seven Killings), 丙 (Indirect Wealth), 甲 (Eating God), read from a Im Water point of view. Its main qi, 甲, puts Eating God (식신 食神) in the Im-in spouse palace. In branch relations, 寅 clashes with 申 (Monkey) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 亥 (Pig), so a partner with a Monkey or Pig day branch will feel noticeably different to a Im-in person. 壬寅 is pillar 39 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Why is Im-in so generous?

The Tiger branch is Wood, which Water feeds. In saju, the element you feed is your output: expression, creativity and nurturing. Im-in has that output right in its spouse palace.

Is Im-in a weak Day Master?

Water that pours heavily into Wood can lean weaker, depending on the full chart. Metal and Water support, and plenty of rest, make a real difference.

What kind of partner suits Im-in?

Someone who refills you, enjoys your adventures and gives back the care you give. Metal types often provide the support you forget to ask for.
