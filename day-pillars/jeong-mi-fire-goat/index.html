Jeong-mi: The Lantern at the Summer Festival

Fire Goat · 정미 · day pillar

Lanterns glowing over a warm summer night. Warm, sociable, nurturing, and stubborn about the people it loves.

  • Yin Fire stem
  • Goat branch · Earth
  • Pillar 44 of 60
  • Partner seat: Eating God 식신
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Jeong-mi (丁未) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Candle birthday type (Jeong Fire) standing on the Goat, lanterns glowing over a warm summer night. Warm, sociable, nurturing, and stubborn about the people it loves.

Jeong-mi places the Candle on 未, the Goat: hot, dry earth at the end of summer, which stores 丁 Fire and 乙 Wood inside. So this candle has a hidden root in its own soil and a little fuel tucked away for later. Picture paper lanterns strung over a night market in late July, warm air, smoke from food stalls, everyone a little sunburned and happy.

You are warm, sociable and generous, with a gift for making gatherings feel like home. Earth is your output, here the Eating God (식신), so you express yourself through feeding, making and caring. You are tougher than you look and fiercely protective of your people. 2027 is a 丁未 year, the same pillar as yours, so expect people to ask you about it. Your shadow is dryness and stubbornness. Hot earth hardens, and Jeong-mi can dig in on grudges or habits long after they stopped serving anyone.

Jeong-mi in love

Eating God in the partner seat (spouse palace) makes you an affectionate, nurturing partner. You love making a home, cooking for someone and building rituals: Sunday markets, Friday movies, annual trips. You are loyal and you enjoy the everyday.

You can be possessive about your people and sensitive to feeling replaced. You may also take care of a partner so thoroughly that they never learn to take care of you. Ask for things. Partners with Water energy cool and refresh you, and help you see beyond your own patch of warm ground.

As a friend

You are the friend who feeds people and remembers birthdays. You are loyal for life and you will fight for your friends. You can be a little territorial about who is in the circle. Let new people in; your table has room.

Red flags

  • You care for everyone and expect them to notice without being told.
  • You hold a grudge like a family recipe.
  • You get jealous of new friends in your partner’s life.
  • You refuse to change a plan because you already pictured it.

YeonYour love is not a debt. Stop keeping a mental ledger of every dinner you cooked. Ask for what you need instead.

At work

Jeong-mi suits warm, hands-on, people-centered work: food, hospitality, teaching, nursing support roles, community programs, crafts, small business. You create loyalty in teams and customers. Keep your energy watered: rest, reflection and people who refill you rather than just enjoy you.

Jeong-mi vs the other Jeong Fire pillars

Jeong-mi is the warmest and most communal Jeong pillar. Jeong-chuk (丁丑) shares the Eating God partner seat but sits on winter earth (丑未 clash), so where you host the summer party, they keep the winter cabin. Jeong-sa (丁巳) is equally warm but louder and more competitive. Jeong-myo (丁卯) is more introverted, Jeong-hae (丁亥) more principled, Jeong-yu (丁酉) more polished. You are the candle that turns into a lantern festival when enough people show up.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Jeong-mi: the 未 Goat branch is Earth and hides 丁 (Friend), 乙 (Indirect Resource), 己 (Eating God), read from a Jeong Fire point of view. Its main qi, 己, puts Eating God (식신 食神) in the Jeong-mi partner seat. In branch relations, 未 clashes with 丑 (Ox) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 午 (Horse), so a partner with a Ox or Horse day branch will feel noticeably different to a Jeong-mi person. 丁未 is pillar 44 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Does 2027 matter for Jeong-mi?

2027 is a 丁未 year, the same stem and branch as your day pillar. In saju that is sometimes called meeting your own pillar. We read it as a year that may feel extra ‘you’, not as a prediction.

Is Jeong-mi a strong Day Master?

The 未 branch stores Fire and Wood, both supportive, so Jeong-mi often has a hidden root. Overall strength still depends on your birth month and other pillars.

How does Jeong-mi love?

Through care, food, presence and ritual. If you date one, show up consistently and say thank you out loud. It matters more than you think.
