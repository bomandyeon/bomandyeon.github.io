Gi-hae: The Riverside Field

A field beside a winter river. Gentle, practical, romantic, and surprisingly good at going with the flow.

  • Yin Earth stem
  • Pig branch · Water
  • Pillar 36 of 60
  • Partner seat: Direct Wealth 정재
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Short answer

Gi-hae (己亥) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Garden Field birthday type (Gi Earth) standing on the Pig, a field beside a winter river. Gentle, practical, romantic, and surprisingly good at going with the flow.

Gi-hae places the Garden Field on 亥, the Pig: deep winter Water with 甲 Wood and a little 戊 Earth inside. Earth controls Water, so this is Gi sitting on its Wealth, Direct Wealth (정재) here. And there is a secret: the hidden 甲 Wood combines with 己 (甲己合), one of the five heavenly combinations. Picture rich farmland on a riverbank in winter, irrigated, fertile, with tree roots holding the banks.

You are gentle, practical and adaptable. You manage resources well and you are good at turning everyday life into something comfortable. The river flows around you constantly, so you are flexible and attuned to people's needs. The hidden 甲己 bond gives you a strong pull toward partnership. The classical caution is that too much Water can wash a field away. You can be swept up by other people's priorities, lose your boundaries and forget where your soil ends and theirs begins.

BomYou make everyday life feel easy and abundant for the people you love. That is a deep kind of care.

Gi-hae in love

Direct Wealth in the partner seat (spouse palace) and the hidden 甲己 combination make love central for Gi-hae. You want a stable, kind partner and a comfortable home. You are affectionate, attentive and practical in love, the partner who handles groceries and knows exactly what to say.

You may give too much or adapt too easily. Water floods; soil erodes. Hold your banks. A partner with Fire or Earth energy, warm and grounding, helps you stay solid while you share your abundance.

As a friend

You are the friend who makes everyone feel comfortable, organizes the potluck and remembers dietary restrictions. You go with the flow and rarely complain. Sometimes you need to complain. Your friends would honestly like to hear it.

Red flags

  • You let other people's plans become your plans by default.
  • You give practical help instead of saying what you feel.
  • You confuse flexibility with having no preferences.
  • You stay in comfortable situations long after they stopped feeling good.

YeonGoing with the flow is lovely until you end up three towns downstream. Pick a direction for yourself.

At work

Gi-hae suits practical, people-centered work: finance, hospitality, property management, logistics, customer relations, small business. You are resourceful and calm. Set clear boundaries around your time. People will happily keep taking it.

Gi-hae vs the other Gi Earth pillars

Gi-hae is the most romantic and resource-minded Gi pillar. Gi-sa (己巳), its clash partner (巳亥), sits on Fire and looks for a nurturing, wise partner; you look for a stable, practical one. Gi-chuk (己丑) and Gi-mi (己未) want equals and are more stubborn. Gi-myo (己卯) is pressured, Gi-yu (己酉) crafty. Your partner seat holds both Wealth and a hidden combination, which is why partnership feels like such a natural part of your life.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gi-hae: the 亥 Pig branch is Water and hides 戊 (Rob Wealth), 甲 (Direct Officer), 壬 (Direct Wealth), read from a Gi Earth point of view. Its main qi, 壬, puts Direct Wealth (정재 正財) in the Gi-hae partner seat. In branch relations, 亥 clashes with 巳 (Snake) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 寅 (Tiger), so a partner with a Snake or Tiger day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gi-hae person. 己亥 is pillar 36 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

What is the hidden combination in Gi-hae?

The 亥 branch holds 甲 Wood, and 甲 with 己 is a heavenly stem combination (甲己合). Classical readers see it as a built-in affinity between you and partnership.

Is Gi-hae a weak Day Master?

Earth on Water can lean weaker, depending on the full chart. Fire and Earth support make a big difference.

What does Gi-hae need in love?

Stability and appreciation. You give a lot of practical care, so a partner who notices and reciprocates is essential.
