Gyeong-in: The Axe in the Forest

An axe in a spring forest. Enterprising, bold, restless, and always clearing a path to the next thing.

  • Yang Metal stem
  • Tiger branch · Wood
  • Pillar 27 of 60
  • Partner seat: Indirect Wealth 편재
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Short answer

Gyeong-in (庚寅) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Sword birthday type (Gyeong Metal) standing on the Tiger, an axe in a spring forest. Enterprising, bold, restless, and always clearing a path to the next thing.

Gyeong-in places the Sword on 寅, the Tiger: the first branch of spring, packed with 甲 Wood. Metal cuts Wood, so Wood is Gyeong's Wealth, Indirect Wealth (편재) here. The Tiger also holds 丙 Fire, Seven Killings for Gyeong, so there is pressure too. Picture an axe in a dense spring forest, sunlight streaming through, trees to fell, paths to clear. You are tool and adventurer at once.

You are enterprising, bold and restless. You see opportunity and go after it. You like action, movement and tangible results. The Tiger branch is often linked to 역마 (the "travel horse" star) in some schools, and many Gyeong-in people love travel and change. The caution is that Metal sitting on strong Wood and Fire can feel unrooted. You can chase too much, burn energy fast and struggle to rest.

BomYou make things happen. Your energy and courage open doors for everyone around you.

Gyeong-in in love

Indirect Wealth in the partner seat (spouse palace) makes you a generous, adventurous partner. You love shared activities: travel, sports, new restaurants, weekend projects. You are fun, practical and decisive.

You may be restless in routine and struggle with emotional depth. You can also show love through action and spending rather than vulnerability. A partner with Earth energy, grounding and supportive, gives you a home base. Someone who shares your sense of adventure keeps the spark alive.

As a friend

You are the friend who plans the road trip and drives most of the way. You are generous, energetic and good in a crisis. You can be impatient with slow friends and you sometimes disappear when a new project grabs you. Check in with your oldest friends; they miss you.

Your friends also know you as the one who gets things moving. When the group chat has been saying 'we should do a trip' for two years, you are the person who finally books the cabin and sends the payment link. That energy keeps friendships alive. Just leave room for slower friends to say yes in their own time.

Red flags

  • You chase the next adventure before finishing this one.
  • You show love with plans and gifts instead of feelings.
  • You get bored in peaceful relationships.
  • You burn out because resting feels like losing.

YeonA relationship is not a weekend trip you can extend or cancel. Unpack the bag, Gyeong-in. Stay a while.

At work

Gyeong-in thrives in entrepreneurial, active and results-driven roles: sales, business development, sports, logistics, construction, travel, field work. You make things happen. Pair yourself with steady collaborators who handle the details you skip.

Gyeong-in vs the other Gyeong Metal pillars

Gyeong-in is the most enterprising and restless Gyeong pillar. Gyeong-sin (庚申), its clash partner (寅申), stands on pure Metal and is self-sufficient where you are always moving. Gyeong-o (庚午) is principled and dutiful, Gyeong-jin (庚辰) and Gyeong-sul (庚戌) are 괴강 pillars, more commanding. Gyeong-ja (庚子) is witty and rebellious. Your partner seat holds Wealth, so your relationships tend to be active and generous, while your siblings look for character, mystery or wit.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gyeong-in: the 寅 Tiger branch is Wood and hides 戊 (Indirect Resource), 丙 (Seven Killings), 甲 (Indirect Wealth), read from a Gyeong Metal point of view. Its main qi, 甲, puts Indirect Wealth (편재 偏財) in the Gyeong-in partner seat. In branch relations, 寅 clashes with 申 (Monkey) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 亥 (Pig), so a partner with a Monkey or Pig day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gyeong-in person. 庚寅 is pillar 27 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Is Gyeong-in an adventurous day pillar?

Often. The Tiger branch is active and some schools link it to the travel star (역마). Combined with Wealth in the partner seat, it produces an enterprising, restless personality.

Is Gyeong-in a weak Day Master?

Metal on Wood and Fire can lean weaker, depending on the full chart. Earth support, mentors, rest and grounding help a lot.

What kind of partner suits Gyeong-in?

Someone grounding but fun, who supports your adventures and gives you a home base. Earth types often fit well.
