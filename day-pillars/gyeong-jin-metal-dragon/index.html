Gyeong-jin: The Dragon's Blade

A blade buried in a dragon's hoard. Commanding, strategic, intensely loyal, and capable of very big moves.

  • Yang Metal stem
  • Dragon branch · Earth
  • Pillar 17 of 60
  • Spouse palace: Indirect Resource 편인
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Gyeong-jin places the Sword on 辰, the Dragon: wet spring earth that stores Water and Wood. Earth produces Metal, so this branch is Gyeong's Resource, Indirect Resource (편인) here. It is also one of the four classic 괴강 (魁罡) pillars, linked to strong will, leadership and intensity. Picture a legendary sword resting in a dragon's cave, half buried in damp earth, surrounded by treasure. Powerful, mysterious, waiting for the right moment.

You are commanding and strategic. You think big and act decisively. You have unusual ideas and a talent for seeing patterns, the Indirect Resource gift. The Dragon's hidden 乙 Wood combines with 庚 (乙庚合), a heavenly combination that adds charm and pull. The 괴강 reputation also comes with a caution: you can be all or nothing, dominant in conversations, and slow to accept that someone else might be right.

BomYou think in big moves and have the courage to make them. People feel braver when you are leading.

Gyeong-jin in love

Indirect Resource in the spouse palace often points to a partner who is unconventional, intellectual or a bit mysterious. You are drawn to people who intrigue you. The hidden 乙庚 combination also makes you magnetic, and you know it.

You can be controlling, or you may test partners to see if they are strong enough. You expect loyalty and offer it fiercely. You need a partner with their own strength who will not be steamrolled, plus Water energy to keep you flexible and honest.

As a friend

You are the friend people follow. You lead the group, plan the adventure and defend your people. You can be intimidating and you do not suffer fools. Once you trust someone, you are loyal for life. Remember to listen as much as you lead.

Red flags

  • You test people to see if they are strong enough to stay.
  • You take control of situations that did not need a commander.
  • You go all in or all out, with nothing in between.
  • You treat disagreement as disrespect.

YeonNot every relationship needs a loyalty exam. Some people are just nice. Let them be nice.

At work

Gyeong-jin is suited to leadership and strategy: executive roles, entrepreneurship, law, finance, research, military strategy, consulting. You see the big picture and act on it. Your growth area is collaboration. The dragon's sword is powerful, but even legends need allies.

You are also unusually good at long-range plans. Where others see next quarter, you see the next five years, and you are often right about where an industry, a friendship group or a city neighborhood is heading.

Gyeong-jin vs the other Gyeong Metal pillars

Gyeong-jin is the most strategic and magnetic Gyeong pillar. Gyeong-sul (庚戌), its clash partner (辰戌), is the other 괴강 Gyeong: dry and fiery where you are wet and mysterious. Gyeong-sin (庚申) is pure Metal and blunt; you are calculating. Gyeong-o (庚午) is honorable and dutiful, Gyeong-in (庚寅) enterprising, Gyeong-ja (庚子) witty and rebellious. Your spouse palace holds Resource plus a hidden combination, which is why your romantic life is intense and a little mysterious.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gyeong-jin: the 辰 Dragon branch is Earth and hides 乙 (Direct Wealth), 癸 (Hurting Officer), 戊 (Indirect Resource), read from a Gyeong Metal point of view. Its main qi, 戊, puts Indirect Resource (편인 偏印) in the Gyeong-jin spouse palace. In branch relations, 辰 clashes with 戌 (Dog) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 酉 (Rooster), so a partner with a Dog or Rooster day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gyeong-jin person. 庚辰 is pillar 17 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

What does 괴강 mean for Gyeong-jin?

괴강 (魁罡) is a classical label for a few strong day pillars, including 庚辰. It is associated with leadership, intensity and willpower. We read it as personality, not prediction.

What is the hidden combination in Gyeong-jin?

The 辰 branch holds 乙 Wood, and 乙 with 庚 is a heavenly stem combination (乙庚合). It adds magnetism and a strong pull toward partnership.

What balances Gyeong-jin?

Water to soften and express Metal, and Fire to refine it. Honest feedback, creative outlets and humility help.
