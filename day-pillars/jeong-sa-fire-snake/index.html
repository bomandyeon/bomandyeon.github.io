Jeong-sa: The Torch at the Start of Summer

A torch burning in early summer heat. Bold, competitive, passionate, and far louder than the average candle.

  • Yin Fire stem
  • Snake branch · Fire
  • Pillar 54 of 60
  • Spouse palace: Rob Wealth 겁재
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Jeong-sa sets the Candle on 巳, the Snake: early summer, Fire rising, with 丙 Fire as its main qi. It is one of the 간여지동 pillars, stem and branch both Fire. A candle sitting on the sun is no longer a candle. It is a torch. Picture flames held high at a May festival, light flickering on faces, a lot of heat in a small space.

You are passionate, quick and competitive. You feel things intensely and act on them fast. The Snake also hides 庚 Metal, your Direct Wealth, so you are sharper with resources and strategy than people expect. The spouse palace holds 丙 Fire, Rob Wealth (겁재), a competitive peer energy. Classical texts would tell a Fire-heavy Jeong to find Water, to cool and give direction. Without it, your passion becomes impatience, and you can burn bridges you later wish you had kept.

BomWhen you care about something, you light it up for everyone. Your passion gives other people permission to care loudly too.

Jeong-sa in love

Rob Wealth in the spouse palace makes love feel like a high-stakes game. You are drawn to confident, competitive people, and you may compete with them. You love intensely, flirt boldly and expect devotion in return.

Jealousy can be a theme, both feeling it and provoking it. You need a partner with their own life, plus a cool head: Water energy, calm and reflective, balances your heat beautifully. When you trust someone, you are fiercely loyal and endlessly fun to be with.

As a friend

You are the friend who organizes the protest, the trip and the surprise party. Your loyalty is fierce and your temper is quick. You forgive faster than you think, but you also say things in the heat of the moment. A short pause before replying saves you many friendships.

Red flags

  • You compete with the person you are supposed to be on a team with.
  • You test loyalty by provoking a little jealousy.
  • You say the burning thing and regret it later.
  • You go all in, then all out, with no middle setting.

YeonJealousy is not proof of love. If you have to start a fire to see if they run toward you, the problem is the fire.

At work

Jeong-sa does well in high-energy, competitive environments: sales, entrepreneurship, media, sports, politics, performance, litigation. You are persuasive and fearless. Your growth edge is patience and collaboration. You do not need to win every meeting. You need to win the right ones.

Jeong-sa vs the other Jeong Fire pillars

Jeong-sa is the boldest and most fiery Jeong pillar. Jeong-hae (丁亥) is its clash partner (巳亥), the calm lantern on water where you are a torch. Jeong-mi (丁未) is equally warm but more nurturing and communal; Jeong-myo (丁卯) is introverted and bookish. Jeong-chuk (丁丑) is the patient opposite of your intensity. Jeong-yu (丁酉) shares your sharpness with resources but channels it into taste. Only you have a competitive peer in the spouse palace, which makes your relationships passionate, loyal and occasionally dramatic.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Jeong-sa: the 巳 Snake branch is Fire and hides 戊 (Hurting Officer), 庚 (Direct Wealth), 丙 (Rob Wealth), read from a Jeong Fire point of view. Its main qi, 丙, puts Rob Wealth (겁재 劫財) in the Jeong-sa spouse palace. In branch relations, 巳 clashes with 亥 (Pig) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 申 (Monkey), so a partner with a Pig or Monkey day branch will feel noticeably different to a Jeong-sa person. 丁巳 is pillar 54 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Is Jeong-sa a strong Day Master?

Usually. Both stem and branch are Fire (간여지동), and the Snake's main qi is 丙 Fire, so the Day Master has strong support. Strength here means drive and resilience, not superiority.

Why is Jeong-sa so competitive in love?

Rob Wealth, a peer energy, sits in your spouse palace. Classically it adds competition and intensity to relationships. Knowing that helps you channel it into shared goals instead of rivalry.

What balances Jeong-sa?

Water first, for cooling and direction, and Metal to give your energy something useful to work on. Calm friends, clear goals and a pause before replying are the daily version.
