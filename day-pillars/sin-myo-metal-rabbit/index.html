Sin-myo: The Jewel Among Blossoms

Metal Rabbit · 신묘 · day pillar

A gem resting in a spring flower garden. Charming, pleasure-loving, generous, and hard to keep in one place.

  • Rabbit branch · Wood

LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Sin-myo (辛卯) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Jewel birthday type (Sin Metal) standing on the Rabbit, a gem resting in a spring flower garden. Charming, pleasure-loving, generous, and hard to keep in one place.

Sin-myo places 辛, the Jewel, on 卯, the Rabbit: pure spring Wood at its April peak. Metal cuts Wood, so for a Sin birthday type (Day Master) Wood is Wealth, Indirect Wealth (편재) here. Picture a delicate necklace dropped on a bed of cherry blossoms. Petals, light, color, a picnic nobody wants to leave. It is pretty, a little indulgent and completely sincere about enjoying itself.

You are charming and sociable, with a real appetite for beauty, food, travel and people. Indirect Wealth reads as resourcefulness and generosity: you spot the good deal, the hidden café, the friend of a friend who can help. Some schools treat 卯 as a 도화 (peach blossom) branch, which is why Sin-myo is often described as socially magnetic. The classical caution is that a soft jewel sitting on a lot of Wood has to work hard to “cut” it. Translation: you can spread yourself across too many plans, too many people and too many small pleasures, and end the month dazzling and depleted.

Underneath the fun there is a sharper mind than people expect. You size up situations fast and you have strong opinions about quality. You simply prefer to deliver them with a smile and a recommendation.

Sin-myo in love

With Indirect Wealth in your partner seat (spouse palace), love is something you want to enjoy together. You are the partner who books the rooftop table, buys tickets to the obscure exhibition and remembers that they mentioned wanting to see the sea. You are playful, affectionate and generous with time and attention.

The shadow is restlessness. You can get twitchy when things become routine, and you may lean on gifts and outings to avoid harder conversations. Romance is not only the highlight reel. Partners with Earth energy, steady and supportive, give your jewel a setting. Partners with Metal energy, your own element, help you feel strong enough to slow down and be known.

As a friend

You are the friend who makes plans happen: birthday dinners, beach weekends, spontaneous karaoke. You are generous and you love introducing people. You can be a little flaky when something shinier comes up, and you sometimes judge friends’ taste out loud. Your friends forgive you because life is simply more fun with you in the group chat.

Red flags

  • You chase the next pretty thing before enjoying this one.
  • You answer heavy feelings with a cute dinner plan.
  • You overextend, then vanish for a week to recover.
  • You keep relationships light so nobody can get close enough to disappoint you.

At work

Sin-myo shines in beauty, hospitality, retail, events, travel, lifestyle media, sales and brand work. You are persuasive and good at reading what people want. Build a steady routine underneath the sparkle. Your talent lands best when it has a base: a calendar, a budget, a few colleagues who say no to you.

Sin-myo vs the other Sin Metal pillars

Sin-myo is the most playful and pleasure-minded of the Sin pillars. Sin-yu (辛酉) is its clash partner (卯酉), pure Metal and serious about standards where you are light and social. Sin-sa (辛巳) seeks a principled partner to admire. Sin-hae (辛亥) is sharper and more rebellious with words. Sin-mi (辛未) and Sin-chuk (辛丑) sit on Resource Earth and live more inwardly. Your partner seat holds Wealth, so your love life leans generous, active and fun, while your siblings look for wisdom, principle or wit.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Sin-myo: the 卯 Rabbit branch is Wood and hides 甲 (Direct Wealth), 乙 (Indirect Wealth), read from a Sin Metal point of view. Its main qi, 乙, puts Indirect Wealth (편재 偏財) in the Sin-myo partner seat. In branch relations, 卯 clashes with 酉 (Rooster) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 戌 (Dog), so a partner with a Rooster or Dog day branch will feel noticeably different to a Sin-myo person. 辛卯 is pillar 28 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Is Sin-myo a peach blossom pillar?

In schools that use the 도화 star, 卯 is one of its four branches, so Sin-myo is often described as charming and socially magnetic. We read that as a personality flavor, not a forecast about romance.

Is Sin-myo a weak Day Master?

Soft Metal on strong Wood can lean weaker, because the birthday type spends energy controlling its Wealth. Your birth month weighs in heavily here. In daily life it shows up as needing more rest than you think.

What kind of partner suits Sin-myo?

Someone grounding and warm who enjoys good things but also invites depth. Earth types often give you the stability that lets your sparkle last.
