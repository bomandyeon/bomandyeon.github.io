Gi-mi: The Summer Field

A garden field in high summer heat. Warm, generous, steady, and quietly stubborn under its easygoing smile.

Short answer

Gi-mi (己未) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Garden Field birthday type (Gi Earth) standing on the Goat, a garden field in high summer heat. Warm, generous, steady, and quietly stubborn under its easygoing smile.

Gi-mi places the Garden Field on 未, the Goat: hot, dry earth at the end of summer, with 丁 Fire and 乙 Wood inside. Earth on Earth, a 간여지동 pillar. The image is farmland in late July: warm, abundant, a little sunburnt, with heat shimmering above the rows. It is generous ground, full of life and food.

You are warm, sociable and steady. You like taking care of people and you are good at it. The hidden 丁 Fire is your Resource, which supports you emotionally and intellectually. The hidden 乙 Wood is Seven Killings, so there is a quiet inner pressure, a sense that you should always be doing more. Classical texts would tell summer Gi to find 癸 Water. Without it, the field gets dry, and you can become stubborn, irritable or stuck in routines you have outgrown.

People often assume summer-field types are simple because you are easy to be around. You are not simple. You store a lot under the surface: old memories, careful observations about who did what, and a surprisingly detailed plan for the next five years. You just prefer to share them over food, slowly, with people who have earned a seat at the table.

BomYou make life feel abundant and warm for everyone around you. Your steady generosity is a real gift.

Gi-mi in love

Friend (비견) in the partner seat (spouse palace) means you want an equal partner, someone who feels like a teammate and best friend. You enjoy sharing everyday life and building comfortable traditions. You are loyal, generous and steady.

You can be stubborn in arguments and sensitive to feeling unappreciated. You may also compete with a partner over who is more tired or who does more. Water types, calm and emotionally fluent, cool your field and keep it fertile.

As a friend

You are the friend who hosts summer barbecues, brings extra snacks and remembers everyone's favorite drink. You are warm and dependable. You can be stubborn about plans and slow to forgive slights. Your friends love your loyalty and tease you about your routines.

Red flags

  • You dig in on small arguments just to avoid losing.
  • You keep score of who does more and never say it out loud.
  • You overwork and call it being responsible.
  • You resist change even when the soil is clearly exhausted.

YeonBeing right about the thermostat is not worth a three-day sulk. Let it go. Drink some water. Literally.

At work

Gi-mi does well in practical, people-centered roles: hospitality, food, teaching, property, community work, healthcare support, small business. You are steady and reliable. Build cooling habits into your routine, rest, reflection and fresh ideas, so you do not dry out.

Gi-mi vs the other Gi Earth pillars

Gi-mi is the warmest and most sociable Gi pillar. Gi-chuk (己丑), its clash partner (丑未), is also Earth on Earth with a Friend partner seat, but winter and reserved. Gi-sa (己巳) is studious, Gi-myo (己卯) accommodating, Gi-yu (己酉) crafty, Gi-hae (己亥) romantic and practical. You are the summer field: generous, warm and harder to move than your relaxed manner suggests.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gi-mi: the 未 Goat branch is Earth and hides 丁 (Indirect Resource), 乙 (Seven Killings), 己 (Friend), read from a Gi Earth point of view. Its main qi, 己, puts Friend (비견 比肩) in the Gi-mi partner seat. In branch relations, 未 clashes with 丑 (Ox) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 午 (Horse), so a partner with a Ox or Horse day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gi-mi person. 己未 is pillar 56 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Is Gi-mi a strong Day Master?

Often, since Earth on Earth gives support and 未 holds Fire. Full strength depends on the whole chart, especially the birth month.

Why is Gi-mi stubborn?

Hot, dry Earth holds firm. The upside is steadiness and loyalty. The thing to practice is bending a little before you are forced to.

What balances Gi-mi?

Water first, for cooling and moisture. Emotional openness, rest and new experiences keep your field fertile.
