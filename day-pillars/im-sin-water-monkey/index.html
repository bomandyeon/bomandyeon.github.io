Im-sin: The River from the Mountain Spring

A fast river pouring out of a rocky spring. Curious, quick, resourceful, and always thinking three moves ahead.

  • Yang Water stem
  • Monkey branch · Metal
  • Pillar 9 of 60
  • Partner seat: Indirect Resource 편인
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Short answer

Im-sin (壬申) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Ocean birthday type (Im Water) standing on the Monkey, a fast river pouring out of a rocky spring. Curious, quick, resourceful, and always thinking three moves ahead.

Im-sin places 壬, the Ocean, on 申, the Monkey: early autumn Metal with 壬 Water and 戊 Earth inside. Metal produces Water, so this branch is Im's Resource, Indirect Resource (편인) here, and 申 is also the branch where Water is "born" (장생) in the twelve-stage cycle. Picture a river bursting out of a granite spring high in the mountains in late August. Cold, clear, fast, and fed from a source that does not run dry.

The Di Tian Sui opens its verse on Im with 壬水通河, Im Water connects the rivers. You are a connector by nature: of people, ideas, facts and opportunities. Your mind is quick and slightly unconventional, the Indirect Resource gift. You learn by absorbing everything and you get bored by anything you have already figured out. The Monkey adds wit and adaptability. The classical caution for strong Im is that it needs banks, 戊 Earth, to give it direction. Without structure you can flood: too many projects, too many tabs open, too many half-finished conversations.

BomYour mind is a river that connects everything it touches. People leave conversations with you smarter and more curious.

Im-sin in love

Indirect Resource in the partner seat (spouse palace) often draws you to partners who are smart, unusual and a bit independent, people who teach you something or see the world sideways. You are curious about the people you love and you remember the odd details: their favorite font, the name of their childhood dog.

The risk is detachment. You can live in your head, analyze feelings instead of having them, and move on to the next fascinating thing. A partner with Earth energy, grounded and steady, gives your river banks to flow between. Commit to fewer people and more depth. You will be surprised how much there is to discover in one person.

As a friend

You are the friend with a podcast recommendation for every situation and a contact for every problem. You are fun, quick and generous with information. You can be hard to pin down, drifting between groups and plans. Your friends appreciate it when you come back with a story and stay for dinner.

Red flags

  • You analyze your feelings until they leave the room.
  • You keep options open so long that nobody feels chosen.
  • You start ten things and finish the two that stayed interesting.
  • You change the subject the moment it becomes personal.

YeonNot every feeling needs a theory. Some just need you to sit there and have them. Try it tonight.

At work

Im-sin thrives in research, tech, journalism, consulting, trading, travel, translation and anything that rewards fast learning and wide networks. You see connections others miss. Give yourself structure, a manager with clear goals or deadlines you set publicly, so your river reaches the sea instead of spreading into a marsh.

Im-sin vs the other Im Water pillars

Im-sin is the most curious and quick-moving Im pillar. Im-in (壬寅), its clash partner (寅申), pours its Water into Wood and is expressive and generous where you are absorbent and analytical. Im-ja (壬子) is Water on Water, deeper and more intense. Im-o (壬午) has a hidden combination in its partner seat and leans romantic and practical. Im-jin (壬辰) and Im-sul (壬戌) carry Seven Killings in the partner seat and feel more pressure. Your partner seat holds Resource, so you look for a partner who stimulates your mind rather than one who challenges or anchors you.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Im-sin: the 申 Monkey branch is Metal and hides 戊 (Seven Killings), 壬 (Friend), 庚 (Indirect Resource), read from a Im Water point of view. Its main qi, 庚, puts Indirect Resource (편인 偏印) in the Im-sin partner seat. In branch relations, 申 clashes with 寅 (Tiger) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 巳 (Snake), so a partner with a Tiger or Snake day branch will feel noticeably different to a Im-sin person. 壬申 is pillar 9 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

What does 'Im Water connects the rivers' mean?

It paraphrases the Di Tian Sui line 壬水通河. Im is yang Water, pictured as rivers and oceans. The line suggests flow, reach and connection, which shows up in personality as curiosity and networking.

Is Im-sin a strong Day Master?

Often well supported, since Metal feeds Water and 申 is where Water is born. The full chart, especially the birth month, decides.

What kind of partner suits Im-sin?

Someone grounded and interesting who gives you direction without trying to dam you. Earth types often provide useful banks.
