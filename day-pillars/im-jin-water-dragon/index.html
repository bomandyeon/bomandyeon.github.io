Im-jin: The Ocean Dragon

A dragon rising out of a storm-dark sea. Commanding, deep, ambitious, and capable of very big tides.

  • Yang Water stem
  • Dragon branch · Earth
  • Pillar 29 of 60
  • Spouse palace: Seven Killings 편관
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Im-jin places 壬, the Ocean, on 辰, the Dragon: damp spring earth that also happens to be the storage vault of Water. Earth controls Water, so the branch is Im's Seven Killings (편관), the most demanding of the Ten Gods. And 壬辰 is one of the four classic 괴강 (魁罡) pillars, a small club known for force of will. In Korean folklore the dragon king lives under the sea and sends the rain. Picture exactly that: a dragon surging out of a dark, heaving ocean, clouds rolling in behind it.

You are commanding and ambitious, with deep reserves of energy and intellect. Because 辰 stores 癸 Water, you have more roots than most Water-on-Earth pillars, which shows up as stamina. You can work, think and feel for a very long time. The hidden 乙 Wood is your Hurting Officer, giving you a sharp tongue and original ideas. Seven Killings adds pressure and drive; the 괴강 label adds a dramatic, all-or-nothing streak. The caution is turbulence. Strong Water under pressure surges, and Im-jin can swing between brilliant focus and emotional storms that leave everyone holding onto the furniture.

BomYou have depth and strength that make people feel protected. When you decide to lead, people line up behind you.

Im-jin in love

With Seven Killings in the spouse palace, you are often drawn to strong, intense, even intimidating partners. A pushover bores you. You want someone with presence, and you love with real depth and protectiveness once you commit.

The shadow is control. You can test partners to see whether they are strong enough, or try to steer the relationship like a ship in bad weather. You do best with someone secure and warm who does not flinch at your intensity and does not try to match it wave for wave. Wood energy helps channel your power into growth; Fire energy brings light to your depths.

As a friend

You are the friend who leads the group, protects the vulnerable one and occasionally causes the drama. You are loyal and unforgettable. You can be domineering in planning and blunt in feedback. Your friends know the storms pass quickly and the loyalty never does.

Red flags

  • You test partners like a storm testing a ship.
  • You take charge of situations nobody asked you to run.
  • You swing from calm to tidal wave without a forecast.
  • You treat compromise as surrender.

YeonYou do not have to flood the room to prove you are an ocean. Indoor voice, dragon. Inside the relationship too.

At work

Im-jin thrives in leadership, strategy, finance, law, research, entrepreneurship and high-stakes fields where big thinking pays off. You see the whole board and you are not scared of bold moves. Build alliances on purpose. Even a dragon gets further with a fleet behind it.

Im-jin vs the other Im Water pillars

Im-jin is the most commanding of the Im pillars. Im-sul (壬戌), its clash partner (辰戌), also carries Seven Killings in the spouse palace, but on dry earth with no stored Water, so it feels more boxed in and disciplined where you have stamina and swagger. Im-ja (壬子) is Water on Water, deeper but less structured. Im-sin (壬申) is curious and analytical. Im-o (壬午) is warm and romantic. Im-in (壬寅) is generous and expressive. As a 괴강 pillar you carry a layer of force and drama your gentler siblings simply do not.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Im-jin: the 辰 Dragon branch is Earth and hides 乙 (Hurting Officer), 癸 (Rob Wealth), 戊 (Seven Killings), read from a Im Water point of view. Its main qi, 戊, puts Seven Killings (편관 偏官) in the Im-jin spouse palace. In branch relations, 辰 clashes with 戌 (Dog) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 酉 (Rooster), so a partner with a Dog or Rooster day branch will feel noticeably different to a Im-jin person. 壬辰 is pillar 29 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

What does 괴강 mean for Im-jin?

괴강 (魁罡) is a classical label for a handful of day pillars, including 壬辰. It is associated with strong will, leadership and intensity. We read it as a personality signal, not a warning.

Is Im-jin a strong Day Master?

Often stronger than other Water-on-Earth pillars, because the Dragon branch stores Water and gives Im a root. The birth month and other pillars still decide the overall balance.

What balances Im-jin?

Wood, which channels Water into growth, and Fire, which warms it. Creative outlets, patience and warm, steady people are the everyday version.
