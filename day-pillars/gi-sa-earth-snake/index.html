Gi-sa: The Sunlit Field

Earth Snake · 기사 · day pillar

A garden field warming in early summer sun. Nurturing, thoughtful, quietly ambitious, and smarter than it lets people see.

  • Yin Earth stem
  • Snake branch · Fire
  • Pillar 6 of 60

LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Gi-sa (己巳) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Garden Field birthday type (Gi Earth) standing on the Snake, a garden field warming in early summer sun. Nurturing, thoughtful, quietly ambitious, and smarter than it lets people see.

Gi-sa places 己, the Garden Field, on 巳, the Snake: early summer, with 丙 Fire as the branch’s main qi. Fire produces Earth, so this is a field soaking up sunshine, its Direct Resource (정인). The Snake also holds 庚 Metal and 戊 Earth. Picture farmland in late May: warm soil, neat rows, everything starting to grow at once, and a snake sunning itself on a stone wall at the edge.

The Di Tian Sui describes Gi Earth as 卑濕 中正蓄藏, low and moist, centered and good at storing. You are the person who quietly holds things: knowledge, secrets, resources, other people’s feelings. The sunlight underneath you means you are well supported, curious and a lifelong learner. People underestimate you because you are modest and unshowy. Then they find out you read the whole manual, took notes and already fixed the problem. The classical warning for Gi in summer is drying out. Without 癸 Water, the field cracks, and you can become anxious, overthinking and quietly controlling when you feel unsupported.

Gi-sa in love

Your partner seat (spouse palace) holds Direct Resource, which classically signals a nurturing partner energy. You want someone who feels safe, wise and steady, a person who teaches you things and looks after you when you are sick. You are attentive and devoted, and you remember every detail of a partner’s life.

The risk is dependence, either leaning on a partner for direction or becoming their unofficial caretaker. You may also keep your real wishes stored away, waiting for the perfect moment to bring them up. There is rarely a perfect moment. Water types, gentle and emotionally open, keep your field soft and fertile.

As a friend

You are the friend who sends the article, the recipe or the link to the apartment listing before anyone asks. You are thoughtful, loyal and a great listener. You can be shy in big groups and a little secretive about your own troubles. Your friends wish you would let them help as much as you help them.

Red flags

  • You store your feelings so carefully you forget where you put them.
  • You look for a parent and call it a partner.
  • You overthink small things until they become big things.
  • You control quietly by organizing everything.

At work

Gi-sa shines in learning-heavy, caring and organizing roles: education, research, library and information science, healthcare, HR, editing, nonprofit management. You are reliable and well informed. Speak up for your ideas; you are often the most prepared person in the room and the least likely to say so.

Gi-sa vs the other Gi Earth pillars

Gi-sa is the most studious and supported of the Gi pillars. Gi-hae (己亥) is its clash partner (巳亥), a field by cold water, more romantic and practical where you are warm and scholarly. Gi-mi (己未) and Gi-chuk (己丑) sit on Earth and are more self-reliant and stubborn. Gi-myo (己卯) lives under Wood pressure, Gi-yu (己酉) pours itself out through Metal into skills and craft. Your partner seat looks for a nurturing partner, which is a real difference from Gi-myo’s attraction to intensity or Gi-chuk’s wish for an equal.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gi-sa: the 巳 Snake branch is Fire and hides 戊 (Rob Wealth), 庚 (Hurting Officer), 丙 (Direct Resource), read from a Gi Earth point of view. Its main qi, 丙, puts Direct Resource (정인 正印) in the Gi-sa partner seat. In branch relations, 巳 clashes with 亥 (Pig) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 申 (Monkey), so a partner with a Pig or Monkey day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gi-sa person. 己巳 is pillar 6 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Why is Gi-sa described as a sunlit field?

The stem is Gi, garden earth, and the branch is 巳, early summer Fire. Fire feeds Earth, so the image is fertile soil warmed by the sun.

What does Direct Resource in the partner seat mean?

Classically it points to a nurturing, supportive partner energy. Many Gi-sa people look for safety and wisdom in love, and also offer it.

What balances Gi-sa?

Water, which keeps warm Earth from drying out. Emotional openness, rest and calm, kind people help you stay soft rather than anxious.
