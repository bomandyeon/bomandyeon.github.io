Im-sul: The Ocean Against the Cliffs

The sea breaking against an autumn cliff. Disciplined, determined, deeper than it looks, and stronger for its limits.

  • Yang Water stem
  • Dog branch · Earth
  • Pillar 59 of 60
  • Spouse palace: Seven Killings 편관
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Im-sul places the Ocean on 戌, the Dog: dry autumn earth holding 辛 Metal and 丁 Fire. Earth controls Water, so the branch is Im's Seven Killings (편관), and some lists count 壬戌 among the 괴강 pillars. Picture the October sea throwing itself against a stone cliff: spray in the air, a booming sound, power meeting a wall that does not move.

The Qiong Tong Bao Jian actually wants Im to meet 戊 Earth, the embankment that turns a flood into a river. So the cliff is not your enemy. It is what gives your ocean shape. You are disciplined and determined, with deep feelings held behind strong boundaries. The hidden 辛 Metal is your Resource, an inner reserve that feeds you when things get hard. The hidden 丁 Fire combines with 壬 (丁壬合), adding warmth, charm and a romantic streak people rarely expect from someone so composed. The caution is pressure. You can feel boxed in, tested or restrained, and you may keep everything behind a seawall until it breaks through all at once.

BomYou turn pressure into strength and limits into shape. Your discipline makes people trust you with the hard things.

Im-sul in love

Seven Killings in the spouse palace draws you to strong, structured partners: people with presence and rules of their own. You respect strength. The hidden 丁壬 combination softens this into real romance once trust is built.

You may feel controlled by reasonable requests, or defend yourself before anyone has attacked. You can also choose partners who restrict you because restriction feels familiar. The right person is firm and kind at once, someone who gives you structure without making you smaller. With them, you are loyal, steady and deeply devoted.

As a friend

You are the disciplined friend who keeps promises and turns up on time. People rely on your steadiness. You can be guarded and slow to share your own troubles. When you finally do, your friends are honored, and a little surprised at how much was behind the wall.

Red flags

  • You treat every boundary as a battle line.
  • You fall for people who restrict you and call it stability.
  • You hold feelings behind a seawall until it breaks.
  • You push through pressure instead of naming it.

YeonCliffs are not the enemy, and neither is a day off. Let the tide go out once in a while.

At work

Im-sul thrives in structured, high-stakes roles: law, finance, operations, research, the military, management. You are excellent under rules that make sense and you rise in hierarchies that reward discipline. Choose leaders who use pressure to shape you, not to grind you down.

Im-sul vs the other Im Water pillars

Im-sul is the most disciplined and contained Im pillar. Im-jin (壬辰), its clash partner (辰戌), also has Seven Killings in the spouse palace, but wet earth stores Water, so Im-jin has more stamina and swagger while you have more restraint. Im-ja (壬子) has no banks at all and leans toward floods. Im-sin (壬申) is curious, Im-o (壬午) warm and romantic, Im-in (壬寅) generous. You are the ocean that learned to use its cliffs, which makes you steadier than your siblings under real pressure.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Im-sul: the 戌 Dog branch is Earth and hides 辛 (Direct Resource), 丁 (Direct Wealth), 戊 (Seven Killings), read from a Im Water point of view. Its main qi, 戊, puts Seven Killings (편관 偏官) in the Im-sul spouse palace. In branch relations, 戌 clashes with 辰 (Dragon) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 卯 (Rabbit), so a partner with a Dragon or Rabbit day branch will feel noticeably different to a Im-sul person. 壬戌 is pillar 59 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Is Im-sul a 괴강 pillar?

Some lists include 壬戌 with 庚辰, 庚戌, 壬辰 and 戊戌; others do not. Either way, it is read as a sign of willpower and intensity, not misfortune.

Is Im-sul a weak Day Master?

Dry Earth controls Water, so many Im-sul charts lean weaker, though the hidden 辛 Metal offers support. The birth month and other pillars decide.

What helps Im-sul feel balanced?

Metal and Water support, and partners or mentors who give structure with kindness. Regular release, like exercise, writing or talking it out, keeps the seawall from cracking.
