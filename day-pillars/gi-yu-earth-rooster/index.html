Gi-yu: The Orchard at Harvest

Earth Rooster · 기유 · day pillar

An orchard heavy with fruit in autumn. Skilled, perceptive, generous with talent, and a perfectionist about details.

  • Yin Earth stem
  • Rooster branch · Metal
  • Pillar 46 of 60
  • Partner seat: Eating God 식신
LoveRed flagsWorkFAQ

Gi-yu (己酉) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Garden Field birthday type (Gi Earth) standing on the Rooster, an orchard heavy with fruit in autumn. Skilled, perceptive, generous with talent, and a perfectionist about details.

Gi-yu places the Garden Field on 酉, the Rooster: refined, pure autumn Metal. Earth produces Metal, so this branch is Gi’s output, the Eating God (식신). The image is an orchard in late September: branches heavy with fruit, everything ready to harvest. Your soil has done the work; now it is producing something beautiful and useful.

You are skilled, detail-minded and expressive through craft. You make things: meals, spreadsheets, garments, code, delightful birthday cards. You have taste and a sharp eye for quality. Eating God energy also gives you a playful, pleasure-loving side. The classical caution is that Earth giving everything to Metal can get depleted. You can over-deliver, perfect endlessly and run your field dry, wondering why you feel tired even when everything looks great.

The Rooster is also the branch of evening and of autumn harvest, the time when work turns into results. Gi-yu people often feel happiest when they can see the finished product: a clean kitchen, a delivered project, a jar of homemade jam on a friend’s shelf. That need for a visible result is a strength. It also means you should pick work where the harvest actually comes, rather than endless planting.

Gi-yu in love

Eating God in your partner seat (spouse palace) makes you an affectionate, fun and generous partner. You love sharing food, trying new things and making your partner’s life better. You notice details: their favorite snack, the song that makes them cry.

You can also be critical, especially about small things, and you may express love through doing rather than saying. Let your partner do things for you too. Fire energy, warm and supportive, refuels your field. Wood energy can feel challenging but helps you grow.

As a friend

You are the friend who makes the best gifts and gives the most specific compliments. You are fun to be around and you love planning experiences. You can be a perfectionist about plans and quietly judgmental about taste. Your friends forgive you because the brunch you planned was perfect.

Red flags

  • You perfect things that were already good enough.
  • You show love by fixing and forget to show it by listening.
  • You notice every flaw and mention too many of them.
  • You give until you are empty and then feel unappreciated.

YeonNot every flaw needs a footnote. Let your partner load the dishwasher wrong. It is fine. Breathe.

At work

Gi-yu thrives in skilled, detail-heavy work: design, culinary arts, crafts, editing, jewelry making, quality assurance, software, fashion. You are a natural artisan. Watch for burnout; your standards are high, and your energy is not infinite.

Gi-yu vs the other Gi Earth pillars

Gi-yu is the most skilled and expressive Gi pillar. Gi-myo (己卯), its clash partner (卯酉), lives under Wood pressure and accommodates; you produce and polish. Gi-sa (己巳) is studious, Gi-hae (己亥) practical and romantic, Gi-chuk (己丑) and Gi-mi (己未) stubborn and self-reliant. Your partner seat holds the Eating God, so your relationships tend to center on shared pleasures, food, play and creative projects, more than any other Gi.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gi-yu: the 酉 Rooster branch is Metal and hides 庚 (Hurting Officer), 辛 (Eating God), read from a Gi Earth point of view. Its main qi, 辛, puts Eating God (식신 食神) in the Gi-yu partner seat. In branch relations, 酉 clashes with 卯 (Rabbit) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 辰 (Dragon), so a partner with a Rabbit or Dragon day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gi-yu person. 己酉 is pillar 46 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

Why is Gi-yu associated with craft and skill?

The Rooster branch is Metal, which Gi Earth produces. In saju, the produced element is your output: talent, expression, craft. Gi-yu’s partner seat holds this energy.

Is Gi-yu a perfectionist?

Often. Refined Metal energy loves precision. The upside is excellent work; the downside is exhaustion and criticism.

What balances Gi-yu?

Fire to support your Earth and rest to refill it. Warm people and fewer projects help.
