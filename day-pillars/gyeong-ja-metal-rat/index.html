Gyeong-ja: The Blade in the Winter Stream

A blade cooling in an icy stream. Witty, sharp, independent, and constitutionally allergic to being told what to do.

Short answer

Gyeong-ja (庚子) is one of the 60 Korean day pillars: the Sword birthday type (Gyeong Metal) standing on the Rat, a blade cooling in an icy stream. Witty, sharp, independent, and constitutionally allergic to being told what to do.

Gyeong-ja places the Sword on 子, the Rat: deep winter Water at midnight. Metal produces Water, so this branch is Gyeong's output, the Hurting Officer (상관) here. Picture a freshly forged blade being plunged into an icy stream: steam, a hiss, and then a cold, polished edge. Classical texts call this 金水傷官, Metal and Water Hurting Officer, a combination famous for intelligence, wit and good looks.

You are clever, articulate and independent. You see through nonsense instantly and say so, often hilariously. You love ideas, debate and freedom. Hurting Officer energy makes you creative and rebellious. The classical caution is cold: a winter blade in ice water lacks warmth. Gyeong in winter wants 丁 or 丙 Fire. Without warmth, you can become cynical, emotionally distant and sharp-tongued in ways that cut people you love.

Gyeong-ja in love

Hurting Officer in the partner seat (spouse palace) makes you a witty, stimulating partner who needs freedom. You fall for clever people who can keep up. You love banter, debate and shared jokes.

You can also be critical, restless and hard to pin down. You may push away a partner by testing their intelligence or patience. You need warmth: a partner with Fire energy who brings affection and enthusiasm, and who can match your mind without competing with it.

As a friend

You are the friend with the best one-liners and the sharpest takes. You are loyal in your own way and you defend friends with words. You can be blunt to the point of rudeness and you sometimes disappear when you need space. Your friends love your brain and occasionally wish you came with a warning label.

Red flags

  • You win arguments with your partner and call it communication.
  • You treat vulnerability like a weakness to hide.
  • You get bored as soon as someone is easy to read.
  • You say the brilliant cruel thing and pretend it was a joke.

YeonBeing right is not the same as being kind. Try warmth instead of wit, just once, and watch what happens.

At work

Gyeong-ja thrives in roles that reward intellect and independence: writing, law, journalism, research, tech, design, comedy, consulting. You are brilliant at critique and innovation. Choose environments that value your voice. Rigid hierarchies feel like a cage.

Gyeong-ja vs the other Gyeong Metal pillars

Gyeong-ja is the wittiest and most rebellious Gyeong pillar. Gyeong-o (庚午), its clash partner (子午), sits on Fire and is honorable and dutiful where you are irreverent. Gyeong-sin (庚申) is pure Metal and blunt; you are sharp and articulate. Gyeong-jin (庚辰) and Gyeong-sul (庚戌) are commanding 괴강 pillars, Gyeong-in (庚寅) is enterprising. Your partner seat holds the Hurting Officer, so you want a clever, free-spirited partner, where Gyeong-o wants a principled one.

Chart notes

Chart notes for Gyeong-ja: the 子 Rat branch is Water and hides 壬 (Eating God), 癸 (Hurting Officer), read from a Gyeong Metal point of view. Its main qi, 癸, puts Hurting Officer (상관 傷官) in the Gyeong-ja partner seat. In branch relations, 子 clashes with 午 (Horse) and forms a six-harmony pair (육합) with 丑 (Ox), so a partner with a Horse or Ox day branch will feel noticeably different to a Gyeong-ja person. 庚子 is pillar 37 of the sixty in the 육십갑자 cycle.

FAQ

What does 金水傷官 mean?

It is a classical phrase for Metal birthday types (Day Masters) with strong Water output, as in Gyeong-ja. It is associated with intelligence, eloquence and attractiveness, plus a rebellious streak.

Is Gyeong-ja cold?

The image is a blade in winter water, so many Gyeong-ja people seem cool at first. Underneath is loyalty and humor. Warmth from Fire people and environments brings it out.

What kind of partner suits Gyeong-ja?

Someone clever, warm and secure, who enjoys your mind without competing with it. Fire types help balance your cold edge.
