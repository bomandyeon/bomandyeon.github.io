Who it's for

For actual couples. Dating properly, living together, engaged, married, or just past the stage where you hide your weird habits. You add your birthday and theirs, and Bom and Yeon read the two of you: how each of you loves, what your chemistry is made of, how you fight and make up, what home feels like, and what helps you last.

Bom notices the soft stuff. Yeon notices the stuff you hoped nobody would. It's a conversation starter, not a verdict. Read it together on the sofa, argue about which bits are true, and know each other a little better by the end. Not for situationships or exes. There are readings for those.

11 illustrated chapters. Yours depends on your chart, your answers and the chapter on the two of you. Opens as a vertical webtoon with Bom and Yeon. Three pillars from your birth date, or four if you add your birth time; no 10-year luck cycles (대운).

Questions

FAQ

Do we both need our birthdays?

Yes, both birth dates. Birth times are optional. Adding yours gives your side of the reading a fourth pillar.

Does it say if we're compatible?

You'll see the compatibility score for your two types, the same number as on our compatibility pages. The reading itself isn't a pass or fail. It's about how your patterns meet. Every couple gets easy parts and homework.

Who is it written to, me or them?

It's written to whoever puts their birthday in first, about the two of you. Swap the order and you get the same relationship from the other side. Yes, you can read both and compare.

Can I get it for a situationship?

This one is for real relationships. If it's undefined, the Situationship reading is the better fit.

Is this a full 궁합, the traditional Korean compatibility reading?

It's a lighter version. It compares your two types, your day branches and how strong each chart is. A traditional 궁합 reads both whole charts and the 10-year luck cycles.

How long is the reading?

11 illustrated chapters. Each chapter opens with a scene and then Bom and Yeon talk it through. What each chapter says depends on your chart and answers. Read it in one go or a chapter at a time, and save it as a PDF to keep.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: how long have you been together; what do you two argue about most; what do you want from this reading. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
