Who it's for

For couples, the real kind: together, committed, maybe living together, maybe married, maybe just past the stage where you pretend you don't have habits. Bom and Yeon read both of your charts: your Day Masters, how your elements meet, your day branches (the 'spouse palace' in saju), and how strong each chart is. Then they write about the two of you: how each of you loves, what your chemistry is made of, how you fight and make up, and what helps you grow over years.

It's a conversation starter, not a verdict. Read it together on the couch, argue about which parts are true, and come away knowing each other a little better. Not for situationships or exes: there are readings for those.

What's personal

What makes it yours

Built from your day, month and year pillars: your season, your element balance, your day branch, both birthdays and your answers if you give them.

  • Your Day MasterOne of 10 souls, from your day pillar. Up to 11 chapters of webtoon, written for it.
  • Your seasonSpring, summer, autumn, winter, or the Earth days at the end of a season, set by the solar terms on your birth date and converted to your time zone, not the calendar month. A season chapter and its own panel.
  • Your strengthStrong or weak, worked out from your day, month and year pillars. A chapter on the blind spot that comes with it, and the element that steadies you.
  • Your element balanceWhat your chart carries most and what it lacks, each with its own section and panel.
  • Your day branchThe animal under your Day Master, the 'spouse palace' in saju. Its own section, one of 12.
  • Your answers, if you give themUp to three quick optional questions before the reading opens. Each answer changes a section. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing.
  • The two of youBoth charts: their Day Master, season and chart strength, what they are to you in saju's ten roles, what your two stems do together, how your elements argue, and your two day branches. Four chapters about the two of you.

Questions

FAQ

Do we both need to enter our birthdays?

Yes. The reading needs both birth dates. Birth times are optional; yours adds a fourth pillar to your side of the reading.

Will it tell us if we're compatible?

It shows your compatibility score for your two Day Masters, the same number as on our compatibility pages, but the reading is about how your patterns meet, not a pass or fail. Every pairing has its easy parts and its homework.

Whose reading is it?

It's written to whoever enters their birthday first, about the two of you. Swap the order and you get the same relationship told from the other side.

Is this for situationships?

No, this one is for real relationships. If it's undefined, the Situationship reading fits better.

Is it a full Korean compatibility reading (궁합)?

It's a lighter version. It compares your Day Masters, your day branches and the strength of each chart. A traditional 궁합 reads both whole charts and luck cycles.

How long is the reading?

11 chapters. A typical reading is around 2,380 words, about a 10-minute read; depending on your chart and answers it runs from roughly 2,200 to 2,600 words. The exact count for yours is printed at the end of the reading.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: how long have you been together; what do you two argue about most; what do you want from this reading. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
