Questions

FAQ

Will this tell me if my ex is coming back?

No, and nothing honest can. Another person's choices aren't written in your birth date. This reading looks at your side: why it ended for you, why you keep thinking about them, and what you can do either way.

Do I need my ex's birthday?

No, it's optional. Your reading is built from your own day, month and year pillars, your season and your element balance. Add their birthday and you get one extra chapter on how your two patterns met and why it hurt the way it did.

Is this fortune telling?

No. We use saju as a personality language for self-reflection, a bit like MBTI with about a thousand years of practice behind it. For entertainment and self-reflection.

Do I need my birth time?

No. Your Day Master comes from your birth date, and we read three pillars from it: year, month and day. If you know your birth time, add it under "Birth time and place" in the form. It adds a fourth pillar, which shifts your element balance and strength count a little. If you were born between 11 pm and midnight, saju schools disagree on your day: we follow the common Korean rule and count you as the next day when you give a time.

Will it tell me to text them or not?

It won't make the decision for you. It gives you questions to ask yourself if they ever reach out, so whatever you choose is actually your choice.

How complete is this reading?

A full Korean saju reading uses four pillars: year, month, day and hour, eight characters in total. With your birth date alone we read three pillars, which is how many Koreans read saju when the hour is unknown; add your birth time and we read all four. We don't include your 10-year luck cycles (대운), which traditional readers use for timing and which also depend on a male or female setting we don't ask for. Solar terms are converted to the time zone you were born in. Where a reading looks at two people, it compares your Day Masters and notes your day branches; a traditional compatibility reading (궁합) compares both whole charts.

How long is the reading?

10 chapters, 11 with their birthday. A typical reading is around 3,210 words; depending on your chart and answers it runs from roughly 2,900 to 3,500 words. The exact count for yours is printed at the end of the reading.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: who ended it; how long ago did it end; what do you want right now, honestly. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
