What makes it yours

Built from your day, month and year pillars, your season, your element balance.

  • Your Day MasterOne of 10 souls, from your day pillar. 6 chapters and six webtoon panels written for it.
  • Your seasonSpring, summer, autumn, winter, or the Earth days at the end of a season, set by the solar terms on your birth date and converted to your time zone, not the calendar month. A season chapter and its own panel.

10 souls × 5 seasons (the four seasons plus the Earth days that close each one) × 2 strengths = 100 core versions, then your element balance on top. Two people with the same Day Master can still get a different reading.

Questions

FAQ

Is it actually mean?

It's honest, with jokes. Yeon roasts your habits, never your worth, and every chapter ends somewhere useful. Bom also insisted on a fix chapter.

What's it based on?

Your Day Master, the element of the day you were born, plus your element balance. Saju is our personality language, like MBTI with about a thousand years of practice behind it.

Can I send it to a friend?

Yes, and it's even better read out loud in a group chat. Just let them enter their own birthday so they get their own roast.

Do I need my birth time?

No. Your Day Master comes from your birth date, and we read three pillars from it: year, month and day. If you know your birth time, add it under "Birth time and place" in the form. It adds a fourth pillar, which shifts your element balance and strength count a little. If you were born between 11 pm and midnight, saju schools disagree on your day: we follow the common Korean rule and count you as the next day when you give a time.

Is this serious advice?

No. For entertainment and self-reflection. If something in it hits home, that part is yours to keep.

How complete is this reading?

A full Korean saju reading uses four pillars: year, month, day and hour, eight characters in total. With your birth date alone we read three pillars, which is how many Koreans read saju when the hour is unknown; add your birth time and we read all four. We don't include your 10-year luck cycles (대운), which traditional readers use for timing and which also depend on a male or female setting we don't ask for. Solar terms are converted to the time zone you were born in. Where a reading looks at two people, it compares your Day Masters and notes your day branches; a traditional compatibility reading (궁합) compares both whole charts.
