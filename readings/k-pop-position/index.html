Questions

Wait, is this about real idols?

Nope. No real idols, groups or birthdays anywhere in it. It borrows the positions fans already know (leader, main vocal, visual, maknae and so on) as a fun way to describe how you are in a group.

How do you pick my position?

From the day you were born. In Korean saju, your birth day gives you one of ten core types, and each type has a home position. The season you were born in, your mix of elements and how strong your chart is then change how you’d play it.

Can I do it with my friends?

Please do. Everyone enters their own birthday and gets their own position. Line them up and build your group. The reading code at the top shows who got what, so nobody can lie about being the visual.

Do I need to know my birth time?

No. Your birth date is enough. A birth time adds two more characters to your chart and can shift your element mix a little.

Okay, but is it accurate?

Saju is a personality language with centuries of practice behind it, a bit like MBTI with older notes. Read it for fun and self-reflection. It doesn’t predict your future, and it says nothing about your singing or dancing.

How long is the reading?

11 illustrated chapters. Each chapter opens with a scene and then Bom and Yeon talk it through. What each chapter says depends on your chart and answers. Read it in one go or a chapter at a time, and save it as a PDF to keep.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: in a group project, you’re usually...; if you had to be famous for one thing. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
