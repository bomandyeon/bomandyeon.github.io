Questions

FAQ

Is this about real idols?

No. There are no real idols, groups or birthdays in this reading. It uses the roles fans know (leader, main vocal, visual, maknae and so on) as a fun language for how you work in a group.

How is my position picked?

From your Day Master, the heavenly stem of the day you were born. Each of the ten souls gets a home position. Your season, your element balance and whether your chart is strong or weak then change how you play it.

Can I do this with my friends?

Please do. Each friend enters their own birthday and gets their own position. Compare readings and build your group. Your position is printed at the top of each reading.

Do I need my birth time?

No. Your birth date is enough for three pillars. A birth time adds a fourth pillar and can change your element balance a little.

Is it accurate?

Saju is a personality language with about a thousand years of practice behind it, like MBTI with older notes. It's for fun and self-reflection, not a prediction, and it says nothing about singing or dancing talent.

How long is the reading?

11 chapters. A typical reading is around 2,560 words, about a 11-minute read; depending on your chart and answers it runs from roughly 2,400 to 2,800 words. The exact count for yours is printed at the end of the reading.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: in a group project, you're usually...; if you had to be famous for one thing. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
