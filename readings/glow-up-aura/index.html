Questions

FAQ

Is this about changing how I look?

No. It's about aura, style, color and how you come across. We never suggest changing your body or face, and nothing here is beauty or medical advice.

Where do the colors come from?

From the five elements in saju, worked out from the same element that balances the rest of your reading: a strong chart leads with a balancing element, a weak chart with the element that supports it. One chapter names your colors, plus one to go easy on; the rest of the reading leaves colors alone.

Do I need my birth time?

No. Your Day Master comes from your birth date, and we read three pillars from it: year, month and day. If you know your birth time, add it under "Birth time and place" in the form. It adds a fourth pillar, which shifts your element balance and strength count a little. If you were born between 11 pm and midnight, saju schools disagree on your day: we follow the common Korean rule and count you as the next day when you give a time.

I don't wear color. Is this still useful?

Yes. Every palette has neutrals, and accessories, textures and photo settings work in black and white too.

Is this for any gender?

Yes. The examples lean toward the styles our readers love, but the aura and color advice works for anyone.

How complete is this reading?

A full Korean saju reading uses four pillars: year, month, day and hour, eight characters in total. With your birth date alone we read three pillars, which is how many Koreans read saju when the hour is unknown; add your birth time and we read all four. We don't include your 10-year luck cycles (대운), which traditional readers use for timing and which also depend on a male or female setting we don't ask for. Solar terms are converted to the time zone you were born in. Where a reading looks at two people, it compares your Day Masters and notes your day branches; a traditional compatibility reading (궁합) compares both whole charts.

How long is the reading?

11 chapters. A typical reading is around 2,970 words, about a 12-minute read; depending on your chart it runs from roughly 2,800 to 3,300 words. The exact count for yours is printed at the end of the reading.
