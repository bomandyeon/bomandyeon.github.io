Questions

FAQ

Is this going to tell me to change how I look?

No. It's about aura, style, colour and how you come across. We never suggest changing your body or your face, and nothing here is beauty or medical advice.

Where do my colours come from?

From the five elements in Korean saju. Your Day Master has a home element with its own colours. Your chart's balance then decides which element should lead your palette, which one backs it up, and which to keep to a small detail.

Do I need to know my birth time?

No. Your birth date gives us your Day Master and three pillars: year, month and day. Know your birth time? Add it under "Birth time and place" in the form. That adds a fourth pillar, which nudges your element balance and strength count a little. Born between 11 pm and midnight? Saju schools disagree about which day that counts as. When you give a time, we follow the common Korean rule and count you as the next day.

I basically only wear black. Is this still useful?

Yes. Every palette has neutrals, and the parts about accessories, textures, photo light and how people read you work in black and white too.

Does this work for any gender?

Yes. Some examples lean toward the styles our readers love most, but the aura and colour advice works for anyone.

How complete is this reading, honestly?

Full Korean saju uses four pillars, year, month, day and hour, so eight characters. From a birth date alone we read three pillars, which is how many Koreans read saju when the hour is unknown. Add your birth time and we read all four. We leave out the 10-year luck cycles (대운): traditional readers use them for timing, and they also need a male or female setting we don't ask for. Solar terms are converted to the time zone you were born in. In readings about two people, we compare your Day Masters and note your day branches; a traditional compatibility reading (궁합) compares both whole charts.

How long is the reading?

11 illustrated chapters. Each chapter opens with a scene and then Bom and Yeon talk it through. What each chapter says depends on your chart. Read it in one go or a chapter at a time, and save it as a PDF to keep.
