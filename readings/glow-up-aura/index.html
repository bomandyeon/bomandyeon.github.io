Questions

FAQ

Is this about changing how I look?

No. It's about aura, style, colour and how you come across. We never suggest changing your body or face, and nothing here is beauty or medical advice.

Where do the colours come from?

From the five elements in saju. Each Day Master has a home element with its own palette, plus the element that feeds it. Your element balance adds a note on what to lean into.

Do I need my birth time?

No. Your Day Master comes from your birth date alone. A birth hour adds a fuller element balance for the bonus notes.

I don't wear colour. Is this still useful?

Yes. Every palette has neutrals, and accessories, textures and photo settings work in black and white too.

Is this for any gender?

Yes. The examples lean toward the styles our readers love, but the aura and colour advice works for anyone.
