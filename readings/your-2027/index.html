Who it's for

For planners, journalers, and anyone who likes knowing what kind of month they're walking into. 2027 is a Jeong-mi year, 丁未, the Fire Goat. Bom and Yeon read what that year means for your Day Master, then go month by month through the twelve saju months, from Ipchun on February 4, 2027 to early February 2028, using each month's real pillar and the role it plays for you in saju.

Think mood calendar, not crystal ball. It won't tell you when you'll get rich, fall in love or get sick, and it doesn't believe in fate. It tells you which months lean social or quiet, bold or careful, so you can plan your year the way you'd plan around the weather.

11 illustrated chapters. Yours depends on your chart, your answers. Opens as a vertical webtoon with Bom and Yeon. Three pillars from your birth date, or four if you add your birth time; no 10-year luck cycles (대운).

Questions

FAQ

Why does it start in February and not January?

Because the saju year turns at Ipchun (입춘), the start of spring, around February 4. The Fire Goat year runs from February 4, 2027 to February 3, 2028, so its twelfth month lands in January 2028. January 2027 still belongs to the Horse year.

How exact are the month dates?

Each saju month starts on its solar term, calculated for your time zone. Your reading prints the start date of every month.

Is it going to predict what happens to me?

No. It describes the mood and focus of each month for your Day Master. No predictions about money, health, love or anything else, and no fixed fate. Use it like a seasonal planner, not a prophecy.

What are the smooth months and the heavy months?

The two months whose elements help your chart's balancing element most, and the two where the elements work against it or clash with your day branch. Smooth means easier to push. Heavy means plan more rest. Neither one guarantees anything.

How is this different from the free 2027 page?

No. The free page gives every soul one paragraph. This reading uses your whole chart and goes through the year one month at a time.

How long is the reading?

11 illustrated chapters. Each chapter opens with a scene and then Bom and Yeon talk it through. What each chapter says depends on your chart and answers. Read it in one go or a chapter at a time, and save it as a PDF to keep.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: what do you want 2027 to be about; how did 2026 feel. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
