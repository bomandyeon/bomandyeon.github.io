Who it's for

For planners, journalers, and anyone who likes to know what kind of month they're walking into. 2027 is a Jeong-mi year, 丁未, the Fire Goat. Bom and Yeon read what that year means for your Day Master, then go through the twelve saju months, from Ipchun on February 4, 2027 to early February 2028, using each month's real pillar and what it is to you in saju's ten roles.

It's a mood calendar, not a forecast. It won't tell you when you'll get rich, fall in love or get sick, and it doesn't believe in fate. It tells you which months lean social or quiet, bold or careful, so you can plan the year like you'd plan around the seasons.

Questions

FAQ

Why does it start in February?

The saju year turns at Ipchun (입춘), the start of spring, around February 4. So the Fire Goat year runs from February 4, 2027 to February 3, 2028, and its twelfth month falls in January 2028. January 2027 still belongs to the Horse year.

Are the month dates exact?

Each month starts on its solar term, calculated for your time zone. The reading prints the start date for every month.

Will it predict events?

No. It describes the mood and focus of each month for your Day Master. No predictions about money, health, love or anything else, and no fixed fate. Use it like a seasonal planner.

What are the 'smoothest' and 'hardest' months?

Two months where the month's elements help your chart's balancing element most, and two where they work against it or clash with your day branch. Smooth means easier to push; hard means plan for more rest. Neither guarantees anything.

Is this the same as the free 2027 page?

No. The free page has one paragraph per soul. This reading uses your full chart and goes month by month.

How long is the reading?

11 chapters. A typical reading is around 2,480 words, about a 10-minute read; depending on your chart and answers it runs from roughly 2,400 to 2,600 words. The exact count for yours is printed at the end of the reading.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: what do you want 2027 to be about; how did 2026 feel. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
