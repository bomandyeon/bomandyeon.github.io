Questions

Why does it ask for a second birthday?

Your birthday drives the whole reading. Theirs adds one chapter on how your two types meet: where you click and where you keep clashing. It can’t tell you what they feel or what they’ll do. Nothing honest can.

Will it tell me if they like me back?

No. Nobody can read another person’s feelings from a date of birth. Yeon would roast anyone who claimed to. What you get is you: why you stay, what you actually want, how you read mixed signals, and how to ask “what are we” in words that sound like you.

I don’t know their birthday. Can I still start?

Yes. Start with yours: the two free chapters are built from your own chart. The full comic compares your two charts, so it needs their birthday too. You can add it before you buy or right after, it’s one date. Don’t know it? Ask them, or check their socials.

So is this fortune telling?

No. We use saju as a personality language for looking at yourself, a bit like MBTI with about a thousand years of practice behind it. It’s for fun and for self-reflection. It doesn’t predict anything.

Will it tell me when to have the talk?

No dates, no deadlines, no “before it’s too late”. It helps you pick your own moment and find your own words. The timing is yours.

How much of a full saju reading is this?

A full Korean saju reading uses four pillars: year, month, day and hour, eight characters in all. From your birth date alone we read three pillars, which is how many Koreans read saju when the hour is unknown. Add your birth time and we read all four. We don’t include your 10-year luck cycles (대운), which traditional readers use for timing and which also depend on a male or female setting we don’t ask for. Solar terms are converted to the time zone you were born in. When a reading looks at two people, it compares your Day Masters and notes your day branches. A traditional compatibility reading (궁합) compares both whole charts.

How long is the reading?

11 illustrated chapters. Each chapter opens with a scene and then Bom and Yeon talk it through. What each chapter says depends on your chart and answers. Read it in one go or a chapter at a time, and save it as a PDF to keep.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: how long has this been going on; who usually reaches out first; what do you want from it. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
