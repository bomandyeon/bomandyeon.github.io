Questions

FAQ

Why does it ask for two birthdays?

Your birthday drives the reading. Theirs adds a note on how your two elements meet. It doesn't tell you what they feel or what they'll do. Nothing honest can.

Will it tell me if they like me?

No. Nobody can read another person's feelings from a date. This reading helps you see why you stay, what you actually want, how you read mixed signals, and how to ask for clarity in a way that suits you.

What if I don't know their birthday?

You can still read the whole thing. Every chapter is built from your own Day Master and element balance.

Is this fortune telling?

No. We use saju as a personality language for self-reflection, a bit like MBTI with about a thousand years of practice behind it. For entertainment and self-reflection.

Does it tell me when to have the talk?

It doesn't give dates or deadlines. It helps you choose your own moment and your own words.

How complete is this reading?

A full Korean saju reading uses four pillars: year, month, day and hour, eight characters in total. With your birth date alone we read three pillars, which is how many Koreans read saju when the hour is unknown; add your birth time and we read all four. We don't include your 10-year luck cycles (대운), which traditional readers use for timing and which also depend on a male or female setting we don't ask for. Solar terms are converted to the time zone you were born in. Where a reading looks at two people, it compares your Day Masters and notes your day branches; a traditional compatibility reading (궁합) compares both whole charts.

How long is the reading?

11 chapters. A typical reading is around 3,110 words, about a 13-minute read; depending on your chart and answers it runs from roughly 3,000 to 3,400 words. The exact count for yours is printed at the end of the reading.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: how long has this been going on; who usually reaches out first; what do you want from it. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
