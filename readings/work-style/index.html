Questions

So will this tell me which job to take?

It shows the kind of work and workplace that tends to suit your chart, with example roles. It isn’t a career test or career advice. Use it to understand what fills you up and what drains you, then decide with real-world information.

Does it predict money or promotions?

No. No income promises, no salary predictions, no promotion dates. It’s about how you work, how you lead and how you get along with people.

I’m a student, or between jobs. Is it still for me?

Yes. One of the optional questions asks where you are right now, and that part is written for your situation. Everything else is about your work style, which shows up in group projects, part-time jobs and whatever comes next.

Can I compare with my team?

Share your reading link and compare. Just remember it’s a personality language, not a performance review. Nobody gets to use their chart as an excuse to skip the Monday meeting.

How long is the reading?

11 illustrated chapters. Each chapter opens with a scene and then Bom and Yeon talk it through. What each chapter says depends on your chart and answers. Read it in one go or a chapter at a time, and save it as a PDF to keep.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: where are you with work right now; what drains you most at work. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
