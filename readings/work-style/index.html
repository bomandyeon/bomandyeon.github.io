Who it's for

For anyone who has wondered why some jobs feel like home and others feel like wearing someone else's shoes. Bom and Yeon read your Day Master, season and elements and translate them into work: your natural pace, your leadership style, the coworkers who drive you up the wall, the way you burn out, and the kind of workplace where you do your best.

It's a self-understanding reading, like MBTI for your work life, great to read before a review, a job change or a fight with a coworker. It doesn't promise income, promotions or a dream job. It helps you understand how you work so you can choose better.

Questions

FAQ

Will this tell me what job to get?

It tells you the job vibe that fits your chart and gives example roles, but it's not a career test or advice. Use it to understand what energizes and drains you, then decide with real-world information.

Does it say anything about money or promotions?

No. There are no income promises, salary predictions or promotion dates. It's about how you work, lead and get along with people.

I'm a student or between jobs. Is it still useful?

Yes. One of the optional questions asks where you are right now, and that section is written for your situation. The rest is about your work style, which you carry into school projects, part-time jobs and whatever comes next.

Can I share it with my team?

You can share your reading link. Comparing work styles with coworkers is fun, as long as everyone remembers it's a personality language, not a performance review.

How long is the reading?

11 chapters. A typical reading is around 2,470 words, about a 10-minute read; depending on your chart and answers it runs from roughly 2,300 to 2,800 words. The exact count for yours is printed at the end of the reading.

What are the questions before the reading?

Up to three, one tap each, all optional: where are you with work right now; what drains you most at work. Each answer changes a section of the reading. Skip any of them and that section is written for not knowing. Your answers stay in your browser and in your reading link.
