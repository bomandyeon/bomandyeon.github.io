Questions

FAQ

Is this my star sign?

No. It uses Korean saju, which starts from the element of the day you were born, worked out with the traditional solar calendar. It's a different system from Western astrology, and much older.

Do I need to know what time I was born?

No. Your core type comes from your birth date, and from it we read three pillars: year, month and day. Know your birth time? Add it under "Birth time and place" in the form. It adds a fourth pillar, which shifts your element balance and strength count a little. Born between 11 pm and midnight? Saju schools disagree on which day that counts as. We follow the common Korean rule and count it as the next day when you give a time.

So is this an attachment-style quiz?

Not a clinical one. We describe patterns that feel a bit like attachment styles, seen through your element. It's for fun and self-reflection, not a diagnosis.

Can I read it for my partner or my crush?

You can enter any birthday, but the reading talks to the person it belongs to, so it's most useful about you. For two people at once, try the compatibility pages.

Will it tell me whether someone's right for me?

It tells you what you tend to need and what you tend to repeat. Who's right for you is still your call. Bom and Yeon just hand you better questions.

How complete is the reading?

A full Korean saju reading uses four pillars (year, month, day and hour), eight characters in all. With your birth date alone we read three, which is how many Koreans read saju when the hour is unknown. Add your birth time and we read all four. We don't include the 10-year luck cycles (대운) that traditional readers use for timing, which also depend on a male or female setting we don't ask for. Solar terms are converted to the time zone you were born in. Where a reading looks at two people, it compares your core types (Day Masters) and notes your day branches. A traditional compatibility reading (궁합) compares both full charts.

How long is the reading?

11 illustrated chapters. Each chapter opens with a scene and then Bom and Yeon talk it through. What each chapter says depends on your chart. Read it in one go or a chapter at a time, and save it as a PDF to keep.
